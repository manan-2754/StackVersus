### Asana

**Pros:**

- Timelines, portfolios and goals
- Clean interface
- Strong integrations

**Cons:**

- Expensive for larger teams
- Single assignee per task

### Monday.com

_Visual work management platform_

**Pros:**

- Visual and intuitive
- Automations and integrations
- Templates for many workflows

**Cons:**

- Costs add up with add-ons

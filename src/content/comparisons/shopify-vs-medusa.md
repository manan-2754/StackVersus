## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Shopify — hosted commerce platform. Medusa — open-source composable commerce engine.
- Licensing differs: Shopify is proprietary while Medusa is open source.
- **Deployment:** Shopify — managed cloud. Medusa — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- Medusa can be started for free, while Shopify requires a paid plan. Shopify pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Shopify — easy setup. Medusa — modular Node.js architecture.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Shopify better than Medusa?

It depends on your requirements. Shopify is a strong fit for merchants wanting a fully hosted store, while Medusa suits developers building custom headless commerce.

### Is Shopify free to use?

Shopify does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Is Medusa free to use?

Yes, you can start with Medusa for free. Pricing model: Free open source; paid managed cloud.

### Can I self-host Shopify or Medusa?

Shopify is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Medusa can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Shopify and Medusa?

Shopify: extra transaction fees without Shopify Payments; app subscriptions add up. Medusa: requires developers; smaller ecosystem.

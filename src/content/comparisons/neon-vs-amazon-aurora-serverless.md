# Neon vs Amazon Aurora Serverless: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Neon is the better pick for serverless apps and preview environments on Postgres. Amazon Aurora Serverless is the better pick for AWS workloads with variable traffic.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Neon

_Serverless Postgres with branching_

**Pros:**

- Scale-to-zero compute
- Instant database branching
- Standard Postgres compatibility

**Cons:**

- Cold starts after scaling to zero
- Some Postgres extensions unavailable

---

### Amazon Aurora Serverless

_Auto-scaling MySQL and PostgreSQL on AWS_

**Pros:**

- Automatic capacity scaling
- MySQL and Postgres compatibility
- Deep AWS integration

**Cons:**

- AWS only
- Pricing can be complex to estimate

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Neon — serverless Postgres with branching. Amazon Aurora Serverless — auto-scaling MySQL and PostgreSQL on AWS.
- Licensing differs: Neon is open source while Amazon Aurora Serverless is proprietary.
- Neon can be started for free, while Amazon Aurora Serverless requires a paid plan. Amazon Aurora Serverless pricing: pay-as-you-go usage pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Neon — scale-to-zero compute. Amazon Aurora Serverless — automatic capacity scaling.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Neon better than Amazon Aurora Serverless?

It depends on your requirements. Neon is a strong fit for serverless apps and preview environments on Postgres, while Amazon Aurora Serverless suits AWS workloads with variable traffic.

### Is Neon free to use?

Yes, you can start with Neon for free. Pricing model: Free tier, then usage-based pricing.

### Is Amazon Aurora Serverless free to use?

Amazon Aurora Serverless does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Pay-as-you-go usage pricing.

### Can I self-host Neon or Amazon Aurora Serverless?

Neon is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Amazon Aurora Serverless is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Neon and Amazon Aurora Serverless?

Neon: cold starts after scaling to zero; some Postgres extensions unavailable. Amazon Aurora Serverless: AWS only; pricing can be complex to estimate.

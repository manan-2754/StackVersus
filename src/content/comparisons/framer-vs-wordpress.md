## Detailed Breakdown

### Framer

_Design-first website builder_

**Pros:**

- Canvas that feels like a design tool
- Built-in animations
- Fast publishing

**Cons:**

- Less CMS depth than Webflow
- Less control over generated code

---

### WordPress

_Open-source CMS powering a large share of the web_

**Pros:**

- Huge plugin and theme ecosystem
- Open source
- Host anywhere

**Cons:**

- Plugin security and maintenance burden
- Performance tuning needed

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Framer — design-first website builder. WordPress — open-source CMS powering a large share of the web.
- Licensing differs: Framer is proprietary while WordPress is open source.
- **Deployment:** Framer — managed cloud. WordPress — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Framer — free tier with paid plans. WordPress — free and open source.
- **Signature strength:** Framer — canvas that feels like a design tool. WordPress — huge plugin and theme ecosystem.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Framer better than WordPress?

It depends on your requirements. Framer is a strong fit for designers who want fast, animated marketing sites, while WordPress suits sites needing maximum flexibility and plugins.

### Is Framer free to use?

Yes, you can start with Framer for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is WordPress free to use?

Yes, you can start with WordPress for free. Pricing model: Free and open source.

### Can I self-host Framer or WordPress?

Framer is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. WordPress can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Framer and WordPress?

Framer: less CMS depth than Webflow; less control over generated code. WordPress: plugin security and maintenance burden; performance tuning needed.

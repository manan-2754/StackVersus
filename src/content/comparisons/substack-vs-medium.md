## Detailed Breakdown

### Substack

_Newsletter platform with a built-in network_

**Pros:**

- Free to start
- Built-in discovery network
- Very simple

**Cons:**

- 10% fee on paid subscriptions
- Limited customization

---

### Medium

_Open publishing platform with a reader network_

**Pros:**

- Built-in audience
- Partner Program earnings
- Clean reading experience

**Cons:**

- You do not own the audience relationship
- Paywall can limit reach

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Substack — newsletter platform with a built-in network. Medium — open publishing platform with a reader network.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Substack — Free; 10% fee on paid subscriptions. Medium — free to publish; paid reader membership.
- **Signature strength:** Substack — free to start. Medium — built-in audience.

Yes, you can start with Medium for free. Pricing model: Free to publish; paid reader membership.

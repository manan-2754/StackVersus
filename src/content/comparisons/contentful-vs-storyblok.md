## Detailed Breakdown

### Contentful

_Enterprise API-first content platform_

**Pros:**

- Mature enterprise governance features
- Strong localization support
- Large app marketplace

**Cons:**

- Paid tiers get expensive quickly
- Content model limits on lower plans

---

### Storyblok

_Headless CMS with a visual editor_

**Pros:**

- Visual editor with live preview
- Component-based content blocks
- Good localization features

**Cons:**

- Proprietary cloud only
- Pricing scales with seats and traffic

- **Positioning:** Contentful — enterprise API-first content platform. Storyblok — headless CMS with a visual editor.

- **Pricing:** Contentful — free tier with paid plans. Storyblok — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Contentful — mature enterprise governance features. Storyblok — Visual editor with live preview.

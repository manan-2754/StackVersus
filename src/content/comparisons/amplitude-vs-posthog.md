## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Amplitude better than PostHog?

It depends on your requirements. Amplitude is a strong fit for larger product orgs needing behavioral analytics and experimentation, while PostHog suits engineering-led teams wanting analytics, replay and flags in one tool.

### Is Amplitude free to use?

Yes, you can start with Amplitude for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is PostHog free to use?

Yes, you can start with PostHog for free. Pricing model: Free tier, then usage-based pricing.

### Can I self-host Amplitude or PostHog?

Amplitude is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. PostHog can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Amplitude and PostHog?

Amplitude: steeper learning curve; premium tiers are expensive. PostHog: breadth means some tools are less deep; self-hosting is only supported at small scale.

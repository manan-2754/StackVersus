## Detailed Breakdown

### MongoDB

_Document database with flexible schemas_

**Pros:**

- Flexible JSON-like documents
- Atlas managed service
- Built-in horizontal sharding

**Cons:**

- SSPL license
- Joins are less natural than in SQL

---

### MariaDB

_Community-developed fork of MySQL_

**Pros:**

- MySQL compatible
- Additional storage engines
- Open governance

**Cons:**

- Gradually diverging from MySQL
- Fewer managed-service options

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** MongoDB — document database with flexible schemas. MariaDB — community-developed fork of MySQL.
- Licensing differs: MongoDB is source-available while MariaDB is open source.
- **Pricing:** MongoDB — free community server; paid Atlas cloud. MariaDB — free and open source.
- **Signature strength:** MongoDB — flexible JSON-like documents. MariaDB — MySQL compatible.

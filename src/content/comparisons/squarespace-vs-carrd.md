## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Squarespace better than Carrd?

It depends on your requirements. Squarespace is a strong fit for creatives and small businesses wanting polished templates, while Carrd suits landing pages and personal profiles.

### Is Squarespace free to use?

Squarespace does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Is Carrd free to use?

Yes, you can start with Carrd for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Squarespace or Carrd?

Squarespace is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Carrd is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Squarespace and Carrd?

Squarespace: limited customization; no free plan. Carrd: one-page focus; limited features.

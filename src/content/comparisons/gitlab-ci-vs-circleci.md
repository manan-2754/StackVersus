# GitLab CI vs CircleCI: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> GitLab CI is the better pick for teams using GitLab for source control. CircleCI is the better pick for fast, configurable cloud CI across code hosts.

## Detailed Breakdown

### GitLab CI

_CI/CD integrated into the GitLab DevSecOps platform_

**Pros:**

- Tightly integrated with GitLab
- Advanced pipelines with DAGs and child pipelines
- Self-hostable runners and instances

**Cons:**

- Best experience requires GitLab
- Advanced features in paid tiers

---

### CircleCI

_Cloud CI/CD platform focused on speed_

**Pros:**

- Fast builds with caching and parallelism
- Orbs for reusable configuration
- Supports GitHub, GitLab and Bitbucket

**Cons:**

- Credit-based pricing can be hard to predict
- Separate tool from your code host

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** GitLab CI — CI/CD integrated into the GitLab DevSecOps platform. CircleCI — cloud CI/CD platform focused on speed.
- Licensing differs: GitLab CI is open-core while CircleCI is proprietary.
- **Deployment:** GitLab CI — self-hosted or managed cloud. CircleCI — managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** GitLab CI — free tier with paid plans. CircleCI — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** GitLab CI — tightly integrated with GitLab. CircleCI — fast builds with caching and parallelism.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is GitLab CI better than CircleCI?

It depends on your requirements. GitLab CI is a strong fit for teams using GitLab for source control, while CircleCI suits fast, configurable cloud CI across code hosts.

### Is GitLab CI free to use?

Yes, you can start with GitLab CI for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is CircleCI free to use?

Yes, you can start with CircleCI for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host GitLab CI or CircleCI?

GitLab CI can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud. CircleCI is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of GitLab CI and CircleCI?

GitLab CI: best experience requires GitLab; advanced features in paid tiers. CircleCI: credit-based pricing can be hard to predict; separate tool from your code host.

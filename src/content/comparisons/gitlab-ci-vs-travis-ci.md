# GitLab CI vs Travis CI: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> GitLab CI is the better pick for teams using GitLab for source control. Travis CI is the better pick for simple CI for small projects.

---

## Detailed Breakdown

### GitLab CI

_CI/CD integrated into the GitLab DevSecOps platform_

**Pros:**

- Tightly integrated with GitLab
- Advanced pipelines with DAGs and child pipelines
- Self-hostable runners and instances

**Cons:**

- Best experience requires GitLab
- Advanced features in paid tiers

---

### Travis CI

_Hosted continuous integration service_

**Pros:**

- Simple YAML configuration
- Long history with open-source projects
- Build matrix support

**Cons:**

- No longer offers unlimited free builds for open source
- Smaller ecosystem than newer tools

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** GitLab CI — CI/CD integrated into the GitLab DevSecOps platform. Travis CI — hosted continuous integration service.
- Licensing differs: GitLab CI is open-core while Travis CI is proprietary.
- **Deployment:** GitLab CI — self-hosted or managed cloud. Travis CI — managed cloud.
- GitLab CI can be started for free, while Travis CI requires a paid plan. Travis CI pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** GitLab CI — tightly integrated with GitLab. Travis CI — simple YAML configuration.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is GitLab CI better than Travis CI?

It depends on your requirements. GitLab CI is a strong fit for teams using GitLab for source control, while Travis CI suits simple CI for small projects.

### Is GitLab CI free to use?

Yes, you can start with GitLab CI for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Travis CI free to use?

Travis CI does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host GitLab CI or Travis CI?

GitLab CI can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud. Travis CI is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of GitLab CI and Travis CI?

GitLab CI: best experience requires GitLab; advanced features in paid tiers. Travis CI: no longer offers unlimited free builds for open source; smaller ecosystem than newer tools.

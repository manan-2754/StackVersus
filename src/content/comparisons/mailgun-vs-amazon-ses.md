### Amazon SES

_Low-cost cloud email sending on AWS_

**Pros:**

- Very low cost per email
- Scales massively
- AWS integration

**Cons:**

- Bare-bones interface
- You manage reputation and setup

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Mailgun — email API service by Sinch. Amazon SES — low-cost cloud email sending on AWS.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Mailgun — free tier with paid plans. Amazon SES — free tier, then usage-based pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Mailgun — powerful routing and inbound parsing. Amazon SES — very low cost per email.

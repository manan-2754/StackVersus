## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is IFTTT better than Microsoft Power Automate?

It depends on your requirements. IFTTT is a strong fit for personal and smart-home automations, while Microsoft Power Automate suits organizations on Microsoft 365.

### Is IFTTT free to use?

Yes, you can start with IFTTT for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Microsoft Power Automate free to use?

Microsoft Power Automate does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host IFTTT or Microsoft Power Automate?

IFTTT is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Microsoft Power Automate is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of IFTTT and Microsoft Power Automate?

IFTTT: limited for business workflows; restricted free plan. Microsoft Power Automate: clunky outside the Microsoft ecosystem; licensing complexity.

## Detailed Breakdown

### PagerDuty

_Incident response and on-call management platform_

**Pros:**

- Robust on-call scheduling and escalation
- Hundreds of integrations
- Event intelligence features

**Cons:**

- Expensive per user
- Advanced features in higher tiers

---

### Opsgenie

_Atlassian on-call and alert management_

**Pros:**

- Tight Jira integration
- Flexible alert routing
- Affordable on-call

**Cons:**

- Atlassian stopped selling Opsgenie in favor of Jira Service Management
- Limited future roadmap

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** PagerDuty — incident response and on-call management platform. Opsgenie — Atlassian on-call and alert management.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** PagerDuty — free tier with paid plans. Opsgenie — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** PagerDuty — robust on-call scheduling and escalation. Opsgenie — tight Jira integration.

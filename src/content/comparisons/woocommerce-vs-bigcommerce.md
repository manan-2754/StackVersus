## Detailed Breakdown

### WooCommerce

_Open-source e-commerce for WordPress_

**Pros:**

- Free core plugin
- Full control
- Huge extension ecosystem

**Cons:**

- Hosting and maintenance are on you
- Performance tuning needed

---

### BigCommerce

_SaaS commerce platform for growing brands_

**Pros:**

- No extra transaction fees
- Many built-in features
- Headless commerce support

**Cons:**

- Plan upgrades tied to revenue
- Smaller app store

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** WooCommerce — open-source e-commerce for WordPress. BigCommerce — SaaS commerce platform for growing brands.
- Licensing differs: WooCommerce is open source while BigCommerce is proprietary.
- **Deployment:** WooCommerce — self-hosted. BigCommerce — managed cloud.
- WooCommerce can be started for free, while BigCommerce requires a paid plan. BigCommerce pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** WooCommerce — free core plugin. BigCommerce — no extra transaction fees.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is WooCommerce better than BigCommerce?

It depends on your requirements. WooCommerce is a strong fit for WordPress sites adding a store, while BigCommerce suits mid-market brands needing built-in features.

### Is WooCommerce free to use?

Yes, you can start with WooCommerce for free. Pricing model: Free and open source.

### Is BigCommerce free to use?

BigCommerce does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host WooCommerce or BigCommerce?

WooCommerce can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted. BigCommerce is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of WooCommerce and BigCommerce?

WooCommerce: hosting and maintenance are on you; performance tuning needed. BigCommerce: plan upgrades tied to revenue; smaller app store.

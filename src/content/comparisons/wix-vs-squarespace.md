## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Wix — drag-and-drop website builder. Squarespace — design-led website builder.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Wix can be started for free, while Squarespace requires a paid plan. Squarespace pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Wix — very easy editor. Squarespace — beautiful templates.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Wix better than Squarespace?

It depends on your requirements. Wix is a strong fit for small businesses wanting an all-in-one site, while Squarespace suits creatives and small businesses wanting polished templates.

### Is Wix free to use?

Yes, you can start with Wix for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Squarespace free to use?

Squarespace does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host Wix or Squarespace?

Wix is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Squarespace is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Wix and Squarespace?

Wix: switching templates is difficult; slower page performance. Squarespace: limited customization; no free plan.

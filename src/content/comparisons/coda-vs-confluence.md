## Detailed Breakdown

### Coda

_All-in-one doc that works like an app_

**Pros:**

- Powerful formulas and Packs
- Docs that behave like apps
- Built-in automations

**Cons:**

- Learning curve for formulas
- Smaller community

---

### Confluence

_Team wiki by Atlassian_

**Pros:**

- Deep Jira integration
- Structured spaces and permissions
- Enterprise features

**Cons:**

- Editor can feel clunky
- Search quality varies

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Coda — all-in-one doc that works like an app. Confluence — team wiki by Atlassian.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Coda — free tier with paid plans. Confluence — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Coda — powerful formulas and Packs. Confluence — deep Jira integration.

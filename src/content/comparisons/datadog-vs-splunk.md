## Detailed Breakdown

### Datadog

_Monitoring and security platform for cloud apps_

**Pros:**

- Hundreds of integrations
- Unified metrics, logs, APM and security
- Polished dashboards

**Cons:**

- Costs can escalate quickly
- Complex per-product pricing

---

### Splunk

_Enterprise data and observability platform by Cisco_

**Pros:**

- Powerful search processing language
- Strong security analytics
- Enterprise-grade scale

**Cons:**

- Very expensive at high ingest volumes
- Steep learning curve

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Datadog — monitoring and security platform for cloud apps. Splunk — enterprise data and observability platform by Cisco.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Deployment:** Datadog — managed cloud. Splunk — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- Datadog can be started for free, while Splunk requires a paid plan. Splunk pricing: custom enterprise pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Datadog — hundreds of integrations. Splunk — powerful search processing language.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Datadog better than Splunk?

It depends on your requirements. Datadog is a strong fit for organizations wanting one platform for metrics, logs and traces, while Splunk suits enterprises with heavy log analytics and security needs.

### Is Datadog free to use?

Yes, you can start with Datadog for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Splunk free to use?

Splunk does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Custom enterprise pricing.

### Can I self-host Datadog or Splunk?

Datadog is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Splunk can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Datadog and Splunk?

Datadog: costs can escalate quickly; complex per-product pricing. Splunk: very expensive at high ingest volumes; steep learning curve.

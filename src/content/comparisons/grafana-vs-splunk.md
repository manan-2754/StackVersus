# Grafana vs Splunk: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Grafana is the better pick for teams wanting open-source dashboards across many data sources. Splunk is the better pick for enterprises with heavy log analytics and security needs.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Grafana

_Open-source observability and dashboards_

**Pros:**

- Connects to many data sources
- Open-source LGTM stack (Loki, Grafana, Tempo, Mimir)
- Grafana Cloud free tier

**Cons:**

- Self-hosting the full stack is complex
- Requires assembling multiple components

---

### Splunk

_Enterprise data and observability platform by Cisco_

**Pros:**

- Powerful search processing language
- Strong security analytics
- Enterprise-grade scale

**Cons:**

- Very expensive at high ingest volumes
- Steep learning curve

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Grafana — open-source observability and dashboards. Splunk — enterprise data and observability platform by Cisco.
- Licensing differs: Grafana is open source while Splunk is proprietary.
- Grafana can be started for free, while Splunk requires a paid plan. Splunk pricing: custom enterprise pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Grafana — connects to many data sources. Splunk — powerful search processing language.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Grafana better than Splunk?

It depends on your requirements. Grafana is a strong fit for teams wanting open-source dashboards across many data sources, while Splunk suits enterprises with heavy log analytics and security needs.

### Is Grafana free to use?

Yes, you can start with Grafana for free. Pricing model: Free open source; paid managed cloud.

### Is Splunk free to use?

Splunk does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Custom enterprise pricing.

### Can I self-host Grafana or Splunk?

Grafana can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud. Splunk can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Grafana and Splunk?

Grafana: self-hosting the full stack is complex; requires assembling multiple components. Splunk: very expensive at high ingest volumes; steep learning curve.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Contentful

_Enterprise API-first content platform_

**Pros:**

- Mature enterprise governance features
- Strong localization support
- Large app marketplace

**Cons:**

- Paid tiers get expensive quickly
- Content model limits on lower plans

---

### Directus

_Data platform that adds an API and admin app to any SQL database_

**Pros:**

- Works on top of existing SQL schemas
- Instant REST and GraphQL APIs
- No-code admin app

**Cons:**

- BSL license requires a paid license for larger organizations
- Admin customization has a learning curve

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Contentful — enterprise API-first content platform. Directus — data platform that adds an API and admin app to any SQL database.
- Licensing differs: Contentful is proprietary while Directus is source-available.
- **Deployment:** Contentful — managed cloud. Directus — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Contentful — free tier with paid plans. Directus — free open source; paid managed cloud.
- **Signature strength:** Contentful — mature enterprise governance features. Directus — works on top of existing SQL schemas.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Contentful better than Directus?

It depends on your requirements. Contentful is a strong fit for large organizations publishing content across many channels, while Directus suits teams that want a CMS on top of an existing SQL database.

### Is Contentful free to use?

Yes, you can start with Contentful for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Directus free to use?

Yes, you can start with Directus for free. Pricing model: Free open source; paid managed cloud.

### Can I self-host Contentful or Directus?

Contentful is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Directus can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Contentful and Directus?

Contentful: paid tiers get expensive quickly; content model limits on lower plans. Directus: BSL license requires a paid license for larger organizations; admin customization has a learning curve.

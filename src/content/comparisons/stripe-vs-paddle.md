# Stripe vs Paddle: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict
> Choose Stripe if you want full control over your checkout experience and have the resources to manage global tax compliance. Choose Paddle if you prefer a Merchant of Record model that handles all tax, VAT, and compliance liabilities automatically.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Stripe
*The complete payments platform for developers*

**Pros:**
- Extensive API and developer documentation
- Massive ecosystem of integrations
- Granular control over checkout and billing logic

**Cons:**
- User is responsible for tax compliance and nexus tracking
- Requires more engineering effort to implement
- Additional costs for advanced features like Stripe Tax

---

### Paddle
*The Merchant of Record for SaaS and software*

**Pros:**
- Acts as Merchant of Record, handling global tax compliance
- Simplified financial reporting and accounting
- Faster time-to-market for international sales

**Cons:**
- Higher transaction fees compared to Stripe
- Less control over the checkout flow and customer data
- Vendor lock-in makes migrating away more complex

---

## Key Differences
- Stripe is a payment processor; Paddle is a Merchant of Record (MoR).
- Stripe requires you to manage your own tax nexus; Paddle assumes the legal liability for tax collection and remittance.
- Stripe offers lower base transaction fees but higher operational overhead; Paddle charges a premium for all-in-one compliance.
- Stripe provides maximum flexibility for custom billing models; Paddle is optimized for standardized SaaS subscription workflows.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to hire a tax accountant if I use Paddle?
Generally no, as Paddle acts as the Merchant of Record and handles the calculation, collection, and remittance of global sales tax and VAT.

### Can I switch from Stripe to Paddle later?
Yes, but it involves migrating customer payment data, which can be complex and may require coordination with support teams to ensure continuity.

### Which platform is cheaper for a high-volume business?
Stripe is typically more cost-effective at scale, provided you have the internal resources to manage the tax and compliance overhead that Paddle otherwise automates.

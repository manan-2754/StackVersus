## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Shortcut — project management for software teams (formerly Clubhouse). YouTrack — issue tracker and project management by JetBrains.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Deployment:** Shortcut — managed cloud. YouTrack — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Shortcut — free tier with paid plans. YouTrack — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Shortcut — stories, epics and iterations. YouTrack — powerful search query language.

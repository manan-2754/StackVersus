## Detailed Breakdown

### Railway

_Infrastructure platform for deploying apps and databases_

**Pros:**

- Very fast setup from a repository
- One-click databases
- Usage-based pricing

**Cons:**

- Costs less predictable than flat plans
- Less control than raw cloud infrastructure

---

### Heroku

_Pioneering platform as a service by Salesforce_

**Pros:**

- Very simple git push deploys
- Mature add-on marketplace
- Well documented

**Cons:**

- No free dynos since 2022
- Higher cost than newer platforms

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Railway — infrastructure platform for deploying apps and databases. Heroku — pioneering platform as a service by Salesforce.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Railway — pay-as-you-go usage pricing. Heroku — paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Railway — very fast setup from a repository. Heroku — very simple git push deploys.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Railway better than Heroku?

It depends on your requirements. Railway is a strong fit for quick deployment of full-stack apps and side projects, while Heroku suits teams that want a mature, simple PaaS.

### Is Railway free to use?

Railway does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Pay-as-you-go usage pricing.

### Is Heroku free to use?

Heroku does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host Railway or Heroku?

Railway is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Heroku is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Railway and Heroku?

Railway: costs less predictable than flat plans; less control than raw cloud infrastructure. Heroku: no free dynos since 2022; higher cost than newer platforms.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Auth0

_Customer identity platform by Okta_

**Pros:**

- Extensive OIDC and SAML support
- Actions for custom auth logic
- Enterprise SSO and MFA

**Cons:**

- Pricing grows steeply with monthly active users
- Complex configuration

---

### FusionAuth

_Developer-focused auth you can self-host_

**Pros:**

- Free self-hosted Community edition
- Runs anywhere including on-premises
- Broad protocol support

**Cons:**

- Not open source
- Self-hosting adds operational work

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Auth0 — customer identity platform by Okta. FusionAuth — developer-focused auth you can self-host.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Deployment:** Auth0 — managed cloud. FusionAuth — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Auth0 — free tier with paid plans. FusionAuth — free Community edition; paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Auth0 — extensive OIDC and SAML support. FusionAuth — free self-hosted Community edition.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Auth0 better than FusionAuth?

It depends on your requirements. Auth0 is a strong fit for enterprises needing flexible, standards-based identity, while FusionAuth suits teams wanting full control with self-hosted auth.

### Is Auth0 free to use?

Yes, you can start with Auth0 for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is FusionAuth free to use?

Yes, you can start with FusionAuth for free. Pricing model: Free Community edition; paid plans.

### Can I self-host Auth0 or FusionAuth?

Auth0 is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. FusionAuth can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Auth0 and FusionAuth?

Auth0: pricing grows steeply with monthly active users; complex configuration. FusionAuth: not open source; self-hosting adds operational work.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Paddle

_Merchant of record for software companies_

**Pros:**

- Acts as merchant of record
- Handles global sales tax and VAT
- Subscription billing built in

**Cons:**

- Higher fees than a pure processor
- Less flexibility for custom flows

---

### Braintree

_PayPal-owned payment gateway for developers_

**Pros:**

- Native PayPal and Venmo support
- Vault and recurring billing
- Mature SDKs

**Cons:**

- Smaller product suite than Stripe
- Slower feature development

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Paddle — merchant of record for software companies. Braintree — PayPal-owned payment gateway for developers.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Paddle — percentage plus fixed fee per transaction. Braintree — per-transaction fees, no monthly fee.
- **Signature strength:** Paddle — acts as merchant of record. Braintree — native PayPal and Venmo support.

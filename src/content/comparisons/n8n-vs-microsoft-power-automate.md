### Is Microsoft Power Automate free to use?

Microsoft Power Automate does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

n8n can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud. Microsoft Power Automate is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of n8n and Microsoft Power Automate?

n8n: fair-code license limits some commercial uses; more technical than Zapier. Microsoft Power Automate: clunky outside the Microsoft ecosystem; licensing complexity.

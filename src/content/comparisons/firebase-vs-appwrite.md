# Firebase vs Appwrite: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Firebase is the better pick for mobile and web apps needing realtime sync quickly. Appwrite is the better pick for teams that want a self-hostable Firebase alternative.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Firebase

_Google’s app development platform_

**Pros:**

- Realtime NoSQL databases
- Deep Google Cloud and analytics integration
- Excellent mobile SDKs

**Cons:**

- Vendor lock-in
- NoSQL querying limitations

---

### Appwrite

_Open-source backend platform for web and mobile_

**Pros:**

- Self-host with Docker
- Auth, databases, storage and functions
- Many client SDKs

**Cons:**

- Smaller community than Firebase
- Cloud offering is younger

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Firebase — Google’s app development platform. Appwrite — open-source backend platform for web and mobile.
- Licensing differs: Firebase is proprietary while Appwrite is open source.
- **Deployment:** Firebase — managed cloud. Appwrite — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Firebase — free tier, then usage-based pricing. Appwrite — free open source; paid managed cloud.
- **Signature strength:** Firebase — realtime NoSQL databases. Appwrite — self-host with Docker.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Firebase better than Appwrite?

It depends on your requirements. Firebase is a strong fit for mobile and web apps needing realtime sync quickly, while Appwrite suits teams that want a self-hostable Firebase alternative.

### Is Firebase free to use?

Yes, you can start with Firebase for free. Pricing model: Free tier, then usage-based pricing.

### Is Appwrite free to use?

Yes, you can start with Appwrite for free. Pricing model: Free open source; paid managed cloud.

### Can I self-host Firebase or Appwrite?

Firebase is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Appwrite can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Firebase and Appwrite?

Firebase: vendor lock-in; NoSQL querying limitations. Appwrite: smaller community than Firebase; cloud offering is younger.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Vercel

_Frontend cloud from the creators of Next.js_

**Pros:**

- Best-in-class Next.js support
- Preview deployments for every branch
- Global edge network

**Cons:**

- Usage-based costs can spike
- Some features lock you into the platform

---

### Heroku

_Pioneering platform as a service by Salesforce_

**Pros:**

- Very simple git push deploys
- Mature add-on marketplace
- Well documented

**Cons:**

- No free dynos since 2022
- Higher cost than newer platforms

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Vercel — frontend cloud from the creators of Next.js. Heroku — pioneering platform as a service by Salesforce.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Vercel can be started for free, while Heroku requires a paid plan. Heroku pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Vercel — best-in-class Next.js support. Heroku — very simple git push deploys.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Vercel better than Heroku?

It depends on your requirements. Vercel is a strong fit for Next.js and frontend teams wanting zero-config deploys, while Heroku suits teams that want a mature, simple PaaS.

### Is Vercel free to use?

Yes, you can start with Vercel for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Heroku free to use?

Heroku does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host Vercel or Heroku?

Vercel is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Heroku is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Vercel and Heroku?

Vercel: usage-based costs can spike; some features lock you into the platform. Heroku: no free dynos since 2022; higher cost than newer platforms.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Asana

_Work management platform for teams_

**Pros:**

- Timelines, portfolios and goals
- Clean interface
- Strong integrations

**Cons:**

- Expensive for larger teams
- Single assignee per task

---

### Basecamp

_Opinionated project management and team communication_

**Pros:**

- Flat-price plan option
- Combines chat, docs and to-dos
- Calm, simple design

**Cons:**

- Limited reporting
- Fewer integrations

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Asana — work management platform for teams. Basecamp — opinionated project management and team communication.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Asana — free tier with paid plans. Basecamp — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Asana — timelines, portfolios and goals. Basecamp — flat-price plan option.

## Detailed Breakdown

### New Relic

_Full-stack observability platform_

**Pros:**

- Free tier with monthly data ingest included
- Unified telemetry database
- Strong APM heritage

**Cons:**

- Per-user pricing for full platform users
- UI can feel complex

---

### Honeycomb

_Observability for high-cardinality event data_

**Pros:**

- Fast queries on high-cardinality data
- BubbleUp outlier analysis
- OpenTelemetry-first

**Cons:**

- Different mental model from metrics dashboards
- Less breadth than all-in-one suites

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** New Relic — full-stack observability platform. Honeycomb — observability for high-cardinality event data.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** New Relic — free tier, then usage-based pricing. Honeycomb — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** New Relic — free tier with monthly data ingest included. Honeycomb — fast queries on high-cardinality data.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is New Relic better than Honeycomb?

It depends on your requirements. New Relic is a strong fit for teams wanting full-stack observability with usage-based pricing, while Honeycomb suits teams debugging distributed systems with OpenTelemetry.

### Is New Relic free to use?

Yes, you can start with New Relic for free. Pricing model: Free tier, then usage-based pricing.

### Is Honeycomb free to use?

Yes, you can start with Honeycomb for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host New Relic or Honeycomb?

New Relic is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Honeycomb is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of New Relic and Honeycomb?

New Relic: per-user pricing for full platform users; UI can feel complex. Honeycomb: different mental model from metrics dashboards; less breadth than all-in-one suites.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Jira better than Plane?

It depends on your requirements. Jira is a strong fit for large organizations with complex workflows, while Plane suits teams wanting a self-hostable Linear or Jira alternative.

### Is Jira free to use?

Yes, you can start with Jira for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Plane free to use?

Yes, you can start with Plane for free. Pricing model: Free open source; paid managed cloud.

### Can I self-host Jira or Plane?

Jira is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Plane can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Jira and Plane?

Jira: can feel slow and complex; significant admin overhead. Plane: younger product; fewer integrations.

# New Relic vs Grafana: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

## Detailed Breakdown

### New Relic

_Full-stack observability platform_

**Pros:**

- Free tier with monthly data ingest included
- Unified telemetry database
- Strong APM heritage

**Cons:**

- Per-user pricing for full platform users
- UI can feel complex

---

### Grafana

_Open-source observability and dashboards_

**Pros:**

- Connects to many data sources
- Open-source LGTM stack (Loki, Grafana, Tempo, Mimir)
- Grafana Cloud free tier

**Cons:**

- Self-hosting the full stack is complex
- Requires assembling multiple components

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** New Relic — full-stack observability platform. Grafana — open-source observability and dashboards.
- Licensing differs: New Relic is proprietary while Grafana is open source.
- **Deployment:** New Relic — managed cloud. Grafana — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** New Relic — free tier, then usage-based pricing. Grafana — free open source; paid managed cloud.
- **Signature strength:** New Relic — free tier with monthly data ingest included. Grafana — connects to many data sources.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is New Relic better than Grafana?

It depends on your requirements. New Relic is a strong fit for teams wanting full-stack observability with usage-based pricing, while Grafana suits teams wanting open-source dashboards across many data sources.

### Is New Relic free to use?

Yes, you can start with New Relic for free. Pricing model: Free tier, then usage-based pricing.

### Is Grafana free to use?

Yes, you can start with Grafana for free. Pricing model: Free open source; paid managed cloud.

### Can I self-host New Relic or Grafana?

New Relic is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Grafana can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of New Relic and Grafana?

New Relic: per-user pricing for full platform users; UI can feel complex. Grafana: self-hosting the full stack is complex; requires assembling multiple components.

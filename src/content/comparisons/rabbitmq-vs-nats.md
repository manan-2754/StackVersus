## Detailed Breakdown

### RabbitMQ

_Widely deployed open-source message broker_

**Pros:**

- Flexible routing
- Multiple protocols
- Mature and well documented

**Cons:**

- Lower throughput than Kafka
- Clustering needs tuning

---

### NATS

_Lightweight, high-performance messaging system_

**Pros:**

- Very lightweight
- JetStream persistence
- Simple operations

**Cons:**

- Smaller ecosystem
- Fewer connectors

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** RabbitMQ — widely deployed open-source message broker. NATS — lightweight, high-performance messaging system.
- Both share the same licensing model (open source), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** RabbitMQ — free and open source. NATS — free and open source.
- **Signature strength:** RabbitMQ — flexible routing. NATS — very lightweight.

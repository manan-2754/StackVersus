# GitLab CI vs Buildkite: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> GitLab CI is the better pick for teams using GitLab for source control. Buildkite is the better pick for large engineering teams running CI on their own compute.

## Detailed Breakdown

### GitLab CI

_CI/CD integrated into the GitLab DevSecOps platform_

**Pros:**

- Tightly integrated with GitLab
- Advanced pipelines with DAGs and child pipelines
- Self-hostable runners and instances

**Cons:**

- Best experience requires GitLab
- Advanced features in paid tiers

---

### Buildkite

_Hybrid CI/CD with agents on your own infrastructure_

**Pros:**

- Agents run on your own infrastructure
- Scales to very large pipelines
- Dynamic pipeline generation

**Cons:**

- You manage the agent infrastructure
- Per-user pricing

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** GitLab CI — CI/CD integrated into the GitLab DevSecOps platform. Buildkite — hybrid CI/CD with agents on your own infrastructure.
- Both share the same licensing model (open-core), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Deployment:** GitLab CI — self-hosted or managed cloud. Buildkite — managed control plane with self-hosted agents.
- **Pricing:** GitLab CI — free tier with paid plans. Buildkite — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** GitLab CI — tightly integrated with GitLab. Buildkite — agents run on your own infrastructure.

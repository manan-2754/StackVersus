## Detailed Breakdown

### Ghost

_Open-source publishing platform for newsletters and memberships_

**Pros:**

- Open source
- Built-in memberships with no platform revenue share
- SEO-friendly

**Cons:**

- Self-hosting requires work
- Managed Ghost(Pro) is paid

---

### Medium

_Open publishing platform with a reader network_

**Pros:**

- Built-in audience
- Partner Program earnings
- Clean reading experience

**Cons:**

- You do not own the audience relationship
- Paywall can limit reach

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Ghost — open-source publishing platform for newsletters and memberships. Medium — open publishing platform with a reader network.
- Licensing differs: Ghost is open source while Medium is proprietary.
- **Deployment:** Ghost — self-hosted or managed cloud. Medium — managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Ghost — free self-hosted; paid Ghost(Pro). Medium — free to publish; paid reader membership.
- **Signature strength:** Ghost — open source. Medium — built-in audience.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Ghost better than Medium?

It depends on your requirements. Ghost is a strong fit for independent publishers wanting ownership, while Medium suits writers wanting reach without running a site.

### Is Ghost free to use?

Yes, you can start with Ghost for free. Pricing model: Free self-hosted; paid Ghost(Pro).

### Is Medium free to use?

Yes, you can start with Medium for free. Pricing model: Free to publish; paid reader membership.

### Can I self-host Ghost or Medium?

Ghost can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud. Medium is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Ghost and Medium?

Ghost: self-hosting requires work; managed Ghost(Pro) is paid. Medium: you do not own the audience relationship; paywall can limit reach.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Braintree

_PayPal-owned payment gateway for developers_

**Pros:**

- Native PayPal and Venmo support
- Vault and recurring billing
- Mature SDKs

**Cons:**

- Smaller product suite than Stripe
- Slower feature development

---

### Adyen

_Enterprise payments platform_

**Pros:**

- One platform for online and in-person payments
- Strong global acquiring
- Advanced risk management

**Cons:**

- Aimed at high-volume merchants
- Less self-serve for small businesses

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Braintree — PayPal-owned payment gateway for developers. Adyen — enterprise payments platform.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Braintree can be started for free, while Adyen requires a paid plan. Adyen pricing: custom enterprise pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Braintree — native PayPal and Venmo support. Adyen — one platform for online and in-person payments.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Braintree better than Adyen?

It depends on your requirements. Braintree is a strong fit for merchants wanting cards, PayPal and Venmo in one gateway, while Adyen suits large enterprises processing payments globally.

### Is Braintree free to use?

Yes, you can start with Braintree for free. Pricing model: Per-transaction fees, no monthly fee.

### Is Adyen free to use?

Adyen does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Custom enterprise pricing.

### Can I self-host Braintree or Adyen?

Braintree is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Adyen is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Braintree and Adyen?

Braintree: smaller product suite than Stripe; slower feature development. Adyen: aimed at high-volume merchants; less self-serve for small businesses.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Prisma

_Type-safe ORM with a schema-first workflow_

**Pros:**

- Declarative schema and migrations
- Excellent autocompletion
- Prisma Studio data browser

**Cons:**

- Abstraction can limit complex SQL
- Heavier than query builders

---

### TypeORM

_Decorator-based ORM for TypeScript and JavaScript_

**Pros:**

- Active Record and Data Mapper patterns
- Supports many databases
- Popular with NestJS

**Cons:**

- Weaker type safety than newer ORMs
- Uneven maintenance history

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Prisma — type-safe ORM with a schema-first workflow. TypeORM — decorator-based ORM for TypeScript and JavaScript.
- Both share the same licensing model (open source), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Prisma — free and open source. TypeORM — free and open source.
- **Signature strength:** Prisma — declarative schema and migrations. TypeORM — active Record and Data Mapper patterns.

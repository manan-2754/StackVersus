- **Positioning:** Medium — open publishing platform with a reader network. Buttondown — minimalist newsletter tool.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Medium — free to publish; paid reader membership. Buttondown — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Medium — built-in audience. Buttondown — markdown-first editor.

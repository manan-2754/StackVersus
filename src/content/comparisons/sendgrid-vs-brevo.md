## Detailed Breakdown

### SendGrid

_Email delivery platform by Twilio_

**Pros:**

- Handles very high volume
- Marketing and transactional email in one platform
- Mature APIs

**Cons:**

- Shared IP deliverability varies
- Support can be slow on lower plans

---

### Brevo

_Marketing and transactional email platform (formerly Sendinblue)_

**Pros:**

- Marketing, SMS and transactional together
- Free plan with daily sends
- Built-in CRM

**Cons:**

- Brevo branding on the free plan
- Less developer-focused

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** SendGrid — email delivery platform by Twilio. Brevo — marketing and transactional email platform (formerly Sendinblue).
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Brevo can be started for free, while SendGrid requires a paid plan. SendGrid pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** SendGrid — handles very high volume. Brevo — marketing, SMS and transactional together.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is SendGrid better than Brevo?

It depends on your requirements. SendGrid is a strong fit for high-volume transactional and marketing email, while Brevo suits small businesses combining campaigns and transactional email.

### Is SendGrid free to use?

SendGrid does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Is Brevo free to use?

Yes, you can start with Brevo for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host SendGrid or Brevo?

SendGrid is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Brevo is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of SendGrid and Brevo?

SendGrid: shared IP deliverability varies; support can be slow on lower plans. Brevo: brevo branding on the free plan; less developer-focused.

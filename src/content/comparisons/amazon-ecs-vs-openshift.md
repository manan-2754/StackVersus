# Amazon ECS vs OpenShift: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

## Detailed Breakdown

### Amazon ECS

_Fully managed container orchestration on AWS_

**Pros:**

- Deep AWS integration
- Fargate for serverless containers
- No control plane fee

**Cons:**

- AWS only
- Less portable than Kubernetes

---

### OpenShift

_Enterprise Kubernetes platform by Red Hat_

**Pros:**

- Enterprise support
- Integrated CI/CD and developer console
- Security-focused defaults

**Cons:**

- Expensive subscriptions
- Opinionated platform

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Amazon ECS — fully managed container orchestration on AWS. OpenShift — enterprise Kubernetes platform by Red Hat.
- Licensing differs: Amazon ECS is proprietary while OpenShift is open-core.
- **Deployment:** Amazon ECS — managed cloud. OpenShift — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Amazon ECS — no extra charge; pay for EC2 or Fargate compute. OpenShift — custom enterprise pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Amazon ECS — deep AWS integration. OpenShift — enterprise support.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Amazon ECS better than OpenShift?

It depends on your requirements. Amazon ECS is a strong fit for AWS teams wanting simpler orchestration than Kubernetes, while OpenShift suits enterprises needing supported Kubernetes with developer tooling.

### Is Amazon ECS free to use?

Amazon ECS does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: No extra charge; pay for EC2 or Fargate compute.

### Is OpenShift free to use?

OpenShift does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Custom enterprise pricing.

### Can I self-host Amazon ECS or OpenShift?

Amazon ECS is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. OpenShift can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Amazon ECS and OpenShift?

Amazon ECS: AWS only; less portable than Kubernetes. OpenShift: expensive subscriptions; opinionated platform.

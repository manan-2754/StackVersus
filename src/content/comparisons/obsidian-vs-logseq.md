## Detailed Breakdown

### Obsidian

_Local-first Markdown knowledge base_

**Pros:**

- Plain Markdown files you own
- Huge plugin ecosystem
- Graph view and backlinks

**Cons:**

- Sync and Publish cost extra
- Limited real-time collaboration

---

### Logseq

_Open-source outliner for networked notes_

**Pros:**

- Block-based outliner
- Open source
- Local Markdown files

**Cons:**

- Database version is still maturing
- Can slow down with large graphs

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Obsidian — local-first Markdown knowledge base. Logseq — open-source outliner for networked notes.
- Licensing differs: Obsidian is proprietary while Logseq is open source.
- **Pricing:** Obsidian — free app; paid Sync and Publish. Logseq — free and open source.
- **Signature strength:** Obsidian — plain Markdown files you own. Logseq — block-based outliner.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Stripe

_Payments infrastructure for the internet_

**Pros:**

- Best-in-class API and documentation
- Broad suite including Billing, Connect and Tax
- Supports many payment methods

**Cons:**

- Tax compliance is on you unless you add Stripe Tax
- Account reviews can affect high-risk businesses

---

### PayPal

_Global online payments platform_

**Pros:**

- Huge consumer trust and reach
- Buyer accounts in many countries
- Simple checkout buttons

**Cons:**

- Higher fees on some transaction types
- Dispute and hold policies can frustrate sellers

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Stripe — payments infrastructure for the internet. PayPal — global online payments platform.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Stripe — per-transaction fees, no monthly fee. PayPal — per-transaction fees, no monthly fee.
- **Signature strength:** Stripe — best-in-class API and documentation. PayPal — huge consumer trust and reach.

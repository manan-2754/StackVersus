# Sanity vs Payload CMS: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Sanity is the better pick for structured content with real-time collaboration. Payload CMS is the better pick for developers who want a code-first CMS inside a Next.js app.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Sanity

_Composable content platform with a customizable React studio_

**Pros:**

- Highly customizable Sanity Studio
- Real-time collaborative editing
- GROQ query language

**Cons:**

- Content Lake backend is proprietary and hosted
- GROQ is another language to learn

---

### Payload CMS

_TypeScript-first headless CMS and app framework_

**Pros:**

- Configuration written entirely in TypeScript
- Installs directly into a Next.js app
- Fine-grained access control

**Cons:**

- Changes require developer involvement
- Smaller ecosystem than older CMSs

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Sanity — composable content platform with a customizable React studio. Payload CMS — TypeScript-first headless CMS and app framework.
- Licensing differs: Sanity is open-core while Payload CMS is open source.
- **Deployment:** Sanity — managed cloud. Payload CMS — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Sanity — free tier with paid plans. Payload CMS — free open source; paid managed cloud.
- **Signature strength:** Sanity — highly customizable Sanity Studio. Payload CMS — configuration written entirely in TypeScript.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Sanity better than Payload CMS?

It depends on your requirements. Sanity is a strong fit for structured content with real-time collaboration, while Payload CMS suits developers who want a code-first CMS inside a Next.js app.

### Is Sanity free to use?

Yes, you can start with Sanity for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Payload CMS free to use?

Yes, you can start with Payload CMS for free. Pricing model: Free open source; paid managed cloud.

### Can I self-host Sanity or Payload CMS?

Sanity is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Payload CMS can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Sanity and Payload CMS?

Sanity: content Lake backend is proprietary and hosted; GROQ is another language to learn. Payload CMS: changes require developer involvement; smaller ecosystem than older CMSs.

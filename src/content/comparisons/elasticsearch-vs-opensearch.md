# Elasticsearch vs OpenSearch: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Elasticsearch is the better pick for large-scale full-text search and log analytics. OpenSearch is the better pick for teams wanting Apache-licensed search, often on AWS.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Elasticsearch

_Distributed search and analytics engine_

**Pros:**

- Powerful queries and aggregations
- Scales to huge datasets
- Large ecosystem

**Cons:**

- Operationally complex
- Resource-intensive

---

### OpenSearch

_Community-driven fork of Elasticsearch_

**Pros:**

- Apache 2.0 license
- Managed option on AWS
- Dashboards and plugins included

**Cons:**

- Feature divergence from Elasticsearch
- Operational complexity

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Elasticsearch — distributed search and analytics engine. OpenSearch — community-driven fork of Elasticsearch.
- Both share the same licensing model (open source), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Elasticsearch — free open source; paid managed cloud. OpenSearch — free and open source.
- **Signature strength:** Elasticsearch — powerful queries and aggregations. OpenSearch — apache 2.0 license.

### Is OpenSearch free to use?

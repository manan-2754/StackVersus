## Detailed Breakdown

### Ghost

_Open-source publishing platform for newsletters and memberships_

**Pros:**

- Open source
- Built-in memberships with no platform revenue share
- SEO-friendly

**Cons:**

- Self-hosting requires work
- Managed Ghost(Pro) is paid

---

### Buttondown

_Minimalist newsletter tool_

**Pros:**

- Markdown-first editor
- API access
- Privacy-friendly

**Cons:**

- Fewer growth tools
- Small company

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Ghost — open-source publishing platform for newsletters and memberships. Buttondown — minimalist newsletter tool.
- Licensing differs: Ghost is open source while Buttondown is proprietary.
- **Deployment:** Ghost — self-hosted or managed cloud. Buttondown — managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Ghost — free self-hosted; paid Ghost(Pro). Buttondown — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Ghost — open source. Buttondown — markdown-first editor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Ghost better than Buttondown?

It depends on your requirements. Ghost is a strong fit for independent publishers wanting ownership, while Buttondown suits developers and writers wanting a simple newsletter.

### Is Ghost free to use?

Yes, you can start with Ghost for free. Pricing model: Free self-hosted; paid Ghost(Pro).

### Is Buttondown free to use?

Yes, you can start with Buttondown for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Ghost or Buttondown?

Ghost can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud. Buttondown is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Ghost and Buttondown?

Ghost: self-hosting requires work; managed Ghost(Pro) is paid. Buttondown: fewer growth tools; small company.

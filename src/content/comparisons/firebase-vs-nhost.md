# Firebase vs Nhost: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Firebase is the better pick for mobile and web apps needing realtime sync quickly. Nhost is the better pick for GraphQL-first apps on Postgres.

---

## Detailed Breakdown

### Firebase

_Google’s app development platform_

**Pros:**

- Realtime NoSQL databases
- Deep Google Cloud and analytics integration
- Excellent mobile SDKs

**Cons:**

- Vendor lock-in
- NoSQL querying limitations

---

### Nhost

_Open-source backend with Postgres and GraphQL_

**Pros:**

- Postgres with a Hasura GraphQL API
- Auth and storage included
- Self-hostable

**Cons:**

- GraphQL-centric
- Smaller ecosystem

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Firebase — Google’s app development platform. Nhost — open-source backend with Postgres and GraphQL.
- Licensing differs: Firebase is proprietary while Nhost is open source.
- **Deployment:** Firebase — managed cloud. Nhost — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Firebase — free tier, then usage-based pricing. Nhost — free open source; paid managed cloud.
- **Signature strength:** Firebase — realtime NoSQL databases. Nhost — Postgres with a Hasura GraphQL API.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Firebase better than Nhost?

It depends on your requirements. Firebase is a strong fit for mobile and web apps needing realtime sync quickly, while Nhost suits GraphQL-first apps on Postgres.

### Is Firebase free to use?

Yes, you can start with Firebase for free. Pricing model: Free tier, then usage-based pricing.

### Is Nhost free to use?

Yes, you can start with Nhost for free. Pricing model: Free open source; paid managed cloud.

### Can I self-host Firebase or Nhost?

Firebase is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Nhost can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Firebase and Nhost?

Firebase: vendor lock-in; NoSQL querying limitations. Nhost: GraphQL-centric; smaller ecosystem.

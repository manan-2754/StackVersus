# Render vs Heroku: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

## Detailed Breakdown

### Render

_Unified cloud for apps, databases and cron jobs_

**Pros:**

- Heroku-like simplicity
- Managed Postgres and key-value stores
- Background workers and cron jobs

**Cons:**

- Free instances spin down when idle
- Fewer regions than hyperscalers

---

### Heroku

_Pioneering platform as a service by Salesforce_

**Pros:**

- Very simple git push deploys
- Mature add-on marketplace
- Well documented

**Cons:**

- No free dynos since 2022
- Higher cost than newer platforms

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Render — unified cloud for apps, databases and cron jobs. Heroku — pioneering platform as a service by Salesforce.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Render can be started for free, while Heroku requires a paid plan. Heroku pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Render — Heroku-like simplicity. Heroku — very simple git push deploys.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Render better than Heroku?

It depends on your requirements. Render is a strong fit for full-stack apps needing web services, databases and background jobs, while Heroku suits teams that want a mature, simple PaaS.

### Is Render free to use?

Yes, you can start with Render for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Heroku free to use?

Heroku does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host Render or Heroku?

Render is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Heroku is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Render and Heroku?

Render: free instances spin down when idle; fewer regions than hyperscalers. Heroku: no free dynos since 2022; higher cost than newer platforms.

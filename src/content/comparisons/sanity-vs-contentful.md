## Detailed Breakdown

### Sanity

_Composable content platform with a customizable React studio_

**Pros:**

- Highly customizable Sanity Studio
- Real-time collaborative editing
- GROQ query language

**Cons:**

- Content Lake backend is proprietary and hosted
- GROQ is another language to learn

---

### Contentful

_Enterprise API-first content platform_

**Pros:**

- Mature enterprise governance features
- Strong localization support
- Large app marketplace

**Cons:**

- Paid tiers get expensive quickly
- Content model limits on lower plans

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Sanity — composable content platform with a customizable React studio. Contentful — enterprise API-first content platform.
- Licensing differs: Sanity is open-core while Contentful is proprietary.
- **Pricing:** Sanity — free tier with paid plans. Contentful — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Sanity — highly customizable Sanity Studio. Contentful — mature enterprise governance features.

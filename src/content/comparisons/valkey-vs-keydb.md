## Detailed Breakdown

### Valkey

_Open-source Redis fork backed by the Linux Foundation_

**Pros:**

- Redis-compatible
- BSD license
- Backed by major cloud vendors

**Cons:**

- Younger project
- Feature divergence from Redis over time

---

### KeyDB

_Multithreaded fork of Redis maintained by Snap_

**Pros:**

- Multithreaded
- Active-active replication
- Redis compatible

**Cons:**

- Development has slowed
- Smaller community

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Valkey — open-source Redis fork backed by the Linux Foundation. KeyDB — multithreaded fork of Redis maintained by Snap.
- Both share the same licensing model (open source), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Deployment:** Valkey — self-hosted or managed cloud. KeyDB — self-hosted.
- **Pricing:** Valkey — free and open source. KeyDB — free and open source.
- **Signature strength:** Valkey — Redis-compatible. KeyDB — multithreaded.

## Detailed Breakdown

### New Relic

_Full-stack observability platform_

**Pros:**

- Free tier with monthly data ingest included
- Unified telemetry database
- Strong APM heritage

**Cons:**

- Per-user pricing for full platform users
- UI can feel complex

---

### Elastic Observability

_Observability built on the Elastic Stack_

**Pros:**

- Powerful log search
- Unified logs, metrics and APM
- Self-managed or Elastic Cloud

**Cons:**

- Cluster management overhead when self-hosted
- Resource-intensive at scale

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** New Relic — full-stack observability platform. Elastic Observability — observability built on the Elastic Stack.
- Licensing differs: New Relic is proprietary while Elastic Observability is open source.
- **Deployment:** New Relic — managed cloud. Elastic Observability — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** New Relic — free tier, then usage-based pricing. Elastic Observability — free open source; paid managed cloud.
- **Signature strength:** New Relic — free tier with monthly data ingest included. Elastic Observability — powerful log search.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is New Relic better than Elastic Observability?

It depends on your requirements. New Relic is a strong fit for teams wanting full-stack observability with usage-based pricing, while Elastic Observability suits teams already using Elasticsearch for logs.

### Is New Relic free to use?

Yes, you can start with New Relic for free. Pricing model: Free tier, then usage-based pricing.

### Is Elastic Observability free to use?

Yes, you can start with Elastic Observability for free. Pricing model: Free open source; paid managed cloud.

### Can I self-host New Relic or Elastic Observability?

New Relic is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Elastic Observability can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of New Relic and Elastic Observability?

New Relic: per-user pricing for full platform users; UI can feel complex. Elastic Observability: cluster management overhead when self-hosted; resource-intensive at scale.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Contentful

_Enterprise API-first content platform_

**Pros:**

- Mature enterprise governance features
- Strong localization support
- Large app marketplace

**Cons:**

- Paid tiers get expensive quickly
- Content model limits on lower plans

---

### Hygraph

_GraphQL-native headless CMS_

**Pros:**

- Native GraphQL API including mutations
- Content federation from external sources
- Strong schema modeling

**Cons:**

- GraphQL-centric approach
- Lower-tier limits can be restrictive

- **Positioning:** Contentful — enterprise API-first content platform. Hygraph — GraphQL-native headless CMS.

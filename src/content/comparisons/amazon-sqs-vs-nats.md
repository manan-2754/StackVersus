## Detailed Breakdown

### Amazon SQS

_Fully managed message queues on AWS_

**Pros:**

- Zero operations
- Scales automatically
- Free tier

**Cons:**

- AWS only
- Fewer features than full brokers

---

### NATS

_Lightweight, high-performance messaging system_

**Pros:**

- Very lightweight
- JetStream persistence
- Simple operations

**Cons:**

- Smaller ecosystem
- Fewer connectors

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Amazon SQS — fully managed message queues on AWS. NATS — lightweight, high-performance messaging system.
- Licensing differs: Amazon SQS is proprietary while NATS is open source.
- **Deployment:** Amazon SQS — managed cloud. NATS — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Amazon SQS — free tier, then usage-based pricing. NATS — free and open source.
- **Signature strength:** Amazon SQS — zero operations. NATS — very lightweight.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Amazon SQS better than NATS?

It depends on your requirements. Amazon SQS is a strong fit for serverless queues on AWS, while NATS suits cloud-native and edge messaging.

### Is Amazon SQS free to use?

Yes, you can start with Amazon SQS for free. Pricing model: Free tier, then usage-based pricing.

### Is NATS free to use?

Yes, you can start with NATS for free. Pricing model: Free and open source.

### Can I self-host Amazon SQS or NATS?

Amazon SQS is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. NATS can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Amazon SQS and NATS?

Amazon SQS: AWS only; fewer features than full brokers. NATS: smaller ecosystem; fewer connectors.

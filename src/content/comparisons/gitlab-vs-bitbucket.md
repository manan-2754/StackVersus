# GitLab vs Bitbucket: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> GitLab is the better pick for teams wanting an all-in-one DevSecOps platform. Bitbucket is the better pick for teams invested in Jira and Atlassian.

## Detailed Breakdown

### GitLab

_Single-application DevSecOps platform_

**Pros:**

- Integrated CI/CD
- Self-hostable
- Built-in security scanning

**Cons:**

- Complex UI
- Premium features are costly

---

### Bitbucket

_Git hosting by Atlassian_

**Pros:**

- Jira integration
- Bitbucket Pipelines
- Free for small teams

**Cons:**

- Smaller community
- Self-hosting requires Data Center

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** GitLab — single-application DevSecOps platform. Bitbucket — git hosting by Atlassian.
- Licensing differs: GitLab is open-core while Bitbucket is proprietary.
- **Pricing:** GitLab — free tier with paid plans. Bitbucket — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** GitLab — integrated CI/CD. Bitbucket — Jira integration.

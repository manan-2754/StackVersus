## Detailed Breakdown

### Vercel

_Frontend cloud from the creators of Next.js_

**Pros:**

- Best-in-class Next.js support
- Preview deployments for every branch
- Global edge network

**Cons:**

- Usage-based costs can spike
- Some features lock you into the platform

---

### Railway

_Infrastructure platform for deploying apps and databases_

**Pros:**

- Very fast setup from a repository
- One-click databases
- Usage-based pricing

**Cons:**

- Costs less predictable than flat plans
- Less control than raw cloud infrastructure

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Vercel — frontend cloud from the creators of Next.js. Railway — infrastructure platform for deploying apps and databases.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Vercel can be started for free, while Railway requires a paid plan. Railway pricing: pay-as-you-go usage pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Vercel — best-in-class Next.js support. Railway — very fast setup from a repository.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Vercel better than Railway?

It depends on your requirements. Vercel is a strong fit for Next.js and frontend teams wanting zero-config deploys, while Railway suits quick deployment of full-stack apps and side projects.

### Is Vercel free to use?

Yes, you can start with Vercel for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Railway free to use?

Railway does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Pay-as-you-go usage pricing.

### Can I self-host Vercel or Railway?

Vercel is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Railway is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Vercel and Railway?

Vercel: usage-based costs can spike; some features lock you into the platform. Railway: costs less predictable than flat plans; less control than raw cloud infrastructure.

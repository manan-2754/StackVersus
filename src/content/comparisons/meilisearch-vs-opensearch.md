## Detailed Breakdown

### Meilisearch

_Open-source, lightning-fast search engine_

**Pros:**

- Very easy setup
- Typo tolerance out of the box
- Hybrid search support

**Cons:**

- Less suited to very large datasets
- Fewer analytics features

---

### OpenSearch

_Community-driven fork of Elasticsearch_

**Pros:**

- Apache 2.0 license
- Managed option on AWS
- Dashboards and plugins included

**Cons:**

- Feature divergence from Elasticsearch
- Operational complexity

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Meilisearch — open-source, lightning-fast search engine. OpenSearch — community-driven fork of Elasticsearch.
- Both share the same licensing model (open source), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Meilisearch — free open source; paid managed cloud. OpenSearch — free and open source.
- **Signature strength:** Meilisearch — very easy setup. OpenSearch — apache 2.0 license.

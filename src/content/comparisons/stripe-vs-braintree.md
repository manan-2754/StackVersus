## Detailed Breakdown

### Stripe

_Payments infrastructure for the internet_

**Pros:**

- Best-in-class API and documentation
- Broad suite including Billing, Connect and Tax
- Supports many payment methods

**Cons:**

- Tax compliance is on you unless you add Stripe Tax
- Account reviews can affect high-risk businesses

---

### Braintree

_PayPal-owned payment gateway for developers_

**Pros:**

- Native PayPal and Venmo support
- Vault and recurring billing
- Mature SDKs

**Cons:**

- Smaller product suite than Stripe
- Slower feature development

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Stripe — payments infrastructure for the internet. Braintree — PayPal-owned payment gateway for developers.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Stripe — per-transaction fees, no monthly fee. Braintree — per-transaction fees, no monthly fee.
- **Signature strength:** Stripe — best-in-class API and documentation. Braintree — native PayPal and Venmo support.

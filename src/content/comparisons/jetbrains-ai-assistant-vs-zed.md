## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is JetBrains AI Assistant better than Zed?

It depends on your requirements. JetBrains AI Assistant is a strong fit for developers using IntelliJ, PyCharm and other JetBrains IDEs, while Zed suits developers wanting a fast native editor.

### Is JetBrains AI Assistant free to use?

Yes, you can start with JetBrains AI Assistant for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Zed free to use?

Yes, you can start with Zed for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host JetBrains AI Assistant or Zed?

JetBrains AI Assistant runs inside your own stack: built into JetBrains IDEs. Zed runs locally on your own machine.

### What are the main drawbacks of JetBrains AI Assistant and Zed?

JetBrains AI Assistant: only for JetBrains IDEs; quota-based usage. Zed: smaller extension ecosystem; newer Windows support.

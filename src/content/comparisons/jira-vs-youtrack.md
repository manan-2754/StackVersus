- Huge marketplace
- Advanced reporting

**Cons:**

- Significant admin overhead

### YouTrack

_Issue tracker and project management by JetBrains_

**Pros:**

- Powerful search query language
- Agile boards and time tracking
- Free for small teams

**Cons:**

- Dense interface
- Smaller marketplace

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Jira — issue and project tracking by Atlassian. YouTrack — issue tracker and project management by JetBrains.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Deployment:** Jira — managed cloud. YouTrack — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Jira — free tier with paid plans. YouTrack — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Jira — extremely customizable. YouTrack — powerful search query language.

It depends on your requirements. Jira is a strong fit for large organizations with complex workflows, while YouTrack suits teams in the JetBrains ecosystem.

### Is YouTrack free to use?

Yes, you can start with YouTrack for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

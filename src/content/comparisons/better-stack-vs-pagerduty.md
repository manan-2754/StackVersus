# Better Stack vs PagerDuty: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Choose Better Stack if you want a modern, unified developer experience with lightning-fast alerting, integrated logs, and transparent pricing. Choose PagerDuty if you require deep enterprise integrations, complex routing rules, and advanced stakeholder communications at scale.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Better Stack

_Modern observability, log management, and incident response in one platform_

**Pros:**

- Unified logs, metrics, and incident response in a single dashboard
- Modern, lightning-fast UI with excellent developer experience
- Transparent pricing without hidden enterprise fees

**Cons:**

- Fewer out-of-the-box niche enterprise ITSM integrations
- Less mature advanced routing and automated workflow engine

---

### PagerDuty

_The standard digital operations management platform for enterprise reliability_

**Pros:**

- Massive ecosystem of pre-built enterprise and ITSM integrations
- Extremely robust escalation policies and dynamic routing logic
- Advanced stakeholder communication and status page capabilities

**Cons:**

- High total cost of ownership with expensive per-user licensing
- Clunky, dated user interface that suffers from legacy bloat

---

## Key Differences

- Better Stack natively combines log management, uptime monitoring, and incident response, whereas PagerDuty focuses primarily on alerting and orchestration.
- PagerDuty uses a traditional per-user pricing model which can get expensive, while Better Stack offers transparent, usage-driven pricing.
- Better Stack provides a modern, high-performance developer UX, whereas PagerDuty has a legacy enterprise architecture with a steeper learning curve.
- PagerDuty excels at complex multi-team escalation matrices, whereas Better Stack focuses on speed, simplicity, and fast MTTR.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which tool offers better pricing transparency?

Better Stack offers clearer, usage-based pricing models that are easy to calculate, while PagerDuty relies on tiered per-user pricing with add-on costs.

### Can I replace my log management tool with Better Stack?

Yes. Better Stack includes high-performance log management natively alongside incident response, eliminating the need for a separate log aggregation tool.

### Which platform is better for complex enterprise routing?

PagerDuty remains the industry standard for highly complex enterprise organizational structures requiring sophisticated escalation policies and rigid access controls.

_Open-source outliner for networked notes_

- Open source

**Cons:**

- Can slow down with large graphs

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Coda better than Logseq?

It depends on your requirements. Coda is a strong fit for teams building doc-based tools and workflows, while Logseq suits outliner fans wanting local, open-source notes.

### Is Coda free to use?

Yes, you can start with Coda for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Logseq free to use?

Yes, you can start with Logseq for free. Pricing model: Free and open source.

### Can I self-host Coda or Logseq?

Coda is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Logseq runs locally on your own machine.

### What are the main drawbacks of Coda and Logseq?

Coda: learning curve for formulas; smaller community. Logseq: database version is still maturing; can slow down with large graphs.

# Zapier vs IFTTT: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Detailed Breakdown

### Zapier

_No-code automation across thousands of apps_

**Pros:**

- Largest app catalog
- Very easy to use
- AI-assisted automation building

**Cons:**

- Task-based pricing gets expensive
- Complex logic is limited

---

### IFTTT

_Simple consumer automation applets_

**Pros:**

- Very simple
- Smart-home integrations
- Mobile app

**Cons:**

- Limited for business workflows
- Restricted free plan

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Zapier — no-code automation across thousands of apps. IFTTT — simple consumer automation applets.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Zapier — free tier with paid plans. IFTTT — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Zapier — largest app catalog. IFTTT — very simple.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Zapier better than IFTTT?

It depends on your requirements. Zapier is a strong fit for non-technical teams automating between SaaS apps, while IFTTT suits personal and smart-home automations.

### Is Zapier free to use?

Yes, you can start with Zapier for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is IFTTT free to use?

Yes, you can start with IFTTT for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Zapier or IFTTT?

Zapier is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. IFTTT is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Zapier and IFTTT?

Zapier: task-based pricing gets expensive; complex logic is limited. IFTTT: limited for business workflows; restricted free plan.

## Detailed Breakdown

### WorkOS

_Enterprise features like SSO and SCIM for B2B SaaS_

**Pros:**

- Easy SAML SSO and directory sync
- AuthKit user management with a free tier
- Admin portal for customer IT teams

**Cons:**

- Per-connection pricing for SSO
- Focused on B2B use cases

---

### FusionAuth

_Developer-focused auth you can self-host_

**Pros:**

- Free self-hosted Community edition
- Runs anywhere including on-premises
- Broad protocol support

**Cons:**

- Not open source
- Self-hosting adds operational work

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** WorkOS — enterprise features like SSO and SCIM for B2B SaaS. FusionAuth — developer-focused auth you can self-host.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Deployment:** WorkOS — managed cloud. FusionAuth — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** WorkOS — free tier with paid plans. FusionAuth — free Community edition; paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** WorkOS — easy SAML SSO and directory sync. FusionAuth — free self-hosted Community edition.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is WorkOS better than FusionAuth?

It depends on your requirements. WorkOS is a strong fit for B2B SaaS teams selling to enterprises, while FusionAuth suits teams wanting full control with self-hosted auth.

### Is WorkOS free to use?

Yes, you can start with WorkOS for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is FusionAuth free to use?

Yes, you can start with FusionAuth for free. Pricing model: Free Community edition; paid plans.

### Can I self-host WorkOS or FusionAuth?

WorkOS is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. FusionAuth can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of WorkOS and FusionAuth?

WorkOS: per-connection pricing for SSO; focused on B2B use cases. FusionAuth: not open source; self-hosting adds operational work.

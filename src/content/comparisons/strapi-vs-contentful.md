## Detailed Breakdown

### Strapi

_Open-source Node.js headless CMS_

**Pros:**

- Admin panel with a visual content-type builder
- REST and GraphQL APIs
- Large plugin ecosystem

**Cons:**

- Major version upgrades can be painful
- Some features gated behind paid plans

---

### Contentful

_Enterprise API-first content platform_

**Pros:**

- Mature enterprise governance features
- Strong localization support
- Large app marketplace

**Cons:**

- Paid tiers get expensive quickly
- Content model limits on lower plans

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Strapi — open-source Node.js headless CMS. Contentful — enterprise API-first content platform.
- Licensing differs: Strapi is open source while Contentful is proprietary.
- **Deployment:** Strapi — self-hosted or managed cloud. Contentful — managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Strapi — free open source; paid managed cloud. Contentful — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Strapi — admin panel with a visual content-type builder. Contentful — mature enterprise governance features.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Strapi better than Contentful?

It depends on your requirements. Strapi is a strong fit for JavaScript teams wanting a self-hosted, customizable CMS, while Contentful suits large organizations publishing content across many channels.

### Is Strapi free to use?

Yes, you can start with Strapi for free. Pricing model: Free open source; paid managed cloud.

### Is Contentful free to use?

Yes, you can start with Contentful for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Strapi or Contentful?

Strapi can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud. Contentful is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Strapi and Contentful?

Strapi: major version upgrades can be painful; some features gated behind paid plans. Contentful: paid tiers get expensive quickly; content model limits on lower plans.

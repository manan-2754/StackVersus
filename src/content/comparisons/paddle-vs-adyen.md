## Detailed Breakdown

### Paddle

_Merchant of record for software companies_

**Pros:**

- Acts as merchant of record
- Handles global sales tax and VAT
- Subscription billing built in

**Cons:**

- Higher fees than a pure processor
- Less flexibility for custom flows

---

### Adyen

_Enterprise payments platform_

**Pros:**

- One platform for online and in-person payments
- Strong global acquiring
- Advanced risk management

**Cons:**

- Aimed at high-volume merchants
- Less self-serve for small businesses

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Paddle — merchant of record for software companies. Adyen — enterprise payments platform.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Paddle can be started for free, while Adyen requires a paid plan. Adyen pricing: custom enterprise pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Paddle — acts as merchant of record. Adyen — one platform for online and in-person payments.

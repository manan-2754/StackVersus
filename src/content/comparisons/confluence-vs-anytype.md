## Detailed Breakdown

### Confluence

_Team wiki by Atlassian_

**Pros:**

- Deep Jira integration
- Structured spaces and permissions
- Enterprise features

**Cons:**

- Editor can feel clunky
- Search quality varies

---

### Anytype

_Local-first, end-to-end encrypted workspace_

**Pros:**

- End-to-end encryption
- Local-first with peer-to-peer sync
- Object-based structure

**Cons:**

- Younger product
- Collaboration features still developing

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Confluence — team wiki by Atlassian. Anytype — local-first, end-to-end encrypted workspace.
- Licensing differs: Confluence is proprietary while Anytype is source-available.
- **Deployment:** Confluence — managed cloud. Anytype — desktop app.
- **Pricing:** Confluence — free tier with paid plans. Anytype — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Confluence — deep Jira integration. Anytype — end-to-end encryption.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Confluence better than Anytype?

It depends on your requirements. Confluence is a strong fit for organizations already using Jira, while Anytype suits privacy-focused users wanting a local-first Notion alternative.

### Is Confluence free to use?

Yes, you can start with Confluence for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Anytype free to use?

Yes, you can start with Anytype for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Confluence or Anytype?

Confluence is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Anytype runs locally on your own machine.

### What are the main drawbacks of Confluence and Anytype?

Confluence: editor can feel clunky; search quality varies. Anytype: younger product; collaboration features still developing.

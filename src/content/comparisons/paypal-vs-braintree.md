## Detailed Breakdown

### PayPal

_Global online payments platform_

**Pros:**

- Huge consumer trust and reach
- Buyer accounts in many countries
- Simple checkout buttons

**Cons:**

- Higher fees on some transaction types
- Dispute and hold policies can frustrate sellers

---

### Braintree

_PayPal-owned payment gateway for developers_

**Pros:**

- Native PayPal and Venmo support
- Vault and recurring billing
- Mature SDKs

**Cons:**

- Smaller product suite than Stripe
- Slower feature development

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** PayPal — global online payments platform. Braintree — PayPal-owned payment gateway for developers.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** PayPal — per-transaction fees, no monthly fee. Braintree — per-transaction fees, no monthly fee.
- **Signature strength:** PayPal — huge consumer trust and reach. Braintree — native PayPal and Venmo support.

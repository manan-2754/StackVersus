## Detailed Breakdown

### Logseq

_Open-source outliner for networked notes_

**Pros:**

- Block-based outliner
- Open source
- Local Markdown files

**Cons:**

- Database version is still maturing
- Can slow down with large graphs

---

### Anytype

_Local-first, end-to-end encrypted workspace_

**Pros:**

- End-to-end encryption
- Local-first with peer-to-peer sync
- Object-based structure

**Cons:**

- Younger product
- Collaboration features still developing

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Logseq — open-source outliner for networked notes. Anytype — local-first, end-to-end encrypted workspace.
- Licensing differs: Logseq is open source while Anytype is source-available.
- **Pricing:** Logseq — free and open source. Anytype — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Logseq — block-based outliner. Anytype — end-to-end encryption.

# Obsidian vs Anytype: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Obsidian is the better pick for individuals who want private, local Markdown notes. Anytype is the better pick for privacy-focused users wanting a local-first Notion alternative.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Obsidian

_Local-first Markdown knowledge base_

**Pros:**

- Plain Markdown files you own
- Huge plugin ecosystem
- Graph view and backlinks

**Cons:**

- Sync and Publish cost extra
- Limited real-time collaboration

---

### Anytype

_Local-first, end-to-end encrypted workspace_

**Pros:**

- End-to-end encryption
- Local-first with peer-to-peer sync
- Object-based structure

**Cons:**

- Younger product
- Collaboration features still developing

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Obsidian — local-first Markdown knowledge base. Anytype — local-first, end-to-end encrypted workspace.
- Licensing differs: Obsidian is proprietary while Anytype is source-available.
- **Pricing:** Obsidian — free app; paid Sync and Publish. Anytype — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Obsidian — plain Markdown files you own. Anytype — end-to-end encryption.

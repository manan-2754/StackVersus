## Detailed Breakdown

### Netlify

_Platform for deploying modern web projects_

**Pros:**

- Simple Git-based deploys
- Built-in forms and serverless functions
- Framework-agnostic

**Cons:**

- Bandwidth and build minutes add up
- Less Next.js-specific optimization than Vercel

---

### Render

_Unified cloud for apps, databases and cron jobs_

**Pros:**

- Heroku-like simplicity
- Managed Postgres and key-value stores
- Background workers and cron jobs

**Cons:**

- Free instances spin down when idle
- Fewer regions than hyperscalers

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Netlify — platform for deploying modern web projects. Render — unified cloud for apps, databases and cron jobs.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Netlify — free tier with paid plans. Render — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Netlify — simple Git-based deploys. Render — Heroku-like simplicity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

It depends on your requirements. Netlify is a strong fit for Jamstack sites and framework-agnostic frontends, while Render suits full-stack apps needing web services, databases and background jobs.

### Is Netlify free to use?

Yes, you can start with Netlify for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Render free to use?

Yes, you can start with Render for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Netlify or Render?

Netlify is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Render is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Netlify and Render?

Netlify: bandwidth and build minutes add up; less Next.js-specific optimization than Vercel. Render: free instances spin down when idle; fewer regions than hyperscalers.

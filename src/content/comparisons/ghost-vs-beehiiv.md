_Open-source publishing platform for newsletters and memberships_

**Pros:**

- Open source
- Built-in memberships with no platform revenue share
- SEO-friendly

**Cons:**

- Self-hosting requires work
- Managed Ghost(Pro) is paid

### Beehiiv

_Newsletter platform built for growth_

**Pros:**

**Cons:**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Ghost better than Beehiiv?

It depends on your requirements. Ghost is a strong fit for independent publishers wanting ownership, while Beehiiv suits newsletter operators focused on growth and ads.

### Is Ghost free to use?

Yes, you can start with Ghost for free. Pricing model: Free self-hosted; paid Ghost(Pro).

### Is Beehiiv free to use?

Yes, you can start with Beehiiv for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Ghost or Beehiiv?

Ghost can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud. Beehiiv is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Ghost and Beehiiv?

Ghost: self-hosting requires work; managed Ghost(Pro) is paid. Beehiiv: advanced features on paid tiers; less built-in discovery than Substack.

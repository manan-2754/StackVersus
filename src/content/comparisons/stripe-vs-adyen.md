## Detailed Breakdown

### Stripe

_Payments infrastructure for the internet_

**Pros:**

- Best-in-class API and documentation
- Broad suite including Billing, Connect and Tax
- Supports many payment methods

**Cons:**

- Tax compliance is on you unless you add Stripe Tax
- Account reviews can affect high-risk businesses

---

### Adyen

_Enterprise payments platform_

**Pros:**

- One platform for online and in-person payments
- Strong global acquiring
- Advanced risk management

**Cons:**

- Aimed at high-volume merchants
- Less self-serve for small businesses

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Stripe — payments infrastructure for the internet. Adyen — enterprise payments platform.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Stripe can be started for free, while Adyen requires a paid plan. Adyen pricing: custom enterprise pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Stripe — best-in-class API and documentation. Adyen — one platform for online and in-person payments.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Stripe better than Adyen?

It depends on your requirements. Stripe is a strong fit for developers who want flexible, programmable payments, while Adyen suits large enterprises processing payments globally.

### Is Stripe free to use?

Yes, you can start with Stripe for free. Pricing model: Per-transaction fees, no monthly fee.

### Is Adyen free to use?

Adyen does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Custom enterprise pricing.

### Can I self-host Stripe or Adyen?

Stripe is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Adyen is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Stripe and Adyen?

Stripe: tax compliance is on you unless you add Stripe Tax; account reviews can affect high-risk businesses. Adyen: aimed at high-volume merchants; less self-serve for small businesses.

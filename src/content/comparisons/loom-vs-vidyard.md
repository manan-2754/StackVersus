# Loom vs Vidyard: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict
> Loom is the superior choice for internal team collaboration and quick, casual communication due to its ease of use. Vidyard is the better investment for sales and marketing teams needing deep analytics, CRM integration, and branded video hosting.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Loom
*Asynchronous video messaging for teams*

**Pros:**
- Extremely low learning curve
- Excellent browser extension and desktop app
- Fast video processing and sharing

**Cons:**
- Limited advanced analytics
- Lacks robust CRM integration
- Fewer customization options for video landing pages

---

### Vidyard
*Video messaging for sales and marketing*

**Pros:**
- Deep integration with Salesforce and HubSpot
- Advanced viewer analytics and tracking
- Customizable video landing pages and CTAs

**Cons:**
- Steeper learning curve for casual users
- More expensive for small teams
- Interface can feel cluttered for simple tasks

---

## Key Differences
- Loom focuses on internal productivity, while Vidyard is built for external sales and marketing engagement.
- Vidyard offers granular viewer analytics, whereas Loom provides basic view counts and emoji reactions.
- Loom is designed for speed and simplicity; Vidyard is designed for lead generation and CRM workflows.
- Vidyard allows for high-level branding and custom CTAs on video pages, which Loom restricts to higher tiers.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which tool is better for sales teams?
Vidyard is better for sales because it tracks exactly when and how long a prospect watches a video, and integrates directly into CRM platforms.

### Is Loom free to use?
Yes, Loom offers a free starter plan that allows for a limited number of videos and recording length, suitable for individual users.

### Can I use these tools for external marketing?
While both can be used externally, Vidyard is specifically optimized for marketing with features like custom thumbnails, SEO-friendly pages, and lead capture forms.

### Together AI

_Cloud for fast inference and fine-tuning of open models_

**Pros:**

- Fast serverless inference
- Fine-tuning
- GPU clusters

**Cons:**

- Focused on open models
- Pricing varies by model

### Fireworks AI

_Fast inference platform for generative AI_

**Pros:**

- Very fast inference
- Function-calling models
- Fine-tuning

**Cons:**

- Smaller model catalog than Hugging Face
- Enterprise features are paid

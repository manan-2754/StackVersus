## Detailed Breakdown

### Make

_Visual automation platform (formerly Integromat)_

**Pros:**

- Visual scenario builder
- Powerful data mapping
- Lower cost per operation

**Cons:**

- Steeper learning curve
- Fewer integrations than Zapier

---

### IFTTT

_Simple consumer automation applets_

**Pros:**

- Very simple
- Smart-home integrations
- Mobile app

**Cons:**

- Limited for business workflows
- Restricted free plan

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Make — Visual automation platform (formerly Integromat). IFTTT — simple consumer automation applets.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Make — free tier with paid plans. IFTTT — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Make — Visual scenario builder. IFTTT — very simple.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Make better than IFTTT?

It depends on your requirements. Make is a strong fit for complex visual scenarios at lower cost, while IFTTT suits personal and smart-home automations.

### Is Make free to use?

Yes, you can start with Make for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is IFTTT free to use?

Yes, you can start with IFTTT for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Make or IFTTT?

Make is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. IFTTT is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Make and IFTTT?

Make: steeper learning curve; fewer integrations than Zapier. IFTTT: limited for business workflows; restricted free plan.

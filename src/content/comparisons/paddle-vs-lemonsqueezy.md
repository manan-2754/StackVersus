## Detailed Breakdown

### Paddle

_Merchant of record for software companies_

**Pros:**

- Acts as merchant of record
- Handles global sales tax and VAT
- Subscription billing built in

**Cons:**

- Higher fees than a pure processor
- Less flexibility for custom flows

---

### Lemonsqueezy

_Merchant of record for digital products, owned by Stripe_

**Pros:**

- Merchant of record handles tax
- Storefronts and checkout out of the box
- Simple setup

**Cons:**

- Higher per-transaction fees
- Fewer enterprise features

- **Positioning:** Paddle — merchant of record for software companies. Lemonsqueezy — merchant of record for digital products, owned by Stripe.

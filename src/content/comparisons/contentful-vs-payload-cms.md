# Contentful vs Payload CMS: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Contentful is the better pick for large organizations publishing content across many channels. Payload CMS is the better pick for developers who want a code-first CMS inside a Next.js app.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Contentful

_Enterprise API-first content platform_

**Pros:**

- Mature enterprise governance features
- Strong localization support
- Large app marketplace

**Cons:**

- Paid tiers get expensive quickly
- Content model limits on lower plans

---

### Payload CMS

_TypeScript-first headless CMS and app framework_

**Pros:**

- Configuration written entirely in TypeScript
- Installs directly into a Next.js app
- Fine-grained access control

**Cons:**

- Changes require developer involvement
- Smaller ecosystem than older CMSs

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Contentful — enterprise API-first content platform. Payload CMS — TypeScript-first headless CMS and app framework.
- Licensing differs: Contentful is proprietary while Payload CMS is open source.
- **Deployment:** Contentful — managed cloud. Payload CMS — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Contentful — free tier with paid plans. Payload CMS — free open source; paid managed cloud.
- **Signature strength:** Contentful — mature enterprise governance features. Payload CMS — configuration written entirely in TypeScript.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Contentful better than Payload CMS?

It depends on your requirements. Contentful is a strong fit for large organizations publishing content across many channels, while Payload CMS suits developers who want a code-first CMS inside a Next.js app.

### Is Contentful free to use?

Yes, you can start with Contentful for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Payload CMS free to use?

Yes, you can start with Payload CMS for free. Pricing model: Free open source; paid managed cloud.

### Can I self-host Contentful or Payload CMS?

Contentful is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Payload CMS can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Contentful and Payload CMS?

Contentful: paid tiers get expensive quickly; content model limits on lower plans. Payload CMS: changes require developer involvement; smaller ecosystem than older CMSs.

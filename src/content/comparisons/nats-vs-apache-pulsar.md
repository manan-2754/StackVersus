## Detailed Breakdown

### NATS

_Lightweight, high-performance messaging system_

**Pros:**

- Very lightweight
- JetStream persistence
- Simple operations

**Cons:**

- Smaller ecosystem
- Fewer connectors

---

### Apache Pulsar

_Cloud-native distributed messaging and streaming_

**Pros:**

- Built-in multi-tenancy
- Tiered storage
- Queue and stream semantics

**Cons:**

- Complex architecture
- Smaller community than Kafka

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** NATS — lightweight, high-performance messaging system. Apache Pulsar — cloud-native distributed messaging and streaming.
- Both share the same licensing model (open source), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** NATS — free and open source. Apache Pulsar — free and open source.
- **Signature strength:** NATS — very lightweight. Apache Pulsar — built-in multi-tenancy.

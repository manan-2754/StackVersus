## Detailed Breakdown

### Notion

_Connected workspace for docs, wikis and projects_

**Pros:**

- Flexible blocks and databases
- Large template gallery
- Built-in AI features

**Cons:**

- Limited offline support
- Can slow down in large workspaces

---

### Evernote

_Long-running note-taking app_

**Pros:**

- Excellent web clipper
- Document scanning with search
- Cross-platform apps

**Cons:**

- Free plan is heavily limited
- Repeated price increases

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Notion — connected workspace for docs, wikis and projects. Evernote — long-running note-taking app.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Notion — free tier with paid plans. Evernote — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Notion — flexible blocks and databases. Evernote — excellent web clipper.

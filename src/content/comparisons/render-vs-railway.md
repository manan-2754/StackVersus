## Detailed Breakdown

### Render

_Unified cloud for apps, databases and cron jobs_

**Pros:**

- Heroku-like simplicity
- Managed Postgres and key-value stores
- Background workers and cron jobs

**Cons:**

- Free instances spin down when idle
- Fewer regions than hyperscalers

---

### Railway

_Infrastructure platform for deploying apps and databases_

**Pros:**

- Very fast setup from a repository
- One-click databases
- Usage-based pricing

**Cons:**

- Costs less predictable than flat plans
- Less control than raw cloud infrastructure

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Render — unified cloud for apps, databases and cron jobs. Railway — infrastructure platform for deploying apps and databases.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Render can be started for free, while Railway requires a paid plan. Railway pricing: pay-as-you-go usage pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Render — Heroku-like simplicity. Railway — very fast setup from a repository.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Render better than Railway?

It depends on your requirements. Render is a strong fit for full-stack apps needing web services, databases and background jobs, while Railway suits quick deployment of full-stack apps and side projects.

### Is Render free to use?

Yes, you can start with Render for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Railway free to use?

Railway does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Pay-as-you-go usage pricing.

### Can I self-host Render or Railway?

Render is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Railway is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Render and Railway?

Render: free instances spin down when idle; fewer regions than hyperscalers. Railway: costs less predictable than flat plans; less control than raw cloud infrastructure.

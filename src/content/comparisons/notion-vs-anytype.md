## Detailed Breakdown

### Notion

_Connected workspace for docs, wikis and projects_

**Pros:**

- Flexible blocks and databases
- Large template gallery
- Built-in AI features

**Cons:**

- Limited offline support
- Can slow down in large workspaces

---

### Anytype

_Local-first, end-to-end encrypted workspace_

**Pros:**

- End-to-end encryption
- Local-first with peer-to-peer sync
- Object-based structure

**Cons:**

- Younger product
- Collaboration features still developing

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Notion — connected workspace for docs, wikis and projects. Anytype — local-first, end-to-end encrypted workspace.
- Licensing differs: Notion is proprietary while Anytype is source-available.
- **Deployment:** Notion — managed cloud. Anytype — desktop app.
- **Pricing:** Notion — free tier with paid plans. Anytype — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Notion — flexible blocks and databases. Anytype — end-to-end encryption.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Notion better than Anytype?

It depends on your requirements. Notion is a strong fit for teams wanting flexible docs and lightweight databases, while Anytype suits privacy-focused users wanting a local-first Notion alternative.

### Is Notion free to use?

Yes, you can start with Notion for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Anytype free to use?

Yes, you can start with Anytype for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Notion or Anytype?

Notion is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Anytype runs locally on your own machine.

### What are the main drawbacks of Notion and Anytype?

Notion: limited offline support; can slow down in large workspaces. Anytype: younger product; collaboration features still developing.

# CircleCI vs Jenkins: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> CircleCI is the better pick for fast, configurable cloud CI across code hosts. Jenkins is the better pick for teams that need maximum control over self-hosted CI.

---

## Detailed Breakdown

### CircleCI

_Cloud CI/CD platform focused on speed_

**Pros:**

- Fast builds with caching and parallelism
- Orbs for reusable configuration
- Supports GitHub, GitLab and Bitbucket

**Cons:**

- Credit-based pricing can be hard to predict
- Separate tool from your code host

---

### Jenkins

_Extensible open-source automation server_

**Pros:**

- Thousands of plugins
- Completely free and self-hosted
- Runs almost any workload

**Cons:**

- Significant maintenance overhead
- Dated UI and plugin compatibility issues

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** CircleCI — cloud CI/CD platform focused on speed. Jenkins — extensible open-source automation server.
- Licensing differs: CircleCI is proprietary while Jenkins is open source.
- **Deployment:** CircleCI — managed cloud. Jenkins — self-hosted.
- **Pricing:** CircleCI — free tier with paid plans. Jenkins — free and open source.
- **Signature strength:** CircleCI — fast builds with caching and parallelism. Jenkins — thousands of plugins.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is CircleCI better than Jenkins?

It depends on your requirements. CircleCI is a strong fit for fast, configurable cloud CI across code hosts, while Jenkins suits teams that need maximum control over self-hosted CI.

### Is CircleCI free to use?

Yes, you can start with CircleCI for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Jenkins free to use?

Yes, you can start with Jenkins for free. Pricing model: Free and open source.

### Can I self-host CircleCI or Jenkins?

CircleCI is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Jenkins can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted.

### What are the main drawbacks of CircleCI and Jenkins?

CircleCI: credit-based pricing can be hard to predict; separate tool from your code host. Jenkins: significant maintenance overhead; dated UI and plugin compatibility issues.

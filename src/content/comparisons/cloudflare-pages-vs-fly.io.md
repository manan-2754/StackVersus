# Cloudflare Pages vs Fly.io: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Cloudflare Pages is the better pick for static and edge-rendered sites that need generous free bandwidth. Fly.io is the better pick for globally distributed apps running in many regions.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Cloudflare Pages

_Static and full-stack hosting on Cloudflare’s network_

**Pros:**

- Generous free tier with unlimited static bandwidth
- Integrates with Workers, R2 and D1
- Fast global network

**Cons:**

- Workers runtime is not full Node.js
- Cloudflare is steering new projects toward Workers

---

### Fly.io

_Platform for running full-stack apps close to users_

**Pros:**

- Runs apps in many regions
- Lightweight Firecracker microVMs
- Good for latency-sensitive apps

**Cons:**

- Steeper learning curve than simpler PaaS
- More operational responsibility

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Cloudflare Pages — static and full-stack hosting on Cloudflare’s network. Fly.io — platform for running full-stack apps close to users.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Cloudflare Pages can be started for free, while Fly.io requires a paid plan. Fly.io pricing: pay-as-you-go usage pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Cloudflare Pages — generous free tier with unlimited static bandwidth. Fly.io — runs apps in many regions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Cloudflare Pages better than Fly.io?

It depends on your requirements. Cloudflare Pages is a strong fit for static and edge-rendered sites that need generous free bandwidth, while Fly.io suits globally distributed apps running in many regions.

### Is Cloudflare Pages free to use?

Yes, you can start with Cloudflare Pages for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Fly.io free to use?

Fly.io does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Pay-as-you-go usage pricing.

### Can I self-host Cloudflare Pages or Fly.io?

Cloudflare Pages is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Fly.io is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Cloudflare Pages and Fly.io?

Cloudflare Pages: workers runtime is not full Node.js; Cloudflare is steering new projects toward Workers. Fly.io: steeper learning curve than simpler PaaS; more operational responsibility.

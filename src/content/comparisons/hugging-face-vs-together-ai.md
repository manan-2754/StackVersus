## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Hugging Face — hub for open models, datasets and inference. Together AI — cloud for fast inference and fine-tuning of open models.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Hugging Face can be started for free, while Together AI requires a paid plan. Together AI pricing: pay-as-you-go usage pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Hugging Face — largest open model hub. Together AI — fast serverless inference.

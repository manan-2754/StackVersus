## Detailed Breakdown

### Kubernetes

_Industry-standard container orchestration_

**Pros:**

- Massive ecosystem
- Managed offerings on every cloud
- Highly extensible

**Cons:**

- Steep learning curve
- Operational complexity

---

### OpenShift

_Enterprise Kubernetes platform by Red Hat_

**Pros:**

- Enterprise support
- Integrated CI/CD and developer console
- Security-focused defaults

**Cons:**

- Expensive subscriptions
- Opinionated platform

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Kubernetes — industry-standard container orchestration. OpenShift — enterprise Kubernetes platform by Red Hat.
- Licensing differs: Kubernetes is open source while OpenShift is open-core.
- **Deployment:** Kubernetes — self-hosted or managed on every major cloud. OpenShift — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- Kubernetes can be started for free, while OpenShift requires a paid plan. OpenShift pricing: custom enterprise pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Kubernetes — massive ecosystem. OpenShift — enterprise support.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Kubernetes better than OpenShift?

It depends on your requirements. Kubernetes is a strong fit for large-scale container workloads, while OpenShift suits enterprises needing supported Kubernetes with developer tooling.

### Is Kubernetes free to use?

Yes, you can start with Kubernetes for free. Pricing model: Free and open source.

### Is OpenShift free to use?

OpenShift does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Custom enterprise pricing.

### Can I self-host Kubernetes or OpenShift?

Kubernetes can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed on every major cloud. OpenShift can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Kubernetes and OpenShift?

Kubernetes: steep learning curve; operational complexity. OpenShift: expensive subscriptions; opinionated platform.

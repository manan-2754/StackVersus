# SendGrid vs Amazon SES: Head-to-Head Comparison

> SendGrid is the better pick for high-volume transactional and marketing email. Amazon SES is the better pick for cost-sensitive high-volume sending on AWS.

## Detailed Breakdown

### SendGrid

_Email delivery platform by Twilio_

**Pros:**

- Handles very high volume
- Marketing and transactional email in one platform
- Mature APIs

**Cons:**

- Shared IP deliverability varies
- Support can be slow on lower plans

---

### Amazon SES

_Low-cost cloud email sending on AWS_

**Pros:**

- Very low cost per email
- Scales massively
- AWS integration

**Cons:**

- Bare-bones interface
- You manage reputation and setup

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** SendGrid — email delivery platform by Twilio. Amazon SES — low-cost cloud email sending on AWS.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Amazon SES can be started for free, while SendGrid requires a paid plan. SendGrid pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** SendGrid — handles very high volume. Amazon SES — very low cost per email.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is SendGrid better than Amazon SES?

It depends on your requirements. SendGrid is a strong fit for high-volume transactional and marketing email, while Amazon SES suits cost-sensitive high-volume sending on AWS.

### Is SendGrid free to use?

SendGrid does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Is Amazon SES free to use?

Yes, you can start with Amazon SES for free. Pricing model: Free tier, then usage-based pricing.

### Can I self-host SendGrid or Amazon SES?

SendGrid is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Amazon SES is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of SendGrid and Amazon SES?

SendGrid: shared IP deliverability varies; support can be slow on lower plans. Amazon SES: bare-bones interface; you manage reputation and setup.

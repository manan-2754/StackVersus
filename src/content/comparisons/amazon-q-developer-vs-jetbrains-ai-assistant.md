It depends on your requirements. Amazon Q Developer is a strong fit for developers building on AWS, while JetBrains AI Assistant suits developers using IntelliJ, PyCharm and other JetBrains IDEs.

### Is Amazon Q Developer free to use?

Yes, you can start with Amazon Q Developer for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is JetBrains AI Assistant free to use?

Yes, you can start with JetBrains AI Assistant for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Amazon Q Developer or JetBrains AI Assistant?

Amazon Q Developer is offered as a managed service: IDE extension and CLI. JetBrains AI Assistant runs inside your own stack: built into JetBrains IDEs.

### What are the main drawbacks of Amazon Q Developer and JetBrains AI Assistant?

Amazon Q Developer: best within AWS; less popular outside AWS. JetBrains AI Assistant: only for JetBrains IDEs; quota-based usage.

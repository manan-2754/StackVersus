## Detailed Breakdown

### Sentry

_Application monitoring and error tracking_

**Pros:**

- Rich stack traces and context
- Performance tracing and session replay
- SDKs for most languages

**Cons:**

- Event quotas require tuning
- Self-hosting is resource-heavy

---

### Bugsnag

_Error monitoring and stability management by SmartBear_

**Pros:**

- Stability score metrics
- Strong mobile SDKs
- Release health tracking

**Cons:**

- Narrower than full APM suites
- Pricing by event volume

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Sentry — application monitoring and error tracking. Bugsnag — error monitoring and stability management by SmartBear.
- Licensing differs: Sentry is source-available while Bugsnag is proprietary.
- **Deployment:** Sentry — self-hosted or managed cloud. Bugsnag — managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Sentry — free tier with paid plans. Bugsnag — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Sentry — rich stack traces and context. Bugsnag — stability score metrics.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Sentry better than Bugsnag?

It depends on your requirements. Sentry is a strong fit for developers wanting detailed error and performance monitoring, while Bugsnag suits mobile apps tracking stability scores.

### Is Sentry free to use?

Yes, you can start with Sentry for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Bugsnag free to use?

Yes, you can start with Bugsnag for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Sentry or Bugsnag?

Sentry can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud. Bugsnag is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Sentry and Bugsnag?

Sentry: event quotas require tuning; self-hosting is resource-heavy. Bugsnag: narrower than full APM suites; pricing by event volume.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Paddle

_Merchant of record for software companies_

**Pros:**

- Acts as merchant of record
- Handles global sales tax and VAT
- Subscription billing built in

**Cons:**

- Higher fees than a pure processor
- Less flexibility for custom flows

---

### PayPal

_Global online payments platform_

**Pros:**

- Huge consumer trust and reach
- Buyer accounts in many countries
- Simple checkout buttons

**Cons:**

- Higher fees on some transaction types
- Dispute and hold policies can frustrate sellers

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Paddle — merchant of record for software companies. PayPal — global online payments platform.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Paddle — percentage plus fixed fee per transaction. PayPal — per-transaction fees, no monthly fee.
- **Signature strength:** Paddle — acts as merchant of record. PayPal — huge consumer trust and reach.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Zapier

_No-code automation across thousands of apps_

**Pros:**

- Largest app catalog
- Very easy to use
- AI-assisted automation building

**Cons:**

- Task-based pricing gets expensive
- Complex logic is limited

---

### Pipedream

_Developer-focused integration platform_

**Pros:**

- Code steps in Node.js and Python
- Many prebuilt actions
- Free tier

**Cons:**

- Requires coding for advanced use
- Less friendly for non-developers

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Zapier — no-code automation across thousands of apps. Pipedream — developer-focused integration platform.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Zapier — free tier with paid plans. Pipedream — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Zapier — largest app catalog. Pipedream — code steps in Node.js and Python.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Zapier better than Pipedream?

It depends on your requirements. Zapier is a strong fit for non-technical teams automating between SaaS apps, while Pipedream suits developers writing code-first integrations.

### Is Zapier free to use?

Yes, you can start with Zapier for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Pipedream free to use?

Yes, you can start with Pipedream for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Zapier or Pipedream?

Zapier is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Pipedream is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Zapier and Pipedream?

Zapier: task-based pricing gets expensive; complex logic is limited. Pipedream: requires coding for advanced use; less friendly for non-developers.

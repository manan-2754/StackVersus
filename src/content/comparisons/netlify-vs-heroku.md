# Netlify vs Heroku: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Netlify is the better pick for Jamstack sites and framework-agnostic frontends. Heroku is the better pick for teams that want a mature, simple PaaS.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Netlify

_Platform for deploying modern web projects_

**Pros:**

- Simple Git-based deploys
- Built-in forms and serverless functions
- Framework-agnostic

**Cons:**

- Bandwidth and build minutes add up
- Less Next.js-specific optimization than Vercel

---

### Heroku

_Pioneering platform as a service by Salesforce_

**Pros:**

- Very simple git push deploys
- Mature add-on marketplace
- Well documented

**Cons:**

- No free dynos since 2022
- Higher cost than newer platforms

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Netlify — platform for deploying modern web projects. Heroku — pioneering platform as a service by Salesforce.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Netlify can be started for free, while Heroku requires a paid plan. Heroku pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Netlify — simple Git-based deploys. Heroku — very simple git push deploys.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Netlify better than Heroku?

It depends on your requirements. Netlify is a strong fit for Jamstack sites and framework-agnostic frontends, while Heroku suits teams that want a mature, simple PaaS.

### Is Netlify free to use?

Yes, you can start with Netlify for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Heroku free to use?

Heroku does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host Netlify or Heroku?

Netlify is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Heroku is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Netlify and Heroku?

Netlify: bandwidth and build minutes add up; less Next.js-specific optimization than Vercel. Heroku: no free dynos since 2022; higher cost than newer platforms.

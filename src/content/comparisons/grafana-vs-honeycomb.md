# Grafana vs Honeycomb: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Grafana is the better pick for teams wanting open-source dashboards across many data sources. Honeycomb is the better pick for teams debugging distributed systems with OpenTelemetry.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Grafana

_Open-source observability and dashboards_

**Pros:**

- Connects to many data sources
- Open-source LGTM stack (Loki, Grafana, Tempo, Mimir)
- Grafana Cloud free tier

**Cons:**

- Self-hosting the full stack is complex
- Requires assembling multiple components

---

### Honeycomb

_Observability for high-cardinality event data_

**Pros:**

- Fast queries on high-cardinality data
- BubbleUp outlier analysis
- OpenTelemetry-first

**Cons:**

- Different mental model from metrics dashboards
- Less breadth than all-in-one suites

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Grafana — open-source observability and dashboards. Honeycomb — observability for high-cardinality event data.
- Licensing differs: Grafana is open source while Honeycomb is proprietary.
- **Deployment:** Grafana — self-hosted or managed cloud. Honeycomb — managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Grafana — free open source; paid managed cloud. Honeycomb — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Grafana — connects to many data sources. Honeycomb — fast queries on high-cardinality data.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Grafana better than Honeycomb?

It depends on your requirements. Grafana is a strong fit for teams wanting open-source dashboards across many data sources, while Honeycomb suits teams debugging distributed systems with OpenTelemetry.

### Is Grafana free to use?

Yes, you can start with Grafana for free. Pricing model: Free open source; paid managed cloud.

### Is Honeycomb free to use?

Yes, you can start with Honeycomb for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Grafana or Honeycomb?

Grafana can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud. Honeycomb is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Grafana and Honeycomb?

Grafana: self-hosting the full stack is complex; requires assembling multiple components. Honeycomb: different mental model from metrics dashboards; less breadth than all-in-one suites.

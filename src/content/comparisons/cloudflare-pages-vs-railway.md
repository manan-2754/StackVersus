## Detailed Breakdown

### Cloudflare Pages

_Static and full-stack hosting on Cloudflare’s network_

**Pros:**

- Generous free tier with unlimited static bandwidth
- Integrates with Workers, R2 and D1
- Fast global network

**Cons:**

- Workers runtime is not full Node.js
- Cloudflare is steering new projects toward Workers

---

### Railway

_Infrastructure platform for deploying apps and databases_

**Pros:**

- Very fast setup from a repository
- One-click databases
- Usage-based pricing

**Cons:**

- Costs less predictable than flat plans
- Less control than raw cloud infrastructure

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Cloudflare Pages — static and full-stack hosting on Cloudflare’s network. Railway — infrastructure platform for deploying apps and databases.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Cloudflare Pages can be started for free, while Railway requires a paid plan. Railway pricing: pay-as-you-go usage pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Cloudflare Pages — generous free tier with unlimited static bandwidth. Railway — very fast setup from a repository.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Cloudflare Pages better than Railway?

It depends on your requirements. Cloudflare Pages is a strong fit for static and edge-rendered sites that need generous free bandwidth, while Railway suits quick deployment of full-stack apps and side projects.

### Is Cloudflare Pages free to use?

Yes, you can start with Cloudflare Pages for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Railway free to use?

Railway does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Pay-as-you-go usage pricing.

### Can I self-host Cloudflare Pages or Railway?

Cloudflare Pages is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Railway is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Cloudflare Pages and Railway?

Cloudflare Pages: workers runtime is not full Node.js; Cloudflare is steering new projects toward Workers. Railway: costs less predictable than flat plans; less control than raw cloud infrastructure.

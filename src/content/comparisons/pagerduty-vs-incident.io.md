## Detailed Breakdown

### PagerDuty

_Incident response and on-call management platform_

**Pros:**

- Robust on-call scheduling and escalation
- Hundreds of integrations
- Event intelligence features

**Cons:**

- Expensive per user
- Advanced features in higher tiers

---

### incident.io

_Slack-native incident management_

**Pros:**

- Runs incidents directly in Slack
- Status pages and on-call
- Automated post-incident workflows

**Cons:**

- Best for Slack-centric teams
- Premium features cost extra

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** PagerDuty — incident response and on-call management platform. incident.io — Slack-native incident management.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** PagerDuty — free tier with paid plans. incident.io — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** PagerDuty — robust on-call scheduling and escalation. incident.io — runs incidents directly in Slack.

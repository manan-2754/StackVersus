- **Positioning:** Semgrep — fast, rule-based static analysis. Veracode — application security testing platform.
- Licensing differs: Semgrep is open-core while Veracode is proprietary.
- **Deployment:** Semgrep — self-hosted or managed cloud. Veracode — managed cloud.
- Semgrep can be started for free, while Veracode requires a paid plan. Veracode pricing: custom enterprise pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Semgrep — easy custom rules. Veracode — SAST, DAST and SCA.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Semgrep better than Veracode?

It depends on your requirements. Semgrep is a strong fit for security teams writing custom rules, while Veracode suits regulated enterprises.

### Is Semgrep free to use?

Yes, you can start with Semgrep for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Veracode free to use?

Veracode does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Custom enterprise pricing.

### Can I self-host Semgrep or Veracode?

Semgrep can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud. Veracode is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Semgrep and Veracode?

Semgrep: advanced features in the paid platform; rule tuning needed. Veracode: expensive; less developer-friendly.

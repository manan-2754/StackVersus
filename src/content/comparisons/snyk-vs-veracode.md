## Detailed Breakdown

### Snyk

_Developer security platform_

**Pros:**

- Dependency, code, container and IaC scanning
- IDE and pull request integration
- Fix suggestions

**Cons:**

- Can get expensive
- Alert noise without tuning

---

### Veracode

_Application security testing platform_

**Pros:**

- SAST, DAST and SCA
- Policy management
- Compliance focus

**Cons:**

- Expensive
- Less developer-friendly

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Snyk — developer security platform. Veracode — application security testing platform.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Snyk can be started for free, while Veracode requires a paid plan. Veracode pricing: custom enterprise pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Snyk — dependency, code, container and IaC scanning. Veracode — SAST, DAST and SCA.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Snyk better than Veracode?

It depends on your requirements. Snyk is a strong fit for developer-first dependency and code scanning, while Veracode suits regulated enterprises.

### Is Snyk free to use?

Yes, you can start with Snyk for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Veracode free to use?

Veracode does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Custom enterprise pricing.

### Can I self-host Snyk or Veracode?

Snyk is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Veracode is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Snyk and Veracode?

Snyk: can get expensive; alert noise without tuning. Veracode: expensive; less developer-friendly.

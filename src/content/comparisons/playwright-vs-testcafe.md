## Detailed Breakdown

### Playwright

_Cross-browser end-to-end testing by Microsoft_

**Pros:**

- Chromium, Firefox and WebKit support
- Auto-waiting reduces flaky tests
- Excellent trace viewer and codegen

**Cons:**

- More concepts to learn than Cypress
- Fewer third-party plugins

---

### TestCafe

_Node.js E2E testing without WebDriver_

**Pros:**

- No WebDriver or browser plugins needed
- Runs on any browser
- Built-in smart waits

**Cons:**

- Less active development than leaders
- Smaller ecosystem

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Playwright — cross-browser end-to-end testing by Microsoft. TestCafe — Node.js E2E testing without WebDriver.
- Both share the same licensing model (open source), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Playwright — free and open source. TestCafe — free and open source.
- **Signature strength:** Playwright — chromium, Firefox and WebKit support. TestCafe — no WebDriver or browser plugins needed.

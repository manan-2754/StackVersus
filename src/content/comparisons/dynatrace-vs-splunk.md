**Cons:**

- Less approachable for small teams

**Pros:**

- Strong security analytics
- Enterprise-grade scale

**Cons:**

- Steep learning curve

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Dynatrace better than Splunk?

It depends on your requirements. Dynatrace is a strong fit for large enterprises wanting automated root-cause analysis, while Splunk suits enterprises with heavy log analytics and security needs.

### Is Dynatrace free to use?

Dynatrace does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Is Splunk free to use?

Splunk does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Custom enterprise pricing.

### Can I self-host Dynatrace or Splunk?

Dynatrace is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Splunk can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Dynatrace and Splunk?

Dynatrace: expensive; less approachable for small teams. Splunk: very expensive at high ingest volumes; steep learning curve.

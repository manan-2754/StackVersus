## Detailed Breakdown

### Ghost

_Open-source publishing platform for newsletters and memberships_

**Pros:**

- Open source
- Built-in memberships with no platform revenue share
- SEO-friendly

**Cons:**

- Self-hosting requires work
- Managed Ghost(Pro) is paid

---

### Kit

_Email marketing for creators (formerly ConvertKit)_

**Pros:**

- Visual automations
- Built-in commerce
- Creator recommendation network

**Cons:**

- Pricing scales by subscriber count
- Limited email design options

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Ghost — open-source publishing platform for newsletters and memberships. Kit — email marketing for creators (formerly ConvertKit).
- Licensing differs: Ghost is open source while Kit is proprietary.
- **Deployment:** Ghost — self-hosted or managed cloud. Kit — managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Ghost — free self-hosted; paid Ghost(Pro). Kit — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Ghost — open source. Kit — Visual automations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Ghost better than Kit?

It depends on your requirements. Ghost is a strong fit for independent publishers wanting ownership, while Kit suits creators selling products via email.

### Is Ghost free to use?

Yes, you can start with Ghost for free. Pricing model: Free self-hosted; paid Ghost(Pro).

### Is Kit free to use?

Yes, you can start with Kit for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Ghost or Kit?

Ghost can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud. Kit is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Ghost and Kit?

Ghost: self-hosting requires work; managed Ghost(Pro) is paid. Kit: pricing scales by subscriber count; limited email design options.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Trello

_Visual Kanban boards by Atlassian_

**Pros:**

- Extremely easy to use
- Power-Ups for extensions
- Free plan

**Cons:**

- Limited for complex projects
- Advanced views require paid plans

---

### ClickUp

_All-in-one productivity and project platform_

**Pros:**

- Highly customizable
- Many views
- Docs and whiteboards built in

**Cons:**

- Can feel overwhelming
- Slowdowns in large workspaces

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Trello — Visual Kanban boards by Atlassian. ClickUp — all-in-one productivity and project platform.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Trello — free tier with paid plans. ClickUp — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Trello — extremely easy to use. ClickUp — highly customizable.

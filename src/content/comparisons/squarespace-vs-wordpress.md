# Squarespace vs WordPress: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Squarespace is the better pick for creatives and small businesses wanting polished templates. WordPress is the better pick for sites needing maximum flexibility and plugins.

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Squarespace — design-led website builder. WordPress — open-source CMS powering a large share of the web.
- Licensing differs: Squarespace is proprietary while WordPress is open source.
- **Deployment:** Squarespace — managed cloud. WordPress — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- WordPress can be started for free, while Squarespace requires a paid plan. Squarespace pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Squarespace — beautiful templates. WordPress — huge plugin and theme ecosystem.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Squarespace better than WordPress?

It depends on your requirements. Squarespace is a strong fit for creatives and small businesses wanting polished templates, while WordPress suits sites needing maximum flexibility and plugins.

### Is Squarespace free to use?

Squarespace does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Is WordPress free to use?

Yes, you can start with WordPress for free. Pricing model: Free and open source.

### Can I self-host Squarespace or WordPress?

Squarespace is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. WordPress can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Squarespace and WordPress?

Squarespace: limited customization; no free plan. WordPress: plugin security and maintenance burden; performance tuning needed.

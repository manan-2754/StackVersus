## Detailed Breakdown

### Auth0

_Customer identity platform by Okta_

**Pros:**

- Extensive OIDC and SAML support
- Actions for custom auth logic
- Enterprise SSO and MFA

**Cons:**

- Pricing grows steeply with monthly active users
- Complex configuration

---

### Stytch

_Authentication and fraud prevention APIs_

**Pros:**

- Strong passwordless options
- Device fingerprinting and fraud tools
- Headless APIs and prebuilt UI

**Cons:**

- Smaller community
- Advanced fraud features cost extra

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Auth0 — customer identity platform by Okta. Stytch — authentication and fraud prevention APIs.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Auth0 — free tier with paid plans. Stytch — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Auth0 — extensive OIDC and SAML support. Stytch — strong passwordless options.

# Buildkite vs Travis CI: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Buildkite is the better pick for large engineering teams running CI on their own compute. Travis CI is the better pick for simple CI for small projects.

---

## Detailed Breakdown

### Buildkite

_Hybrid CI/CD with agents on your own infrastructure_

**Pros:**

- Agents run on your own infrastructure
- Scales to very large pipelines
- Dynamic pipeline generation

**Cons:**

- You manage the agent infrastructure
- Per-user pricing

---

### Travis CI

_Hosted continuous integration service_

**Pros:**

- Simple YAML configuration
- Long history with open-source projects
- Build matrix support

**Cons:**

- No longer offers unlimited free builds for open source
- Smaller ecosystem than newer tools

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Buildkite — hybrid CI/CD with agents on your own infrastructure. Travis CI — hosted continuous integration service.
- Licensing differs: Buildkite is open-core while Travis CI is proprietary.
- **Deployment:** Buildkite — managed control plane with self-hosted agents. Travis CI — managed cloud.
- Buildkite can be started for free, while Travis CI requires a paid plan. Travis CI pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Buildkite — agents run on your own infrastructure. Travis CI — simple YAML configuration.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Buildkite better than Travis CI?

It depends on your requirements. Buildkite is a strong fit for large engineering teams running CI on their own compute, while Travis CI suits simple CI for small projects.

### Is Buildkite free to use?

Yes, you can start with Buildkite for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Travis CI free to use?

Travis CI does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host Buildkite or Travis CI?

Buildkite can be self-hosted. Deployment options: managed control plane with self-hosted agents. Travis CI is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Buildkite and Travis CI?

Buildkite: you manage the agent infrastructure; per-user pricing. Travis CI: no longer offers unlimited free builds for open source; smaller ecosystem than newer tools.

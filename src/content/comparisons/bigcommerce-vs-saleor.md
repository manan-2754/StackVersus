# BigCommerce vs Saleor: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> BigCommerce is the better pick for mid-market brands needing built-in features. Saleor is the better pick for headless commerce with a GraphQL API.

## Detailed Breakdown

### BigCommerce

_SaaS commerce platform for growing brands_

**Pros:**

- No extra transaction fees
- Many built-in features
- Headless commerce support

**Cons:**

- Plan upgrades tied to revenue
- Smaller app store

---

### Saleor

_Open-source GraphQL-first commerce platform_

**Pros:**

- GraphQL API
- Python and Django backend
- Multi-channel support

**Cons:**

- Smaller community
- Requires development resources

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** BigCommerce — SaaS commerce platform for growing brands. Saleor — open-source GraphQL-first commerce platform.
- Licensing differs: BigCommerce is proprietary while Saleor is open source.
- **Deployment:** BigCommerce — managed cloud. Saleor — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- Saleor can be started for free, while BigCommerce requires a paid plan. BigCommerce pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** BigCommerce — no extra transaction fees. Saleor — GraphQL API.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is BigCommerce better than Saleor?

It depends on your requirements. BigCommerce is a strong fit for mid-market brands needing built-in features, while Saleor suits headless commerce with a GraphQL API.

### Is BigCommerce free to use?

BigCommerce does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Is Saleor free to use?

Yes, you can start with Saleor for free. Pricing model: Free open source; paid managed cloud.

### Can I self-host BigCommerce or Saleor?

BigCommerce is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Saleor can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of BigCommerce and Saleor?

BigCommerce: plan upgrades tied to revenue; smaller app store. Saleor: smaller community; requires development resources.

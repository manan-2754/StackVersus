## Detailed Breakdown

### Opsgenie

_Atlassian on-call and alert management_

**Pros:**

- Tight Jira integration
- Flexible alert routing
- Affordable on-call

**Cons:**

- Atlassian stopped selling Opsgenie in favor of Jira Service Management
- Limited future roadmap

---

### incident.io

_Slack-native incident management_

**Pros:**

- Runs incidents directly in Slack
- Status pages and on-call
- Automated post-incident workflows

**Cons:**

- Best for Slack-centric teams
- Premium features cost extra

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Opsgenie — Atlassian on-call and alert management. incident.io — Slack-native incident management.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Opsgenie — free tier with paid plans. incident.io — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Opsgenie — tight Jira integration. incident.io — runs incidents directly in Slack.

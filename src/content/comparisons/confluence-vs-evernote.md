## Detailed Breakdown

### Confluence

_Team wiki by Atlassian_

**Pros:**

- Deep Jira integration
- Structured spaces and permissions
- Enterprise features

**Cons:**

- Editor can feel clunky
- Search quality varies

---

### Evernote

_Long-running note-taking app_

**Pros:**

- Excellent web clipper
- Document scanning with search
- Cross-platform apps

**Cons:**

- Free plan is heavily limited
- Repeated price increases

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Confluence — team wiki by Atlassian. Evernote — long-running note-taking app.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Confluence — free tier with paid plans. Evernote — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Confluence — deep Jira integration. Evernote — excellent web clipper.

### Is Evernote free to use?

Yes, you can start with Evernote for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Notion

_Connected workspace for docs, wikis and projects_

**Pros:**

- Flexible blocks and databases
- Large template gallery
- Built-in AI features

**Cons:**

- Limited offline support
- Can slow down in large workspaces

---

### Confluence

_Team wiki by Atlassian_

**Pros:**

- Deep Jira integration
- Structured spaces and permissions
- Enterprise features

**Cons:**

- Editor can feel clunky
- Search quality varies

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Notion — connected workspace for docs, wikis and projects. Confluence — team wiki by Atlassian.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Notion — free tier with paid plans. Confluence — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Notion — flexible blocks and databases. Confluence — deep Jira integration.

It depends on your requirements. Notion is a strong fit for teams wanting flexible docs and lightweight databases, while Confluence suits organizations already using Jira.

Yes, you can start with Notion for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Confluence free to use?

Yes, you can start with Confluence for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Notion or Confluence?

Notion is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Confluence is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

Notion: limited offline support; can slow down in large workspaces. Confluence: editor can feel clunky; search quality varies.

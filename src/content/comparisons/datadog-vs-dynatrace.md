## Detailed Breakdown

### Datadog

_Monitoring and security platform for cloud apps_

**Pros:**

- Hundreds of integrations
- Unified metrics, logs, APM and security
- Polished dashboards

**Cons:**

- Costs can escalate quickly
- Complex per-product pricing

---

### Dynatrace

_AI-powered observability and security platform_

**Pros:**

- Automatic discovery with OneAgent
- AI-driven root-cause analysis
- Strong enterprise support

**Cons:**

- Expensive
- Less approachable for small teams

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Datadog — monitoring and security platform for cloud apps. Dynatrace — AI-powered observability and security platform.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Datadog can be started for free, while Dynatrace requires a paid plan. Dynatrace pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Datadog — hundreds of integrations. Dynatrace — automatic discovery with OneAgent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Datadog better than Dynatrace?

It depends on your requirements. Datadog is a strong fit for organizations wanting one platform for metrics, logs and traces, while Dynatrace suits large enterprises wanting automated root-cause analysis.

### Is Datadog free to use?

Yes, you can start with Datadog for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Dynatrace free to use?

Dynatrace does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host Datadog or Dynatrace?

Datadog is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Dynatrace is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Datadog and Dynatrace?

Datadog: costs can escalate quickly; complex per-product pricing. Dynatrace: expensive; less approachable for small teams.

- Many views

**Cons:**

- Can feel overwhelming
- Slowdowns in large workspaces

### Basecamp

_Opinionated project management and team communication_

**Pros:**

- Flat-price plan option
- Combines chat, docs and to-dos
- Calm, simple design

**Cons:**

- Limited reporting
- Fewer integrations

- **Signature strength:** ClickUp — highly customizable. Basecamp — flat-price plan option.

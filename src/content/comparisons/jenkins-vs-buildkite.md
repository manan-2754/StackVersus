# Jenkins vs Buildkite: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Jenkins is the better pick for teams that need maximum control over self-hosted CI. Buildkite is the better pick for large engineering teams running CI on their own compute.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Jenkins

_Extensible open-source automation server_

**Pros:**

- Thousands of plugins
- Completely free and self-hosted
- Runs almost any workload

**Cons:**

- Significant maintenance overhead
- Dated UI and plugin compatibility issues

---

### Buildkite

_Hybrid CI/CD with agents on your own infrastructure_

**Pros:**

- Agents run on your own infrastructure
- Scales to very large pipelines
- Dynamic pipeline generation

**Cons:**

- You manage the agent infrastructure
- Per-user pricing

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Jenkins — extensible open-source automation server. Buildkite — hybrid CI/CD with agents on your own infrastructure.
- Licensing differs: Jenkins is open source while Buildkite is open-core.
- **Deployment:** Jenkins — self-hosted. Buildkite — managed control plane with self-hosted agents.
- **Pricing:** Jenkins — free and open source. Buildkite — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Jenkins — thousands of plugins. Buildkite — agents run on your own infrastructure.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Dynatrace

_AI-powered observability and security platform_

**Pros:**

- Automatic discovery with OneAgent
- AI-driven root-cause analysis
- Strong enterprise support

**Cons:**

- Expensive
- Less approachable for small teams

---

### Elastic Observability

_Observability built on the Elastic Stack_

**Pros:**

- Powerful log search
- Unified logs, metrics and APM
- Self-managed or Elastic Cloud

**Cons:**

- Cluster management overhead when self-hosted
- Resource-intensive at scale

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Dynatrace — AI-powered observability and security platform. Elastic Observability — observability built on the Elastic Stack.
- Licensing differs: Dynatrace is proprietary while Elastic Observability is open source.
- **Deployment:** Dynatrace — managed cloud. Elastic Observability — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- Elastic Observability can be started for free, while Dynatrace requires a paid plan. Dynatrace pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Dynatrace — automatic discovery with OneAgent. Elastic Observability — powerful log search.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Dynatrace better than Elastic Observability?

It depends on your requirements. Dynatrace is a strong fit for large enterprises wanting automated root-cause analysis, while Elastic Observability suits teams already using Elasticsearch for logs.

### Is Dynatrace free to use?

Dynatrace does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Is Elastic Observability free to use?

Yes, you can start with Elastic Observability for free. Pricing model: Free open source; paid managed cloud.

### Can I self-host Dynatrace or Elastic Observability?

Dynatrace is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Elastic Observability can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Dynatrace and Elastic Observability?

Dynatrace: expensive; less approachable for small teams. Elastic Observability: cluster management overhead when self-hosted; resource-intensive at scale.

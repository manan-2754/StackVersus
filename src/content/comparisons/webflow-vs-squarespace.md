## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Webflow — Visual web development platform. Squarespace — design-led website builder.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Webflow can be started for free, while Squarespace requires a paid plan. Squarespace pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Webflow — fine-grained visual CSS control. Squarespace — beautiful templates.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Webflow better than Squarespace?

It depends on your requirements. Webflow is a strong fit for designers building production marketing sites, while Squarespace suits creatives and small businesses wanting polished templates.

### Is Webflow free to use?

Yes, you can start with Webflow for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Squarespace free to use?

Squarespace does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host Webflow or Squarespace?

Webflow is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Squarespace is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Webflow and Squarespace?

Webflow: learning curve; separate site and workspace plans. Squarespace: limited customization; no free plan.

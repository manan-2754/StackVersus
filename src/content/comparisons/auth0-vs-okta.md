## Detailed Breakdown

### Auth0

_Customer identity platform by Okta_

**Pros:**

- Extensive OIDC and SAML support
- Actions for custom auth logic
- Enterprise SSO and MFA

**Cons:**

- Pricing grows steeply with monthly active users
- Complex configuration

---

### Okta

_Enterprise workforce and customer identity_

**Pros:**

- Industry-standard workforce SSO
- Thousands of prebuilt integrations
- Strong compliance posture

**Cons:**

- Expensive for small teams
- Heavyweight for simple apps

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Auth0 — customer identity platform by Okta. Okta — enterprise workforce and customer identity.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Auth0 can be started for free, while Okta requires a paid plan. Okta pricing: custom enterprise pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Auth0 — extensive OIDC and SAML support. Okta — industry-standard workforce SSO.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Auth0 better than Okta?

It depends on your requirements. Auth0 is a strong fit for enterprises needing flexible, standards-based identity, while Okta suits large enterprises managing employee identity.

### Is Auth0 free to use?

Yes, you can start with Auth0 for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Okta free to use?

Okta does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Custom enterprise pricing.

### Can I self-host Auth0 or Okta?

Auth0 is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Okta is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Auth0 and Okta?

Auth0: pricing grows steeply with monthly active users; complex configuration. Okta: expensive for small teams; heavyweight for simple apps.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Honeycomb

_Observability for high-cardinality event data_

**Pros:**

- Fast queries on high-cardinality data
- BubbleUp outlier analysis
- OpenTelemetry-first

**Cons:**

- Different mental model from metrics dashboards
- Less breadth than all-in-one suites

---

### Elastic Observability

_Observability built on the Elastic Stack_

**Pros:**

- Powerful log search
- Unified logs, metrics and APM
- Self-managed or Elastic Cloud

**Cons:**

- Cluster management overhead when self-hosted
- Resource-intensive at scale

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Honeycomb — observability for high-cardinality event data. Elastic Observability — observability built on the Elastic Stack.
- Licensing differs: Honeycomb is proprietary while Elastic Observability is open source.
- **Deployment:** Honeycomb — managed cloud. Elastic Observability — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Honeycomb — free tier with paid plans. Elastic Observability — free open source; paid managed cloud.
- **Signature strength:** Honeycomb — fast queries on high-cardinality data. Elastic Observability — powerful log search.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Replicate

_Run and fine-tune models through a cloud API_

**Pros:**

- Simple API for thousands of models
- Cog for packaging models
- Per-second billing

**Cons:**

- Cold starts for less popular models
- Costs at scale

---

### Fireworks AI

_Fast inference platform for generative AI_

**Pros:**

- Very fast inference
- Function-calling models
- Fine-tuning

**Cons:**

- Smaller model catalog than Hugging Face
- Enterprise features are paid

Replicate does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Pay-as-you-go usage pricing.

### Is Fireworks AI free to use?

Fireworks AI does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Pay-as-you-go usage pricing.

### Can I self-host Replicate or Fireworks AI?

Replicate is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Fireworks AI is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

Replicate: cold starts for less popular models; costs at scale. Fireworks AI: smaller model catalog than Hugging Face; enterprise features are paid.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Asana

_Work management platform for teams_

**Pros:**

- Timelines, portfolios and goals
- Clean interface
- Strong integrations

**Cons:**

- Expensive for larger teams
- Single assignee per task

---

### ClickUp

_All-in-one productivity and project platform_

**Pros:**

- Highly customizable
- Many views
- Docs and whiteboards built in

**Cons:**

- Can feel overwhelming
- Slowdowns in large workspaces

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Asana — work management platform for teams. ClickUp — all-in-one productivity and project platform.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Asana — free tier with paid plans. ClickUp — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Asana — timelines, portfolios and goals. ClickUp — highly customizable.

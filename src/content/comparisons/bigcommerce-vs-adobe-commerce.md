# BigCommerce vs Adobe Commerce: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> BigCommerce is the better pick for mid-market brands needing built-in features. Adobe Commerce is the better pick for large enterprises with complex catalogs.

## Detailed Breakdown

### BigCommerce

_SaaS commerce platform for growing brands_

**Pros:**

- No extra transaction fees
- Many built-in features
- Headless commerce support

**Cons:**

- Plan upgrades tied to revenue
- Smaller app store

---

### Adobe Commerce

_Enterprise commerce platform built on Magento_

**Pros:**

- Highly customizable
- B2B features
- Open-source edition available

**Cons:**

- Expensive to run and develop
- Complex architecture

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** BigCommerce — SaaS commerce platform for growing brands. Adobe Commerce — enterprise commerce platform built on Magento.
- Licensing differs: BigCommerce is proprietary while Adobe Commerce is open-core.
- **Deployment:** BigCommerce — managed cloud. Adobe Commerce — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- Adobe Commerce can be started for free, while BigCommerce requires a paid plan. BigCommerce pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** BigCommerce — no extra transaction fees. Adobe Commerce — highly customizable.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is BigCommerce better than Adobe Commerce?

It depends on your requirements. BigCommerce is a strong fit for mid-market brands needing built-in features, while Adobe Commerce suits large enterprises with complex catalogs.

### Is BigCommerce free to use?

BigCommerce does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Is Adobe Commerce free to use?

Yes, you can start with Adobe Commerce for free. Pricing model: Free Magento Open Source; licensed Adobe Commerce.

### Can I self-host BigCommerce or Adobe Commerce?

BigCommerce is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Adobe Commerce can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of BigCommerce and Adobe Commerce?

BigCommerce: plan upgrades tied to revenue; smaller app store. Adobe Commerce: expensive to run and develop; complex architecture.

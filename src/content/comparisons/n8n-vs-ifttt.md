## Detailed Breakdown

### n8n

_Workflow automation with code when you need it_

**Pros:**

- Self-hostable
- JavaScript and Python code nodes
- AI agent workflows

**Cons:**

- Fair-code license limits some commercial uses
- More technical than Zapier

---

### IFTTT

_Simple consumer automation applets_

**Pros:**

- Very simple
- Smart-home integrations
- Mobile app

**Cons:**

- Limited for business workflows
- Restricted free plan

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** n8n — workflow automation with code when you need it. IFTTT — simple consumer automation applets.
- Licensing differs: n8n is source-available while IFTTT is proprietary.
- **Deployment:** n8n — self-hosted or managed cloud. IFTTT — managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** n8n — free self-hosted community edition; paid cloud. IFTTT — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** n8n — self-hostable. IFTTT — very simple.

## Frequently Asked Questions

It depends on your requirements. n8n is a strong fit for technical teams wanting self-hosted automation, while IFTTT suits personal and smart-home automations.

### Is n8n free to use?

Yes, you can start with n8n for free. Pricing model: Free self-hosted community edition; paid cloud.

### Is IFTTT free to use?

Yes, you can start with IFTTT for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host n8n or IFTTT?

n8n can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud. IFTTT is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of n8n and IFTTT?

n8n: fair-code license limits some commercial uses; more technical than Zapier. IFTTT: limited for business workflows; restricted free plan.

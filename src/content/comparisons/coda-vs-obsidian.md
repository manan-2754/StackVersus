## Detailed Breakdown

### Coda

_All-in-one doc that works like an app_

**Pros:**

- Powerful formulas and Packs
- Docs that behave like apps
- Built-in automations

**Cons:**

- Learning curve for formulas
- Smaller community

---

### Obsidian

_Local-first Markdown knowledge base_

**Pros:**

- Plain Markdown files you own
- Huge plugin ecosystem
- Graph view and backlinks

**Cons:**

- Sync and Publish cost extra
- Limited real-time collaboration

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Coda — all-in-one doc that works like an app. Obsidian — local-first Markdown knowledge base.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Deployment:** Coda — managed cloud. Obsidian — desktop app.
- **Pricing:** Coda — free tier with paid plans. Obsidian — free app; paid Sync and Publish.
- **Signature strength:** Coda — powerful formulas and Packs. Obsidian — plain Markdown files you own.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Coda better than Obsidian?

It depends on your requirements. Coda is a strong fit for teams building doc-based tools and workflows, while Obsidian suits individuals who want private, local Markdown notes.

### Is Coda free to use?

Yes, you can start with Coda for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Obsidian free to use?

Yes, you can start with Obsidian for free. Pricing model: Free app; paid Sync and Publish.

### Can I self-host Coda or Obsidian?

Coda is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Obsidian runs locally on your own machine.

### What are the main drawbacks of Coda and Obsidian?

Coda: learning curve for formulas; smaller community. Obsidian: sync and Publish cost extra; limited real-time collaboration.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Windsurf better than JetBrains AI Assistant?

It depends on your requirements. Windsurf is a strong fit for developers wanting an agent-first IDE, while JetBrains AI Assistant suits developers using IntelliJ, PyCharm and other JetBrains IDEs.

### Is Windsurf free to use?

Yes, you can start with Windsurf for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is JetBrains AI Assistant free to use?

Yes, you can start with JetBrains AI Assistant for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Windsurf or JetBrains AI Assistant?

Windsurf runs locally on your own machine. JetBrains AI Assistant runs inside your own stack: built into JetBrains IDEs.

### What are the main drawbacks of Windsurf and JetBrains AI Assistant?

Windsurf: rapid product changes; smaller community than Cursor. JetBrains AI Assistant: only for JetBrains IDEs; quota-based usage.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Opsgenie

_Atlassian on-call and alert management_

**Pros:**

- Tight Jira integration
- Flexible alert routing
- Affordable on-call

**Cons:**

- Atlassian stopped selling Opsgenie in favor of Jira Service Management
- Limited future roadmap

---

### Rootly

_Incident management in Slack and Microsoft Teams_

**Pros:**

- Highly configurable workflows
- Slack and Microsoft Teams support
- Retrospective tooling

**Cons:**

- Many options to configure
- Smaller community

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Opsgenie — Atlassian on-call and alert management. Rootly — incident management in Slack and Microsoft Teams.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Opsgenie can be started for free, while Rootly requires a paid plan. Rootly pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Opsgenie — tight Jira integration. Rootly — highly configurable workflows.

It depends on your requirements. Opsgenie is a strong fit for existing Atlassian customers, while Rootly suits teams automating incident workflows.

Yes, you can start with Opsgenie for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Rootly free to use?

Rootly does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host Opsgenie or Rootly?

Opsgenie is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Rootly is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

Opsgenie: Atlassian stopped selling Opsgenie in favor of Jira Service Management; limited future roadmap. Rootly: many options to configure; smaller community.

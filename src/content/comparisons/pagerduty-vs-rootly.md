## Detailed Breakdown

### PagerDuty

_Incident response and on-call management platform_

**Pros:**

- Robust on-call scheduling and escalation
- Hundreds of integrations
- Event intelligence features

**Cons:**

- Expensive per user
- Advanced features in higher tiers

---

### Rootly

_Incident management in Slack and Microsoft Teams_

**Pros:**

- Highly configurable workflows
- Slack and Microsoft Teams support
- Retrospective tooling

**Cons:**

- Many options to configure
- Smaller community

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** PagerDuty — incident response and on-call management platform. Rootly — incident management in Slack and Microsoft Teams.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- PagerDuty can be started for free, while Rootly requires a paid plan. Rootly pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** PagerDuty — robust on-call scheduling and escalation. Rootly — highly configurable workflows.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is PagerDuty better than Rootly?

It depends on your requirements. PagerDuty is a strong fit for organizations needing mature on-call and escalation, while Rootly suits teams automating incident workflows.

### Is PagerDuty free to use?

Yes, you can start with PagerDuty for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Rootly free to use?

Rootly does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host PagerDuty or Rootly?

PagerDuty is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Rootly is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of PagerDuty and Rootly?

PagerDuty: expensive per user; advanced features in higher tiers. Rootly: many options to configure; smaller community.

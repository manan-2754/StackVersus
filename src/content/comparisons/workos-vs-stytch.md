## Detailed Breakdown

### WorkOS

_Enterprise features like SSO and SCIM for B2B SaaS_

**Pros:**

- Easy SAML SSO and directory sync
- AuthKit user management with a free tier
- Admin portal for customer IT teams

**Cons:**

- Per-connection pricing for SSO
- Focused on B2B use cases

---

### Stytch

_Authentication and fraud prevention APIs_

**Pros:**

- Strong passwordless options
- Device fingerprinting and fraud tools
- Headless APIs and prebuilt UI

**Cons:**

- Smaller community
- Advanced fraud features cost extra

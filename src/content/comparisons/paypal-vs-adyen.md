## Detailed Breakdown

### PayPal

_Global online payments platform_

**Pros:**

- Huge consumer trust and reach
- Buyer accounts in many countries
- Simple checkout buttons

**Cons:**

- Higher fees on some transaction types
- Dispute and hold policies can frustrate sellers

---

### Adyen

_Enterprise payments platform_

**Pros:**

- One platform for online and in-person payments
- Strong global acquiring
- Advanced risk management

**Cons:**

- Aimed at high-volume merchants
- Less self-serve for small businesses

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** PayPal — global online payments platform. Adyen — enterprise payments platform.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- PayPal can be started for free, while Adyen requires a paid plan. Adyen pricing: custom enterprise pricing.
- **Signature strength:** PayPal — huge consumer trust and reach. Adyen — one platform for online and in-person payments.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is PayPal better than Adyen?

It depends on your requirements. PayPal is a strong fit for businesses whose customers expect PayPal checkout, while Adyen suits large enterprises processing payments globally.

### Is PayPal free to use?

Yes, you can start with PayPal for free. Pricing model: Per-transaction fees, no monthly fee.

### Is Adyen free to use?

Adyen does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Custom enterprise pricing.

### Can I self-host PayPal or Adyen?

PayPal is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Adyen is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of PayPal and Adyen?

PayPal: higher fees on some transaction types; dispute and hold policies can frustrate sellers. Adyen: aimed at high-volume merchants; less self-serve for small businesses.

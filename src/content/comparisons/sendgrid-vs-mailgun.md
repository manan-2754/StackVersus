## Detailed Breakdown

### SendGrid

_Email delivery platform by Twilio_

**Pros:**

- Handles very high volume
- Marketing and transactional email in one platform
- Mature APIs

**Cons:**

- Shared IP deliverability varies
- Support can be slow on lower plans

---

### Mailgun

_Email API service by Sinch_

**Pros:**

- Powerful routing and inbound parsing
- Email validation API
- Detailed logs

**Cons:**

- Free tier is limited
- Plan tiers can be confusing

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** SendGrid — email delivery platform by Twilio. Mailgun — email API service by Sinch.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Mailgun can be started for free, while SendGrid requires a paid plan. SendGrid pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** SendGrid — handles very high volume. Mailgun — powerful routing and inbound parsing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is SendGrid better than Mailgun?

It depends on your requirements. SendGrid is a strong fit for high-volume transactional and marketing email, while Mailgun suits developers needing flexible sending and email validation.

### Is SendGrid free to use?

SendGrid does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Is Mailgun free to use?

Yes, you can start with Mailgun for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host SendGrid or Mailgun?

SendGrid is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Mailgun is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of SendGrid and Mailgun?

SendGrid: shared IP deliverability varies; support can be slow on lower plans. Mailgun: free tier is limited; plan tiers can be confusing.

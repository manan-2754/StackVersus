## Detailed Breakdown

### Wix

_Drag-and-drop website builder_

**Pros:**

- Very easy editor
- Many templates
- Large app market

**Cons:**

- Switching templates is difficult
- Slower page performance

---

### WordPress

_Open-source CMS powering a large share of the web_

**Pros:**

- Huge plugin and theme ecosystem
- Open source
- Host anywhere

**Cons:**

- Plugin security and maintenance burden
- Performance tuning needed

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Wix — drag-and-drop website builder. WordPress — open-source CMS powering a large share of the web.
- Licensing differs: Wix is proprietary while WordPress is open source.
- **Deployment:** Wix — managed cloud. WordPress — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Wix — free tier with paid plans. WordPress — free and open source.
- **Signature strength:** Wix — very easy editor. WordPress — huge plugin and theme ecosystem.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Wix better than WordPress?

It depends on your requirements. Wix is a strong fit for small businesses wanting an all-in-one site, while WordPress suits sites needing maximum flexibility and plugins.

### Is Wix free to use?

Yes, you can start with Wix for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is WordPress free to use?

Yes, you can start with WordPress for free. Pricing model: Free and open source.

### Can I self-host Wix or WordPress?

Wix is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. WordPress can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Wix and WordPress?

Wix: switching templates is difficult; slower page performance. WordPress: plugin security and maintenance burden; performance tuning needed.

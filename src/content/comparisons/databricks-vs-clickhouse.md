# Databricks vs ClickHouse: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Databricks is the better pick for teams combining data engineering, ML and analytics. ClickHouse is the better pick for real-time analytics on large event datasets.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Databricks

_Data intelligence platform built on the lakehouse_

**Pros:**

- Unified lakehouse with Delta Lake
- Strong ML and Spark support
- Multi-cloud

**Cons:**

- Complex pricing
- Steeper learning curve for SQL-only users

---

### ClickHouse

_Open-source column-oriented OLAP database_

**Pros:**

- Extremely fast aggregations
- Open source
- Managed ClickHouse Cloud option

**Cons:**

- Joins and updates are less ergonomic
- Requires operational tuning

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Databricks — data intelligence platform built on the lakehouse. ClickHouse — open-source column-oriented OLAP database.
- Licensing differs: Databricks is proprietary while ClickHouse is open source.
- **Deployment:** Databricks — managed cloud. ClickHouse — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- ClickHouse can be started for free, while Databricks requires a paid plan. Databricks pricing: pay-as-you-go usage pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Databricks — unified lakehouse with Delta Lake. ClickHouse — extremely fast aggregations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Databricks better than ClickHouse?

It depends on your requirements. Databricks is a strong fit for teams combining data engineering, ML and analytics, while ClickHouse suits real-time analytics on large event datasets.

### Is Databricks free to use?

Databricks does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Pay-as-you-go usage pricing.

### Is ClickHouse free to use?

Yes, you can start with ClickHouse for free. Pricing model: Free open source; paid managed cloud.

### Can I self-host Databricks or ClickHouse?

Databricks is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. ClickHouse can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Databricks and ClickHouse?

Databricks: complex pricing; steeper learning curve for SQL-only users. ClickHouse: joins and updates are less ergonomic; requires operational tuning.

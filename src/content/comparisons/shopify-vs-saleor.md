# Shopify vs Saleor: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Shopify is the better pick for merchants wanting a fully hosted store. Saleor is the better pick for headless commerce with a GraphQL API.

---

| Feature           | Shopify                                  | Saleor                               |

## Detailed Breakdown

### Shopify

_Hosted commerce platform_

**Pros:**

- Easy setup
- Huge app ecosystem
- Reliable checkout

**Cons:**

- Extra transaction fees without Shopify Payments
- App subscriptions add up

---

### Saleor

_Open-source GraphQL-first commerce platform_

**Pros:**

- GraphQL API
- Python and Django backend
- Multi-channel support

**Cons:**

- Smaller community
- Requires development resources

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Shopify — hosted commerce platform. Saleor — open-source GraphQL-first commerce platform.
- Licensing differs: Shopify is proprietary while Saleor is open source.
- **Deployment:** Shopify — managed cloud. Saleor — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- Saleor can be started for free, while Shopify requires a paid plan. Shopify pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Shopify — easy setup. Saleor — GraphQL API.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Shopify better than Saleor?

It depends on your requirements. Shopify is a strong fit for merchants wanting a fully hosted store, while Saleor suits headless commerce with a GraphQL API.

### Is Shopify free to use?

Shopify does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Is Saleor free to use?

Yes, you can start with Saleor for free. Pricing model: Free open source; paid managed cloud.

### Can I self-host Shopify or Saleor?

Shopify is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Saleor can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Shopify and Saleor?

Shopify: extra transaction fees without Shopify Payments; app subscriptions add up. Saleor: smaller community; requires development resources.

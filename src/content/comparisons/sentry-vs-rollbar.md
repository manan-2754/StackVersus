# Sentry vs Rollbar: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Sentry is the better pick for developers wanting detailed error and performance monitoring. Rollbar is the better pick for teams wanting simple error alerting with smart grouping.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Sentry

_Application monitoring and error tracking_

**Pros:**

- Rich stack traces and context
- Performance tracing and session replay
- SDKs for most languages

**Cons:**

- Event quotas require tuning
- Self-hosting is resource-heavy

---

### Rollbar

_Real-time error tracking with smart grouping_

**Pros:**

- Smart error grouping
- Deploy tracking
- Straightforward setup

**Cons:**

- Fewer performance features than Sentry
- Smaller ecosystem

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Sentry — application monitoring and error tracking. Rollbar — real-time error tracking with smart grouping.
- Licensing differs: Sentry is source-available while Rollbar is proprietary.
- **Deployment:** Sentry — self-hosted or managed cloud. Rollbar — managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Sentry — free tier with paid plans. Rollbar — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Sentry — rich stack traces and context. Rollbar — smart error grouping.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Sentry better than Rollbar?

It depends on your requirements. Sentry is a strong fit for developers wanting detailed error and performance monitoring, while Rollbar suits teams wanting simple error alerting with smart grouping.

### Is Sentry free to use?

Yes, you can start with Sentry for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Rollbar free to use?

Yes, you can start with Rollbar for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Sentry or Rollbar?

Sentry can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud. Rollbar is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Sentry and Rollbar?

Sentry: event quotas require tuning; self-hosting is resource-heavy. Rollbar: fewer performance features than Sentry; smaller ecosystem.

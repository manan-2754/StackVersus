**Pros:**

- Excellent web clipper
- Document scanning with search
- Cross-platform apps

**Cons:**

- Free plan is heavily limited
- Repeated price increases

### Anytype

_Local-first, end-to-end encrypted workspace_

**Pros:**

- End-to-end encryption
- Local-first with peer-to-peer sync
- Object-based structure

**Cons:**

- Younger product
- Collaboration features still developing

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Evernote — long-running note-taking app. Anytype — local-first, end-to-end encrypted workspace.
- Licensing differs: Evernote is proprietary while Anytype is source-available.
- **Deployment:** Evernote — managed cloud. Anytype — desktop app.
- **Pricing:** Evernote — free tier with paid plans. Anytype — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Evernote — excellent web clipper. Anytype — end-to-end encryption.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Evernote better than Anytype?

It depends on your requirements. Evernote is a strong fit for personal note capture and web clipping, while Anytype suits privacy-focused users wanting a local-first Notion alternative.

### Is Evernote free to use?

Yes, you can start with Evernote for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Anytype free to use?

Yes, you can start with Anytype for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Evernote or Anytype?

Evernote is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Anytype runs locally on your own machine.

### What are the main drawbacks of Evernote and Anytype?

Evernote: free plan is heavily limited; repeated price increases. Anytype: younger product; collaboration features still developing.

# Cloudflare Pages vs Heroku: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Cloudflare Pages is the better pick for static and edge-rendered sites that need generous free bandwidth. Heroku is the better pick for teams that want a mature, simple PaaS.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Cloudflare Pages

_Static and full-stack hosting on Cloudflare’s network_

**Pros:**

- Generous free tier with unlimited static bandwidth
- Integrates with Workers, R2 and D1
- Fast global network

**Cons:**

- Workers runtime is not full Node.js
- Cloudflare is steering new projects toward Workers

---

### Heroku

_Pioneering platform as a service by Salesforce_

**Pros:**

- Very simple git push deploys
- Mature add-on marketplace
- Well documented

**Cons:**

- No free dynos since 2022
- Higher cost than newer platforms

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Cloudflare Pages — static and full-stack hosting on Cloudflare’s network. Heroku — pioneering platform as a service by Salesforce.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Cloudflare Pages can be started for free, while Heroku requires a paid plan. Heroku pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Cloudflare Pages — generous free tier with unlimited static bandwidth. Heroku — very simple git push deploys.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Cloudflare Pages better than Heroku?

It depends on your requirements. Cloudflare Pages is a strong fit for static and edge-rendered sites that need generous free bandwidth, while Heroku suits teams that want a mature, simple PaaS.

### Is Cloudflare Pages free to use?

Yes, you can start with Cloudflare Pages for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Heroku free to use?

Heroku does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host Cloudflare Pages or Heroku?

Cloudflare Pages is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Heroku is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Cloudflare Pages and Heroku?

Cloudflare Pages: workers runtime is not full Node.js; Cloudflare is steering new projects toward Workers. Heroku: no free dynos since 2022; higher cost than newer platforms.

# Jenkins vs Travis CI: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Jenkins is the better pick for teams that need maximum control over self-hosted CI. Travis CI is the better pick for simple CI for small projects.

---

## Detailed Breakdown

### Jenkins

_Extensible open-source automation server_

**Pros:**

- Thousands of plugins
- Completely free and self-hosted
- Runs almost any workload

**Cons:**

- Significant maintenance overhead
- Dated UI and plugin compatibility issues

---

### Travis CI

_Hosted continuous integration service_

**Pros:**

- Simple YAML configuration
- Long history with open-source projects
- Build matrix support

**Cons:**

- No longer offers unlimited free builds for open source
- Smaller ecosystem than newer tools

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Jenkins — extensible open-source automation server. Travis CI — hosted continuous integration service.
- Licensing differs: Jenkins is open source while Travis CI is proprietary.
- **Deployment:** Jenkins — self-hosted. Travis CI — managed cloud.
- Jenkins can be started for free, while Travis CI requires a paid plan. Travis CI pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Jenkins — thousands of plugins. Travis CI — simple YAML configuration.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Jenkins better than Travis CI?

It depends on your requirements. Jenkins is a strong fit for teams that need maximum control over self-hosted CI, while Travis CI suits simple CI for small projects.

### Is Jenkins free to use?

Yes, you can start with Jenkins for free. Pricing model: Free and open source.

### Is Travis CI free to use?

Travis CI does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host Jenkins or Travis CI?

Jenkins can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted. Travis CI is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Jenkins and Travis CI?

Jenkins: significant maintenance overhead; dated UI and plugin compatibility issues. Travis CI: no longer offers unlimited free builds for open source; smaller ecosystem than newer tools.

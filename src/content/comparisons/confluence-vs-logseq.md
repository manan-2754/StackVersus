## Detailed Breakdown

### Confluence

_Team wiki by Atlassian_

**Pros:**

- Deep Jira integration
- Structured spaces and permissions
- Enterprise features

**Cons:**

- Editor can feel clunky
- Search quality varies

---

### Logseq

_Open-source outliner for networked notes_

**Pros:**

- Block-based outliner
- Open source
- Local Markdown files

**Cons:**

- Database version is still maturing
- Can slow down with large graphs

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Confluence — team wiki by Atlassian. Logseq — open-source outliner for networked notes.
- Licensing differs: Confluence is proprietary while Logseq is open source.
- **Deployment:** Confluence — managed cloud. Logseq — desktop app.
- **Pricing:** Confluence — free tier with paid plans. Logseq — free and open source.
- **Signature strength:** Confluence — deep Jira integration. Logseq — block-based outliner.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Confluence better than Logseq?

It depends on your requirements. Confluence is a strong fit for organizations already using Jira, while Logseq suits outliner fans wanting local, open-source notes.

### Is Confluence free to use?

Yes, you can start with Confluence for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Logseq free to use?

Yes, you can start with Logseq for free. Pricing model: Free and open source.

### Can I self-host Confluence or Logseq?

Confluence is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Logseq runs locally on your own machine.

### What are the main drawbacks of Confluence and Logseq?

Confluence: editor can feel clunky; search quality varies. Logseq: database version is still maturing; can slow down with large graphs.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Netlify

_Platform for deploying modern web projects_

**Pros:**

- Simple Git-based deploys
- Built-in forms and serverless functions
- Framework-agnostic

**Cons:**

- Bandwidth and build minutes add up
- Less Next.js-specific optimization than Vercel

---

### Railway

_Infrastructure platform for deploying apps and databases_

**Pros:**

- Very fast setup from a repository
- One-click databases
- Usage-based pricing

**Cons:**

- Costs less predictable than flat plans
- Less control than raw cloud infrastructure

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Netlify — platform for deploying modern web projects. Railway — infrastructure platform for deploying apps and databases.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Netlify can be started for free, while Railway requires a paid plan. Railway pricing: pay-as-you-go usage pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Netlify — simple Git-based deploys. Railway — very fast setup from a repository.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Netlify better than Railway?

It depends on your requirements. Netlify is a strong fit for Jamstack sites and framework-agnostic frontends, while Railway suits quick deployment of full-stack apps and side projects.

### Is Netlify free to use?

Yes, you can start with Netlify for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Railway free to use?

Railway does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Pay-as-you-go usage pricing.

### Can I self-host Netlify or Railway?

Netlify is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Railway is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Netlify and Railway?

Netlify: bandwidth and build minutes add up; less Next.js-specific optimization than Vercel. Railway: costs less predictable than flat plans; less control than raw cloud infrastructure.

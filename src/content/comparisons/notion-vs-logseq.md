## Detailed Breakdown

### Notion

_Connected workspace for docs, wikis and projects_

**Pros:**

- Flexible blocks and databases
- Large template gallery
- Built-in AI features

**Cons:**

- Limited offline support
- Can slow down in large workspaces

---

### Logseq

_Open-source outliner for networked notes_

**Pros:**

- Block-based outliner
- Open source
- Local Markdown files

**Cons:**

- Database version is still maturing
- Can slow down with large graphs

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Notion — connected workspace for docs, wikis and projects. Logseq — open-source outliner for networked notes.
- Licensing differs: Notion is proprietary while Logseq is open source.
- **Deployment:** Notion — managed cloud. Logseq — desktop app.
- **Pricing:** Notion — free tier with paid plans. Logseq — free and open source.
- **Signature strength:** Notion — flexible blocks and databases. Logseq — block-based outliner.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Notion better than Logseq?

It depends on your requirements. Notion is a strong fit for teams wanting flexible docs and lightweight databases, while Logseq suits outliner fans wanting local, open-source notes.

### Is Notion free to use?

Yes, you can start with Notion for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Logseq free to use?

Yes, you can start with Logseq for free. Pricing model: Free and open source.

### Can I self-host Notion or Logseq?

Notion is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Logseq runs locally on your own machine.

### What are the main drawbacks of Notion and Logseq?

Notion: limited offline support; can slow down in large workspaces. Logseq: database version is still maturing; can slow down with large graphs.

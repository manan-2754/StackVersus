## Detailed Breakdown

### PostHog

_Open-source product analytics suite_

**Pros:**

- Analytics, session replay, feature flags and experiments
- Generous free usage tier
- Open source

**Cons:**

- Breadth means some tools are less deep
- Self-hosting is only supported at small scale

---

### Pendo

_Product experience platform with analytics and in-app guides_

**Pros:**

- In-app guides and walkthroughs
- Feedback and NPS tools
- Analytics without engineering work

**Cons:**

- Expensive for small teams
- Analytics less deep than dedicated tools

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** PostHog — open-source product analytics suite. Pendo — product experience platform with analytics and in-app guides.
- Licensing differs: PostHog is open source while Pendo is proprietary.
- **Deployment:** PostHog — self-hosted or managed cloud. Pendo — managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** PostHog — free tier, then usage-based pricing. Pendo — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** PostHog — analytics, session replay, feature flags and experiments. Pendo — in-app guides and walkthroughs.

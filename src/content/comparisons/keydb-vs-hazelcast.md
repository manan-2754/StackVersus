## Detailed Breakdown

### KeyDB

_Multithreaded fork of Redis maintained by Snap_

**Pros:**

- Multithreaded
- Active-active replication
- Redis compatible

**Cons:**

- Development has slowed
- Smaller community

---

### Hazelcast

_Distributed in-memory computing platform_

**Pros:**

- Distributed data structures
- Stream processing
- Strong Java integration

**Cons:**

- Heavier than simple caches
- Enterprise features are paid

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** KeyDB — multithreaded fork of Redis maintained by Snap. Hazelcast — distributed in-memory computing platform.
- Licensing differs: KeyDB is open source while Hazelcast is open-core.
- **Deployment:** KeyDB — self-hosted. Hazelcast — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** KeyDB — free and open source. Hazelcast — free community edition; paid enterprise.
- **Signature strength:** KeyDB — multithreaded. Hazelcast — distributed data structures.

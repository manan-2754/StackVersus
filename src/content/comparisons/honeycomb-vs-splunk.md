# Honeycomb vs Splunk: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Detailed Breakdown

### Honeycomb

_Observability for high-cardinality event data_

**Pros:**

- Fast queries on high-cardinality data
- BubbleUp outlier analysis
- OpenTelemetry-first

**Cons:**

- Different mental model from metrics dashboards
- Less breadth than all-in-one suites

---

### Splunk

_Enterprise data and observability platform by Cisco_

**Pros:**

- Powerful search processing language
- Strong security analytics
- Enterprise-grade scale

**Cons:**

- Very expensive at high ingest volumes
- Steep learning curve

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Honeycomb — observability for high-cardinality event data. Splunk — enterprise data and observability platform by Cisco.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Deployment:** Honeycomb — managed cloud. Splunk — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- Honeycomb can be started for free, while Splunk requires a paid plan. Splunk pricing: custom enterprise pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Honeycomb — fast queries on high-cardinality data. Splunk — powerful search processing language.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Honeycomb better than Splunk?

It depends on your requirements. Honeycomb is a strong fit for teams debugging distributed systems with OpenTelemetry, while Splunk suits enterprises with heavy log analytics and security needs.

### Is Honeycomb free to use?

Yes, you can start with Honeycomb for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Splunk free to use?

Splunk does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Custom enterprise pricing.

### Can I self-host Honeycomb or Splunk?

Honeycomb is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Splunk can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Honeycomb and Splunk?

Honeycomb: different mental model from metrics dashboards; less breadth than all-in-one suites. Splunk: very expensive at high ingest volumes; steep learning curve.

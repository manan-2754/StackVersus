## Detailed Breakdown

### BigCommerce

_SaaS commerce platform for growing brands_

**Pros:**

- No extra transaction fees
- Many built-in features
- Headless commerce support

**Cons:**

- Plan upgrades tied to revenue
- Smaller app store

---

### Medusa

_Open-source composable commerce engine_

**Pros:**

- Modular Node.js architecture
- Open source
- Headless by design

**Cons:**

- Requires developers
- Smaller ecosystem

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** BigCommerce — SaaS commerce platform for growing brands. Medusa — open-source composable commerce engine.
- Licensing differs: BigCommerce is proprietary while Medusa is open source.
- **Deployment:** BigCommerce — managed cloud. Medusa — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- Medusa can be started for free, while BigCommerce requires a paid plan. BigCommerce pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** BigCommerce — no extra transaction fees. Medusa — modular Node.js architecture.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is BigCommerce better than Medusa?

It depends on your requirements. BigCommerce is a strong fit for mid-market brands needing built-in features, while Medusa suits developers building custom headless commerce.

### Is BigCommerce free to use?

BigCommerce does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Is Medusa free to use?

Yes, you can start with Medusa for free. Pricing model: Free open source; paid managed cloud.

### Can I self-host BigCommerce or Medusa?

BigCommerce is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Medusa can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of BigCommerce and Medusa?

BigCommerce: plan upgrades tied to revenue; smaller app store. Medusa: requires developers; smaller ecosystem.

# Obsidian vs Evernote: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Obsidian is the better pick for individuals who want private, local Markdown notes. Evernote is the better pick for personal note capture and web clipping.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Obsidian

_Local-first Markdown knowledge base_

**Pros:**

- Plain Markdown files you own
- Huge plugin ecosystem
- Graph view and backlinks

**Cons:**

- Sync and Publish cost extra
- Limited real-time collaboration

---

### Evernote

_Long-running note-taking app_

**Pros:**

- Excellent web clipper
- Document scanning with search
- Cross-platform apps

**Cons:**

- Free plan is heavily limited
- Repeated price increases

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Obsidian — local-first Markdown knowledge base. Evernote — long-running note-taking app.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Deployment:** Obsidian — desktop app. Evernote — managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Obsidian — free app; paid Sync and Publish. Evernote — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Obsidian — plain Markdown files you own. Evernote — excellent web clipper.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Obsidian better than Evernote?

It depends on your requirements. Obsidian is a strong fit for individuals who want private, local Markdown notes, while Evernote suits personal note capture and web clipping.

### Is Obsidian free to use?

Yes, you can start with Obsidian for free. Pricing model: Free app; paid Sync and Publish.

### Is Evernote free to use?

Yes, you can start with Evernote for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Obsidian or Evernote?

Obsidian runs locally on your own machine. Evernote is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Obsidian and Evernote?

Obsidian: sync and Publish cost extra; limited real-time collaboration. Evernote: free plan is heavily limited; repeated price increases.

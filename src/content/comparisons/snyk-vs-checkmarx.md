**Pros:**

- Fix suggestions

**Cons:**

**Pros:**

- Compliance features

**Cons:**

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Snyk — developer security platform. Checkmarx — enterprise application security testing.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Deployment:** Snyk — managed cloud. Checkmarx — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- Snyk can be started for free, while Checkmarx requires a paid plan. Checkmarx pricing: custom enterprise pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Snyk — dependency, code, container and IaC scanning. Checkmarx — broad SAST and SCA coverage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Snyk better than Checkmarx?

It depends on your requirements. Snyk is a strong fit for developer-first dependency and code scanning, while Checkmarx suits large enterprises with compliance needs.

### Is Snyk free to use?

Yes, you can start with Snyk for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Checkmarx free to use?

Checkmarx does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Custom enterprise pricing.

### Can I self-host Snyk or Checkmarx?

Snyk is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Checkmarx can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Snyk and Checkmarx?

Snyk: can get expensive; alert noise without tuning. Checkmarx: expensive; slower scans.

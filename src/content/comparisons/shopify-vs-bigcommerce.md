# Shopify vs BigCommerce: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Shopify is the better pick for merchants wanting a fully hosted store. BigCommerce is the better pick for mid-market brands needing built-in features.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Shopify

_Hosted commerce platform_

**Pros:**

- Easy setup
- Huge app ecosystem
- Reliable checkout

**Cons:**

- Extra transaction fees without Shopify Payments
- App subscriptions add up

---

### BigCommerce

_SaaS commerce platform for growing brands_

**Pros:**

- No extra transaction fees
- Many built-in features
- Headless commerce support

**Cons:**

- Plan upgrades tied to revenue
- Smaller app store

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Shopify — hosted commerce platform. BigCommerce — SaaS commerce platform for growing brands.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Shopify — paid plans; free trial available. BigCommerce — paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Shopify — easy setup. BigCommerce — no extra transaction fees.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Shopify better than BigCommerce?

It depends on your requirements. Shopify is a strong fit for merchants wanting a fully hosted store, while BigCommerce suits mid-market brands needing built-in features.

### Is Shopify free to use?

Shopify does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Is BigCommerce free to use?

BigCommerce does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host Shopify or BigCommerce?

Shopify is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. BigCommerce is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Shopify and BigCommerce?

Shopify: extra transaction fees without Shopify Payments; app subscriptions add up. BigCommerce: plan upgrades tied to revenue; smaller app store.

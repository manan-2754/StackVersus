# Render vs Fly.io: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Render is the better pick for full-stack apps needing web services, databases and background jobs. Fly.io is the better pick for globally distributed apps running in many regions.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Render

_Unified cloud for apps, databases and cron jobs_

**Pros:**

- Heroku-like simplicity
- Managed Postgres and key-value stores
- Background workers and cron jobs

**Cons:**

- Free instances spin down when idle
- Fewer regions than hyperscalers

---

### Fly.io

_Platform for running full-stack apps close to users_

**Pros:**

- Runs apps in many regions
- Lightweight Firecracker microVMs
- Good for latency-sensitive apps

**Cons:**

- Steeper learning curve than simpler PaaS
- More operational responsibility

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Render — unified cloud for apps, databases and cron jobs. Fly.io — platform for running full-stack apps close to users.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Render can be started for free, while Fly.io requires a paid plan. Fly.io pricing: pay-as-you-go usage pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Render — Heroku-like simplicity. Fly.io — runs apps in many regions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Render better than Fly.io?

It depends on your requirements. Render is a strong fit for full-stack apps needing web services, databases and background jobs, while Fly.io suits globally distributed apps running in many regions.

### Is Render free to use?

Yes, you can start with Render for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Fly.io free to use?

Fly.io does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Pay-as-you-go usage pricing.

### Can I self-host Render or Fly.io?

Render is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Fly.io is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Render and Fly.io?

Render: free instances spin down when idle; fewer regions than hyperscalers. Fly.io: steeper learning curve than simpler PaaS; more operational responsibility.

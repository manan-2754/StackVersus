## Detailed Breakdown

### Asana

_Work management platform for teams_

**Pros:**

- Timelines, portfolios and goals
- Clean interface
- Strong integrations

**Cons:**

- Expensive for larger teams
- Single assignee per task

### Wrike

_Enterprise work management platform_

**Pros:**

- Gantt charts and resource management
- Custom workflows
- Enterprise security

**Cons:**

- Steep learning curve
- Cluttered interface

- **Signature strength:** Asana — timelines, portfolios and goals. Wrike — gantt charts and resource management.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Amazon SES

_Low-cost cloud email sending on AWS_

**Pros:**

- Very low cost per email
- Scales massively
- AWS integration

**Cons:**

- Bare-bones interface
- You manage reputation and setup

---

### Brevo

_Marketing and transactional email platform (formerly Sendinblue)_

**Pros:**

- Marketing, SMS and transactional together
- Free plan with daily sends
- Built-in CRM

**Cons:**

- Brevo branding on the free plan
- Less developer-focused

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Amazon SES — low-cost cloud email sending on AWS. Brevo — marketing and transactional email platform (formerly Sendinblue).

- **Signature strength:** Amazon SES — very low cost per email. Brevo — marketing, SMS and transactional together.

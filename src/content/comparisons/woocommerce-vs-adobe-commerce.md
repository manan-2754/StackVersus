## Detailed Breakdown

### WooCommerce

_Open-source e-commerce for WordPress_

**Pros:**

- Free core plugin
- Full control
- Huge extension ecosystem

**Cons:**

- Hosting and maintenance are on you
- Performance tuning needed

---

### Adobe Commerce

_Enterprise commerce platform built on Magento_

**Pros:**

- Highly customizable
- B2B features
- Open-source edition available

**Cons:**

- Expensive to run and develop
- Complex architecture

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** WooCommerce — open-source e-commerce for WordPress. Adobe Commerce — enterprise commerce platform built on Magento.
- Licensing differs: WooCommerce is open source while Adobe Commerce is open-core.
- **Deployment:** WooCommerce — self-hosted. Adobe Commerce — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** WooCommerce — free and open source. Adobe Commerce — free Magento Open Source; licensed Adobe Commerce.
- **Signature strength:** WooCommerce — free core plugin. Adobe Commerce — highly customizable.

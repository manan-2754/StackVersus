# CircleCI vs Buildkite: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> CircleCI is the better pick for fast, configurable cloud CI across code hosts. Buildkite is the better pick for large engineering teams running CI on their own compute.

## Detailed Breakdown

### CircleCI

_Cloud CI/CD platform focused on speed_

**Pros:**

- Fast builds with caching and parallelism
- Orbs for reusable configuration
- Supports GitHub, GitLab and Bitbucket

**Cons:**

- Credit-based pricing can be hard to predict
- Separate tool from your code host

---

### Buildkite

_Hybrid CI/CD with agents on your own infrastructure_

**Pros:**

- Agents run on your own infrastructure
- Scales to very large pipelines
- Dynamic pipeline generation

**Cons:**

- You manage the agent infrastructure
- Per-user pricing

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** CircleCI — cloud CI/CD platform focused on speed. Buildkite — hybrid CI/CD with agents on your own infrastructure.
- Licensing differs: CircleCI is proprietary while Buildkite is open-core.
- **Deployment:** CircleCI — managed cloud. Buildkite — managed control plane with self-hosted agents.
- **Pricing:** CircleCI — free tier with paid plans. Buildkite — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** CircleCI — fast builds with caching and parallelism. Buildkite — agents run on your own infrastructure.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is CircleCI better than Buildkite?

It depends on your requirements. CircleCI is a strong fit for fast, configurable cloud CI across code hosts, while Buildkite suits large engineering teams running CI on their own compute.

### Is CircleCI free to use?

Yes, you can start with CircleCI for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Buildkite free to use?

Yes, you can start with Buildkite for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host CircleCI or Buildkite?

CircleCI is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Buildkite can be self-hosted. Deployment options: managed control plane with self-hosted agents.

### What are the main drawbacks of CircleCI and Buildkite?

CircleCI: credit-based pricing can be hard to predict; separate tool from your code host. Buildkite: you manage the agent infrastructure; per-user pricing.

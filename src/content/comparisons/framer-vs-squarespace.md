## Detailed Breakdown

### Framer

_Design-first website builder_

**Pros:**

- Canvas that feels like a design tool
- Built-in animations
- Fast publishing

**Cons:**

- Less CMS depth than Webflow
- Less control over generated code

---

### Squarespace

_Design-led website builder_

**Pros:**

- Beautiful templates
- All-in-one hosting and commerce
- Easy to use

**Cons:**

- Limited customization
- No free plan

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Framer — design-first website builder. Squarespace — design-led website builder.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Framer can be started for free, while Squarespace requires a paid plan. Squarespace pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Framer — canvas that feels like a design tool. Squarespace — beautiful templates.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Framer better than Squarespace?

It depends on your requirements. Framer is a strong fit for designers who want fast, animated marketing sites, while Squarespace suits creatives and small businesses wanting polished templates.

### Is Framer free to use?

Yes, you can start with Framer for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Squarespace free to use?

Squarespace does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host Framer or Squarespace?

Framer is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Squarespace is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Framer and Squarespace?

Framer: less CMS depth than Webflow; less control over generated code. Squarespace: limited customization; no free plan.

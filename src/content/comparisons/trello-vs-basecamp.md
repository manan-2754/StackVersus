## Detailed Breakdown

### Trello

_Visual Kanban boards by Atlassian_

**Pros:**

- Extremely easy to use
- Power-Ups for extensions
- Free plan

**Cons:**

- Limited for complex projects
- Advanced views require paid plans

---

### Basecamp

_Opinionated project management and team communication_

**Pros:**

- Flat-price plan option
- Combines chat, docs and to-dos
- Calm, simple design

**Cons:**

- Limited reporting
- Fewer integrations

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Trello — Visual Kanban boards by Atlassian. Basecamp — opinionated project management and team communication.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Trello — free tier with paid plans. Basecamp — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Trello — extremely easy to use. Basecamp — flat-price plan option.

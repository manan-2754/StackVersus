## Detailed Breakdown

### Stytch

_Authentication and fraud prevention APIs_

**Pros:**

- Strong passwordless options
- Device fingerprinting and fraud tools
- Headless APIs and prebuilt UI

**Cons:**

- Smaller community
- Advanced fraud features cost extra

---

### FusionAuth

_Developer-focused auth you can self-host_

**Pros:**

- Free self-hosted Community edition
- Runs anywhere including on-premises
- Broad protocol support

**Cons:**

- Not open source
- Self-hosting adds operational work

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Stytch — authentication and fraud prevention APIs. FusionAuth — developer-focused auth you can self-host.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Deployment:** Stytch — managed cloud. FusionAuth — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Stytch — free tier with paid plans. FusionAuth — free Community edition; paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Stytch — strong passwordless options. FusionAuth — free self-hosted Community edition.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Stytch better than FusionAuth?

It depends on your requirements. Stytch is a strong fit for teams wanting passwordless auth with fraud protection, while FusionAuth suits teams wanting full control with self-hosted auth.

### Is Stytch free to use?

Yes, you can start with Stytch for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is FusionAuth free to use?

Yes, you can start with FusionAuth for free. Pricing model: Free Community edition; paid plans.

### Can I self-host Stytch or FusionAuth?

Stytch is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. FusionAuth can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Stytch and FusionAuth?

Stytch: smaller community; advanced fraud features cost extra. FusionAuth: not open source; self-hosting adds operational work.

**Pros:**

**Cons:**

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Kit — email marketing for creators (formerly ConvertKit). Medium — open publishing platform with a reader network.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Kit — free tier with paid plans. Medium — free to publish; paid reader membership.
- **Signature strength:** Kit — Visual automations. Medium — built-in audience.

### Is Medium free to use?

Yes, you can start with Medium for free. Pricing model: Free to publish; paid reader membership.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Vercel

_Frontend cloud from the creators of Next.js_

**Pros:**

- Best-in-class Next.js support
- Preview deployments for every branch
- Global edge network

**Cons:**

- Usage-based costs can spike
- Some features lock you into the platform

---

### Fly.io

_Platform for running full-stack apps close to users_

**Pros:**

- Runs apps in many regions
- Lightweight Firecracker microVMs
- Good for latency-sensitive apps

**Cons:**

- Steeper learning curve than simpler PaaS
- More operational responsibility

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Vercel — frontend cloud from the creators of Next.js. Fly.io — platform for running full-stack apps close to users.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Vercel can be started for free, while Fly.io requires a paid plan. Fly.io pricing: pay-as-you-go usage pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Vercel — best-in-class Next.js support. Fly.io — runs apps in many regions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Vercel better than Fly.io?

It depends on your requirements. Vercel is a strong fit for Next.js and frontend teams wanting zero-config deploys, while Fly.io suits globally distributed apps running in many regions.

### Is Vercel free to use?

Yes, you can start with Vercel for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Fly.io free to use?

Fly.io does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Pay-as-you-go usage pricing.

### Can I self-host Vercel or Fly.io?

Vercel is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Fly.io is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Vercel and Fly.io?

Vercel: usage-based costs can spike; some features lock you into the platform. Fly.io: steeper learning curve than simpler PaaS; more operational responsibility.

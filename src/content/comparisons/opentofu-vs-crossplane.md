## Detailed Breakdown

### OpenTofu

_Open-source Terraform fork under the Linux Foundation_

**Pros:**

- Drop-in Terraform compatibility
- MPL-licensed and community governed
- Adds features like state encryption

**Cons:**

- Gradual divergence from Terraform
- Smaller vendor support

---

### Crossplane

_Kubernetes-native control plane for cloud infrastructure_

**Pros:**

- Manage infrastructure through Kubernetes APIs
- Compositions for platform abstractions
- Continuous reconciliation

**Cons:**

- Requires Kubernetes expertise
- Steeper learning curve

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** OpenTofu — open-source Terraform fork under the Linux Foundation. Crossplane — Kubernetes-native control plane for cloud infrastructure.
- Both share the same licensing model (open source), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Deployment:** OpenTofu — CLI. Crossplane — runs in your Kubernetes cluster.
- **Pricing:** OpenTofu — free and open source. Crossplane — free and open source.
- **Signature strength:** OpenTofu — drop-in Terraform compatibility. Crossplane — manage infrastructure through Kubernetes APIs.

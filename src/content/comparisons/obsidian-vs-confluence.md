# Obsidian vs Confluence: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Obsidian is the better pick for individuals who want private, local Markdown notes. Confluence is the better pick for organizations already using Jira.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Obsidian

_Local-first Markdown knowledge base_

**Pros:**

- Plain Markdown files you own
- Huge plugin ecosystem
- Graph view and backlinks

**Cons:**

- Sync and Publish cost extra
- Limited real-time collaboration

---

### Confluence

_Team wiki by Atlassian_

**Pros:**

- Deep Jira integration
- Structured spaces and permissions
- Enterprise features

**Cons:**

- Editor can feel clunky
- Search quality varies

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Obsidian — local-first Markdown knowledge base. Confluence — team wiki by Atlassian.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Deployment:** Obsidian — desktop app. Confluence — managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Obsidian — free app; paid Sync and Publish. Confluence — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Obsidian — plain Markdown files you own. Confluence — deep Jira integration.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Obsidian better than Confluence?

It depends on your requirements. Obsidian is a strong fit for individuals who want private, local Markdown notes, while Confluence suits organizations already using Jira.

### Is Obsidian free to use?

Yes, you can start with Obsidian for free. Pricing model: Free app; paid Sync and Publish.

### Is Confluence free to use?

Yes, you can start with Confluence for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Obsidian or Confluence?

Obsidian runs locally on your own machine. Confluence is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Obsidian and Confluence?

Obsidian: sync and Publish cost extra; limited real-time collaboration. Confluence: editor can feel clunky; search quality varies.

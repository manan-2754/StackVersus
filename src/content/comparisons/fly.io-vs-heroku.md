# Fly.io vs Heroku: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Fly.io is the better pick for globally distributed apps running in many regions. Heroku is the better pick for teams that want a mature, simple PaaS.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Fly.io

_Platform for running full-stack apps close to users_

**Pros:**

- Runs apps in many regions
- Lightweight Firecracker microVMs
- Good for latency-sensitive apps

**Cons:**

- Steeper learning curve than simpler PaaS
- More operational responsibility

---

### Heroku

_Pioneering platform as a service by Salesforce_

**Pros:**

- Very simple git push deploys
- Mature add-on marketplace
- Well documented

**Cons:**

- No free dynos since 2022
- Higher cost than newer platforms

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Fly.io — platform for running full-stack apps close to users. Heroku — pioneering platform as a service by Salesforce.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Fly.io — pay-as-you-go usage pricing. Heroku — paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Fly.io — runs apps in many regions. Heroku — very simple git push deploys.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Fly.io better than Heroku?

It depends on your requirements. Fly.io is a strong fit for globally distributed apps running in many regions, while Heroku suits teams that want a mature, simple PaaS.

### Is Fly.io free to use?

Fly.io does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Pay-as-you-go usage pricing.

### Is Heroku free to use?

Heroku does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host Fly.io or Heroku?

Fly.io is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Heroku is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Fly.io and Heroku?

Fly.io: steeper learning curve than simpler PaaS; more operational responsibility. Heroku: no free dynos since 2022; higher cost than newer platforms.

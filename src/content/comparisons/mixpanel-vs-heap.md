## Detailed Breakdown

### Mixpanel

_Event-based product analytics_

**Pros:**

- Fast, intuitive reports
- Strong funnel and retention analysis
- Generous free plan

**Cons:**

- Event volume pricing can grow
- Requires a careful tracking plan

---

### Heap

_Product analytics with automatic event capture_

**Pros:**

- Autocapture of user interactions
- Retroactive event definitions
- Session replay available

**Cons:**

- Autocapture can produce noisy data
- Pricing is not transparent

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Mixpanel — event-based product analytics. Heap — product analytics with automatic event capture.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Mixpanel — free tier with paid plans. Heap — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Mixpanel — fast, intuitive reports. Heap — autocapture of user interactions.

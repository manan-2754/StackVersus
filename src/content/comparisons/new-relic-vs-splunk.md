# New Relic vs Splunk: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Detailed Breakdown

### New Relic

_Full-stack observability platform_

**Pros:**

- Free tier with monthly data ingest included
- Unified telemetry database
- Strong APM heritage

**Cons:**

- Per-user pricing for full platform users
- UI can feel complex

---

### Splunk

_Enterprise data and observability platform by Cisco_

**Pros:**

- Powerful search processing language
- Strong security analytics
- Enterprise-grade scale

**Cons:**

- Very expensive at high ingest volumes
- Steep learning curve

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** New Relic — full-stack observability platform. Splunk — enterprise data and observability platform by Cisco.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Deployment:** New Relic — managed cloud. Splunk — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- New Relic can be started for free, while Splunk requires a paid plan. Splunk pricing: custom enterprise pricing.
- **Signature strength:** New Relic — free tier with monthly data ingest included. Splunk — powerful search processing language.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is New Relic better than Splunk?

It depends on your requirements. New Relic is a strong fit for teams wanting full-stack observability with usage-based pricing, while Splunk suits enterprises with heavy log analytics and security needs.

### Is New Relic free to use?

Yes, you can start with New Relic for free. Pricing model: Free tier, then usage-based pricing.

### Is Splunk free to use?

Splunk does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Custom enterprise pricing.

### Can I self-host New Relic or Splunk?

New Relic is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Splunk can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of New Relic and Splunk?

New Relic: per-user pricing for full platform users; UI can feel complex. Splunk: very expensive at high ingest volumes; steep learning curve.

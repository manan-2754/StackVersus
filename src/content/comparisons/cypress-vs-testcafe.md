## Detailed Breakdown

### Cypress

_Developer-friendly E2E and component testing_

**Pros:**

- Interactive time-travel debugging
- Easy setup
- Component testing support

**Cons:**

- Limited multi-tab and cross-origin support
- Parallelization relies on paid Cypress Cloud

---

### TestCafe

_Node.js E2E testing without WebDriver_

**Pros:**

- No WebDriver or browser plugins needed
- Runs on any browser
- Built-in smart waits

**Cons:**

- Less active development than leaders
- Smaller ecosystem

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Cypress — developer-friendly E2E and component testing. TestCafe — Node.js E2E testing without WebDriver.
- Both share the same licensing model (open source), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Cypress — free open-source app; paid Cypress Cloud. TestCafe — free and open source.
- **Signature strength:** Cypress — interactive time-travel debugging. TestCafe — no WebDriver or browser plugins needed.

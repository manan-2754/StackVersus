## Detailed Breakdown

### Lemonsqueezy

_Merchant of record for digital products, owned by Stripe_

**Pros:**

- Merchant of record handles tax
- Storefronts and checkout out of the box
- Simple setup

**Cons:**

- Higher per-transaction fees
- Fewer enterprise features

---

### Braintree

_PayPal-owned payment gateway for developers_

**Pros:**

- Native PayPal and Venmo support
- Vault and recurring billing
- Mature SDKs

**Cons:**

- Smaller product suite than Stripe
- Slower feature development

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Lemonsqueezy — merchant of record for digital products, owned by Stripe. Braintree — PayPal-owned payment gateway for developers.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Lemonsqueezy — percentage plus fixed fee per transaction. Braintree — per-transaction fees, no monthly fee.
- **Signature strength:** Lemonsqueezy — merchant of record handles tax. Braintree — native PayPal and Venmo support.

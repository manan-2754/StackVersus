# Zapier vs n8n: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Zapier is the better pick for non-technical teams automating between SaaS apps. n8n is the better pick for technical teams wanting self-hosted automation.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Zapier

_No-code automation across thousands of apps_

**Pros:**

- Largest app catalog
- Very easy to use
- AI-assisted automation building

**Cons:**

- Task-based pricing gets expensive
- Complex logic is limited

---

### n8n

_Workflow automation with code when you need it_

**Pros:**

- Self-hostable
- JavaScript and Python code nodes
- AI agent workflows

**Cons:**

- Fair-code license limits some commercial uses
- More technical than Zapier

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Zapier — no-code automation across thousands of apps. n8n — workflow automation with code when you need it.
- Licensing differs: Zapier is proprietary while n8n is source-available.
- **Deployment:** Zapier — managed cloud. n8n — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Zapier — free tier with paid plans. n8n — free self-hosted community edition; paid cloud.
- **Signature strength:** Zapier — largest app catalog. n8n — self-hostable.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Zapier better than n8n?

It depends on your requirements. Zapier is a strong fit for non-technical teams automating between SaaS apps, while n8n suits technical teams wanting self-hosted automation.

### Is Zapier free to use?

Yes, you can start with Zapier for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is n8n free to use?

Yes, you can start with n8n for free. Pricing model: Free self-hosted community edition; paid cloud.

### Can I self-host Zapier or n8n?

Zapier is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. n8n can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Zapier and n8n?

Zapier: task-based pricing gets expensive; complex logic is limited. n8n: fair-code license limits some commercial uses; more technical than Zapier.

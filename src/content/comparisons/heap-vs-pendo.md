## Detailed Breakdown

### Heap

_Product analytics with automatic event capture_

**Pros:**

- Autocapture of user interactions
- Retroactive event definitions
- Session replay available

**Cons:**

- Autocapture can produce noisy data
- Pricing is not transparent

---

### Pendo

_Product experience platform with analytics and in-app guides_

**Pros:**

- In-app guides and walkthroughs
- Feedback and NPS tools
- Analytics without engineering work

**Cons:**

- Expensive for small teams
- Analytics less deep than dedicated tools

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Heap — product analytics with automatic event capture. Pendo — product experience platform with analytics and in-app guides.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Heap — free tier with paid plans. Pendo — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Heap — autocapture of user interactions. Pendo — in-app guides and walkthroughs.

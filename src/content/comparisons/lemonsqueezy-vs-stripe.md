**Cons:**

### Stripe

**Pros:**

**Cons:**

## Key Differences

- Merchant of Record Status: Lemon Squeezy acts as the MoR taking legal liability for tax collection, whereas Stripe is a payment processor requiring you to manage your own tax compliance.
- Fee Structure: Lemon Squeezy charges a higher flat percentage plus fee to cover taxes and compliance, while Stripe charges lower standard processing rates.
- Customization and APIs: Stripe offers granular API control for complex billing logic, whereas Lemon Squeezy provides an out-of-the-box hosted checkout experience.
- Global Reach: Lemon Squeezy instantly enables global sales by handling local tax laws natively, while Stripe requires third-party tools like Stripe Tax for global compliance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who handles chargebacks and fraud?

Lemon Squeezy handles chargebacks and fraud management as part of their Merchant of Record service. With Stripe, you are directly liable for chargebacks and must manage disputes through their dashboard.

### Can I use Stripe for global sales tax compliance?

Stripe does not automatically act as your Merchant of Record, but you can add Stripe Tax to calculate and collect taxes, though you remain legally responsible for filing and remitting them.

### Which platform is better for a SaaS startup?

If you want to launch quickly without worrying about international tax nexus and compliance, Lemon Squeezy is ideal. If you have engineering resources and need custom checkout flows, Stripe is better.

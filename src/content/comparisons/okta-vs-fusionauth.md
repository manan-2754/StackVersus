## Detailed Breakdown

### Okta

_Enterprise workforce and customer identity_

**Pros:**

- Industry-standard workforce SSO
- Thousands of prebuilt integrations
- Strong compliance posture

**Cons:**

- Expensive for small teams
- Heavyweight for simple apps

---

### FusionAuth

_Developer-focused auth you can self-host_

**Pros:**

- Free self-hosted Community edition
- Runs anywhere including on-premises
- Broad protocol support

**Cons:**

- Not open source
- Self-hosting adds operational work

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Okta — enterprise workforce and customer identity. FusionAuth — developer-focused auth you can self-host.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Deployment:** Okta — managed cloud. FusionAuth — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- FusionAuth can be started for free, while Okta requires a paid plan. Okta pricing: custom enterprise pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Okta — industry-standard workforce SSO. FusionAuth — free self-hosted Community edition.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Okta better than FusionAuth?

It depends on your requirements. Okta is a strong fit for large enterprises managing employee identity, while FusionAuth suits teams wanting full control with self-hosted auth.

### Is Okta free to use?

Okta does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Custom enterprise pricing.

### Is FusionAuth free to use?

Yes, you can start with FusionAuth for free. Pricing model: Free Community edition; paid plans.

### Can I self-host Okta or FusionAuth?

Okta is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. FusionAuth can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Okta and FusionAuth?

Okta: expensive for small teams; heavyweight for simple apps. FusionAuth: not open source; self-hosting adds operational work.

### Can I self-host PostHog or FullStory?

PostHog can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud. FullStory is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of PostHog and FullStory?

PostHog: breadth means some tools are less deep; self-hosting is only supported at small scale. FullStory: premium pricing; privacy configuration required.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Meilisearch

_Open-source, lightning-fast search engine_

**Pros:**

- Very easy setup
- Typo tolerance out of the box
- Hybrid search support

**Cons:**

- Less suited to very large datasets
- Fewer analytics features

---

### Elasticsearch

_Distributed search and analytics engine_

**Pros:**

- Powerful queries and aggregations
- Scales to huge datasets
- Large ecosystem

**Cons:**

- Operationally complex
- Resource-intensive

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Meilisearch — open-source, lightning-fast search engine. Elasticsearch — distributed search and analytics engine.
- Both share the same licensing model (open source), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Meilisearch — free open source; paid managed cloud. Elasticsearch — free open source; paid managed cloud.
- **Signature strength:** Meilisearch — very easy setup. Elasticsearch — powerful queries and aggregations.

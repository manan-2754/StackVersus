# Shopify vs Adobe Commerce: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Shopify is the better pick for merchants wanting a fully hosted store. Adobe Commerce is the better pick for large enterprises with complex catalogs.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Shopify

_Hosted commerce platform_

**Pros:**

- Easy setup
- Huge app ecosystem
- Reliable checkout

**Cons:**

- Extra transaction fees without Shopify Payments
- App subscriptions add up

---

### Adobe Commerce

_Enterprise commerce platform built on Magento_

**Pros:**

- Highly customizable
- B2B features
- Open-source edition available

**Cons:**

- Expensive to run and develop
- Complex architecture

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Shopify — hosted commerce platform. Adobe Commerce — enterprise commerce platform built on Magento.
- Licensing differs: Shopify is proprietary while Adobe Commerce is open-core.
- **Deployment:** Shopify — managed cloud. Adobe Commerce — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- Adobe Commerce can be started for free, while Shopify requires a paid plan. Shopify pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Shopify — easy setup. Adobe Commerce — highly customizable.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Shopify better than Adobe Commerce?

It depends on your requirements. Shopify is a strong fit for merchants wanting a fully hosted store, while Adobe Commerce suits large enterprises with complex catalogs.

### Is Shopify free to use?

Shopify does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Is Adobe Commerce free to use?

Yes, you can start with Adobe Commerce for free. Pricing model: Free Magento Open Source; licensed Adobe Commerce.

### Can I self-host Shopify or Adobe Commerce?

Shopify is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Adobe Commerce can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Shopify and Adobe Commerce?

Shopify: extra transaction fees without Shopify Payments; app subscriptions add up. Adobe Commerce: expensive to run and develop; complex architecture.

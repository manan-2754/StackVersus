## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Rootly better than FireHydrant?

It depends on your requirements. Rootly is a strong fit for teams automating incident workflows, while FireHydrant suits teams wanting runbooks and service catalogs.

### Is Rootly free to use?

Rootly does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Is FireHydrant free to use?

Yes, you can start with FireHydrant for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Rootly or FireHydrant?

Rootly is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. FireHydrant is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Rootly and FireHydrant?

Rootly: many options to configure; smaller community. FireHydrant: smaller ecosystem; some features overlap with other tools.

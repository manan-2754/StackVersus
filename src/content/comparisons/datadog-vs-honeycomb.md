## Detailed Breakdown

### Datadog

_Monitoring and security platform for cloud apps_

**Pros:**

- Hundreds of integrations
- Unified metrics, logs, APM and security
- Polished dashboards

**Cons:**

- Costs can escalate quickly
- Complex per-product pricing

---

### Honeycomb

_Observability for high-cardinality event data_

**Pros:**

- Fast queries on high-cardinality data
- BubbleUp outlier analysis
- OpenTelemetry-first

**Cons:**

- Different mental model from metrics dashboards
- Less breadth than all-in-one suites

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Datadog — monitoring and security platform for cloud apps. Honeycomb — observability for high-cardinality event data.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Datadog — free tier with paid plans. Honeycomb — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Datadog — hundreds of integrations. Honeycomb — fast queries on high-cardinality data.

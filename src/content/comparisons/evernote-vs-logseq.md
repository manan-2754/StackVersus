## Detailed Breakdown

### Evernote

_Long-running note-taking app_

**Pros:**

- Excellent web clipper
- Document scanning with search
- Cross-platform apps

**Cons:**

- Free plan is heavily limited
- Repeated price increases

---

### Logseq

_Open-source outliner for networked notes_

**Pros:**

- Block-based outliner
- Open source
- Local Markdown files

**Cons:**

- Database version is still maturing
- Can slow down with large graphs

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Evernote — long-running note-taking app. Logseq — open-source outliner for networked notes.
- Licensing differs: Evernote is proprietary while Logseq is open source.
- **Deployment:** Evernote — managed cloud. Logseq — desktop app.
- **Pricing:** Evernote — free tier with paid plans. Logseq — free and open source.
- **Signature strength:** Evernote — excellent web clipper. Logseq — block-based outliner.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Evernote better than Logseq?

It depends on your requirements. Evernote is a strong fit for personal note capture and web clipping, while Logseq suits outliner fans wanting local, open-source notes.

### Is Evernote free to use?

Yes, you can start with Evernote for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Logseq free to use?

Yes, you can start with Logseq for free. Pricing model: Free and open source.

### Can I self-host Evernote or Logseq?

Evernote is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Logseq runs locally on your own machine.

### What are the main drawbacks of Evernote and Logseq?

Evernote: free plan is heavily limited; repeated price increases. Logseq: database version is still maturing; can slow down with large graphs.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Railway

_Infrastructure platform for deploying apps and databases_

**Pros:**

- Very fast setup from a repository
- One-click databases
- Usage-based pricing

**Cons:**

- Costs less predictable than flat plans
- Less control than raw cloud infrastructure

---

### Fly.io

_Platform for running full-stack apps close to users_

**Pros:**

- Runs apps in many regions
- Lightweight Firecracker microVMs
- Good for latency-sensitive apps

**Cons:**

- Steeper learning curve than simpler PaaS
- More operational responsibility

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Railway — infrastructure platform for deploying apps and databases. Fly.io — platform for running full-stack apps close to users.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- **Pricing:** Railway — pay-as-you-go usage pricing. Fly.io — pay-as-you-go usage pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Railway — very fast setup from a repository. Fly.io — runs apps in many regions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Railway better than Fly.io?

It depends on your requirements. Railway is a strong fit for quick deployment of full-stack apps and side projects, while Fly.io suits globally distributed apps running in many regions.

### Is Railway free to use?

Railway does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Pay-as-you-go usage pricing.

### Is Fly.io free to use?

Fly.io does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Pay-as-you-go usage pricing.

### Can I self-host Railway or Fly.io?

Railway is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Fly.io is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Railway and Fly.io?

Railway: costs less predictable than flat plans; less control than raw cloud infrastructure. Fly.io: steeper learning curve than simpler PaaS; more operational responsibility.

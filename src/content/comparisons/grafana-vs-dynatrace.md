# Grafana vs Dynatrace: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Grafana is the better pick for teams wanting open-source dashboards across many data sources. Dynatrace is the better pick for large enterprises wanting automated root-cause analysis.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Grafana

_Open-source observability and dashboards_

**Pros:**

- Connects to many data sources
- Open-source LGTM stack (Loki, Grafana, Tempo, Mimir)
- Grafana Cloud free tier

**Cons:**

- Self-hosting the full stack is complex
- Requires assembling multiple components

---

### Dynatrace

_AI-powered observability and security platform_

**Pros:**

- Automatic discovery with OneAgent
- AI-driven root-cause analysis
- Strong enterprise support

**Cons:**

- Expensive
- Less approachable for small teams

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Grafana — open-source observability and dashboards. Dynatrace — AI-powered observability and security platform.
- Licensing differs: Grafana is open source while Dynatrace is proprietary.
- **Deployment:** Grafana — self-hosted or managed cloud. Dynatrace — managed cloud.
- Grafana can be started for free, while Dynatrace requires a paid plan. Dynatrace pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Grafana — connects to many data sources. Dynatrace — automatic discovery with OneAgent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Grafana better than Dynatrace?

It depends on your requirements. Grafana is a strong fit for teams wanting open-source dashboards across many data sources, while Dynatrace suits large enterprises wanting automated root-cause analysis.

### Is Grafana free to use?

Yes, you can start with Grafana for free. Pricing model: Free open source; paid managed cloud.

### Is Dynatrace free to use?

Dynatrace does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host Grafana or Dynatrace?

Grafana can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud. Dynatrace is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Grafana and Dynatrace?

Grafana: self-hosting the full stack is complex; requires assembling multiple components. Dynatrace: expensive; less approachable for small teams.

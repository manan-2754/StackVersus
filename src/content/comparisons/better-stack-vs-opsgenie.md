## Detailed Breakdown

### Better Stack

_Uptime monitoring, logs and incident management_

**Pros:**

- Uptime monitoring and status pages
- Integrated log management
- Modern UI

**Cons:**

- Less mature enterprise features than PagerDuty
- Many products to configure

---

### Opsgenie

_Atlassian on-call and alert management_

**Pros:**

- Tight Jira integration
- Flexible alert routing
- Affordable on-call

**Cons:**

- Atlassian stopped selling Opsgenie in favor of Jira Service Management
- Limited future roadmap

- **Positioning:** Better Stack — uptime monitoring, logs and incident management. Opsgenie — Atlassian on-call and alert management.

- **Signature strength:** Better Stack — uptime monitoring and status pages. Opsgenie — tight Jira integration.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Mixpanel

_Event-based product analytics_

**Pros:**

- Fast, intuitive reports
- Strong funnel and retention analysis
- Generous free plan

**Cons:**

- Event volume pricing can grow
- Requires a careful tracking plan

---

### Pendo

_Product experience platform with analytics and in-app guides_

**Pros:**

- In-app guides and walkthroughs
- Feedback and NPS tools
- Analytics without engineering work

**Cons:**

- Expensive for small teams
- Analytics less deep than dedicated tools

## Detailed Breakdown

### WorkOS

_Enterprise features like SSO and SCIM for B2B SaaS_

**Pros:**

- Easy SAML SSO and directory sync
- AuthKit user management with a free tier
- Admin portal for customer IT teams

**Cons:**

- Per-connection pricing for SSO
- Focused on B2B use cases

---

### Okta

_Enterprise workforce and customer identity_

**Pros:**

- Industry-standard workforce SSO
- Thousands of prebuilt integrations
- Strong compliance posture

**Cons:**

- Expensive for small teams
- Heavyweight for simple apps

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** WorkOS — enterprise features like SSO and SCIM for B2B SaaS. Okta — enterprise workforce and customer identity.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- WorkOS can be started for free, while Okta requires a paid plan. Okta pricing: custom enterprise pricing.
- **Signature strength:** WorkOS — easy SAML SSO and directory sync. Okta — industry-standard workforce SSO.

Okta does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Custom enterprise pricing.

### Can I self-host WorkOS or Okta?

WorkOS is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Okta is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of WorkOS and Okta?

WorkOS: per-connection pricing for SSO; focused on B2B use cases. Okta: expensive for small teams; heavyweight for simple apps.

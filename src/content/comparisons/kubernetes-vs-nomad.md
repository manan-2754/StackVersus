## Detailed Breakdown

### Kubernetes

_Industry-standard container orchestration_

**Pros:**

- Massive ecosystem
- Managed offerings on every cloud
- Highly extensible

**Cons:**

- Steep learning curve
- Operational complexity

---

### Nomad

_Flexible workload orchestrator by HashiCorp_

**Pros:**

- Single binary with simple operations
- Runs containers and non-containerized apps
- Integrates with Consul and Vault

**Cons:**

- BSL license
- Smaller ecosystem than Kubernetes

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Kubernetes — industry-standard container orchestration. Nomad — flexible workload orchestrator by HashiCorp.
- Licensing differs: Kubernetes is open source while Nomad is source-available.
- **Deployment:** Kubernetes — self-hosted or managed on every major cloud. Nomad — self-hosted.
- **Pricing:** Kubernetes — free and open source. Nomad — free community edition; paid enterprise.
- **Signature strength:** Kubernetes — massive ecosystem. Nomad — single binary with simple operations.

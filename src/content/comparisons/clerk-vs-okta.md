## Detailed Breakdown

### Clerk

_Drop-in authentication and user management for web apps_

**Pros:**

- Polished prebuilt components
- Organizations and multi-tenancy
- Excellent Next.js integration

**Cons:**

- Less flexible outside JavaScript frameworks
- Costs rise with active users

---

### Okta

_Enterprise workforce and customer identity_

**Pros:**

- Industry-standard workforce SSO
- Thousands of prebuilt integrations
- Strong compliance posture

**Cons:**

- Expensive for small teams
- Heavyweight for simple apps

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Clerk — drop-in authentication and user management for web apps. Okta — enterprise workforce and customer identity.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Clerk can be started for free, while Okta requires a paid plan. Okta pricing: custom enterprise pricing.
- **Signature strength:** Clerk — polished prebuilt components. Okta — industry-standard workforce SSO.

### Is Clerk better than Okta?

It depends on your requirements. Clerk is a strong fit for React and Next.js apps wanting prebuilt auth UI, while Okta suits large enterprises managing employee identity.

Yes, you can start with Clerk for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Okta free to use?

Okta does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Custom enterprise pricing.

### Can I self-host Clerk or Okta?

Clerk is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Okta is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Clerk and Okta?

Clerk: less flexible outside JavaScript frameworks; costs rise with active users. Okta: expensive for small teams; heavyweight for simple apps.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Rollbar better than Raygun?

It depends on your requirements. Rollbar is a strong fit for teams wanting simple error alerting with smart grouping, while Raygun suits teams wanting crash reporting and RUM in one place.

### Is Rollbar free to use?

Yes, you can start with Rollbar for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Raygun free to use?

Raygun does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host Rollbar or Raygun?

Rollbar is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Raygun is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Rollbar and Raygun?

Rollbar: fewer performance features than Sentry; smaller ecosystem. Raygun: no permanent free tier; smaller community.

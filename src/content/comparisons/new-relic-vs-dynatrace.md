## Detailed Breakdown

### New Relic

_Full-stack observability platform_

**Pros:**

- Free tier with monthly data ingest included
- Unified telemetry database
- Strong APM heritage

**Cons:**

- Per-user pricing for full platform users
- UI can feel complex

---

### Dynatrace

_AI-powered observability and security platform_

**Pros:**

- Automatic discovery with OneAgent
- AI-driven root-cause analysis
- Strong enterprise support

**Cons:**

- Expensive
- Less approachable for small teams

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** New Relic — full-stack observability platform. Dynatrace — AI-powered observability and security platform.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- New Relic can be started for free, while Dynatrace requires a paid plan. Dynatrace pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** New Relic — free tier with monthly data ingest included. Dynatrace — automatic discovery with OneAgent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is New Relic better than Dynatrace?

It depends on your requirements. New Relic is a strong fit for teams wanting full-stack observability with usage-based pricing, while Dynatrace suits large enterprises wanting automated root-cause analysis.

### Is New Relic free to use?

Yes, you can start with New Relic for free. Pricing model: Free tier, then usage-based pricing.

### Is Dynatrace free to use?

Dynatrace does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host New Relic or Dynatrace?

New Relic is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Dynatrace is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of New Relic and Dynatrace?

New Relic: per-user pricing for full platform users; UI can feel complex. Dynatrace: expensive; less approachable for small teams.

# Sentry vs Raygun: Head-to-Head Comparison

> Sentry is the better pick for developers wanting detailed error and performance monitoring. Raygun is the better pick for teams wanting crash reporting and RUM in one place.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Sentry

_Application monitoring and error tracking_

**Pros:**

- Rich stack traces and context
- Performance tracing and session replay
- SDKs for most languages

**Cons:**

- Event quotas require tuning
- Self-hosting is resource-heavy

---

### Raygun

_Crash reporting, real user monitoring and APM_

**Pros:**

- Crash reporting plus real user monitoring
- Strong .NET support
- Deployment tracking

**Cons:**

- No permanent free tier
- Smaller community

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Sentry — application monitoring and error tracking. Raygun — crash reporting, real user monitoring and APM.
- Licensing differs: Sentry is source-available while Raygun is proprietary.
- **Deployment:** Sentry — self-hosted or managed cloud. Raygun — managed cloud.
- Sentry can be started for free, while Raygun requires a paid plan. Raygun pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** Sentry — rich stack traces and context. Raygun — crash reporting plus real user monitoring.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Sentry better than Raygun?

It depends on your requirements. Sentry is a strong fit for developers wanting detailed error and performance monitoring, while Raygun suits teams wanting crash reporting and RUM in one place.

### Is Sentry free to use?

Yes, you can start with Sentry for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Raygun free to use?

Raygun does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Can I self-host Sentry or Raygun?

Sentry can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud. Raygun is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Sentry and Raygun?

Sentry: event quotas require tuning; self-hosting is resource-heavy. Raygun: no permanent free tier; smaller community.

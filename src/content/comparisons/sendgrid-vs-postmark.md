## Detailed Breakdown

### SendGrid

_Email delivery platform by Twilio_

**Pros:**

- Handles very high volume
- Marketing and transactional email in one platform
- Mature APIs

**Cons:**

- Shared IP deliverability varies
- Support can be slow on lower plans

---

### Postmark

_Transactional email focused on deliverability_

**Pros:**

- Excellent deliverability
- Separate message streams
- Great documentation

**Cons:**

- Higher price per email
- Limited marketing features

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** SendGrid — email delivery platform by Twilio. Postmark — transactional email focused on deliverability.
- Both share the same licensing model (proprietary), so the decision comes down to features and workflow fit.
- Postmark can be started for free, while SendGrid requires a paid plan. SendGrid pricing: paid plans; free trial available.
- **Signature strength:** SendGrid — handles very high volume. Postmark — excellent deliverability.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is SendGrid better than Postmark?

It depends on your requirements. SendGrid is a strong fit for high-volume transactional and marketing email, while Postmark suits apps where transactional deliverability is critical.

### Is SendGrid free to use?

SendGrid does not have a permanent free plan. Pricing model: Paid plans; free trial available.

### Is Postmark free to use?

Yes, you can start with Postmark for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host SendGrid or Postmark?

SendGrid is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Postmark is offered as a managed service: managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of SendGrid and Postmark?

SendGrid: shared IP deliverability varies; support can be slow on lower plans. Postmark: higher price per email; limited marketing features.

# Algolia vs OpenSearch: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Algolia is the better pick for e-commerce and content sites needing fast hosted search. OpenSearch is the better pick for teams wanting Apache-licensed search, often on AWS.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Algolia

_Hosted search and discovery API_

**Pros:**

- Very fast hosted search
- InstantSearch UI libraries
- Merchandising and analytics

**Cons:**

- Expensive at scale
- Proprietary

---

### OpenSearch

_Community-driven fork of Elasticsearch_

**Pros:**

- Apache 2.0 license
- Managed option on AWS
- Dashboards and plugins included

**Cons:**

- Feature divergence from Elasticsearch
- Operational complexity

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Algolia — hosted search and discovery API. OpenSearch — community-driven fork of Elasticsearch.
- Licensing differs: Algolia is proprietary while OpenSearch is open source.
- **Deployment:** Algolia — managed cloud. OpenSearch — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Algolia — free tier, then usage-based pricing. OpenSearch — free and open source.
- **Signature strength:** Algolia — very fast hosted search. OpenSearch — apache 2.0 license.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Algolia better than OpenSearch?

It depends on your requirements. Algolia is a strong fit for e-commerce and content sites needing fast hosted search, while OpenSearch suits teams wanting Apache-licensed search, often on AWS.

### Is Algolia free to use?

Yes, you can start with Algolia for free. Pricing model: Free tier, then usage-based pricing.

### Is OpenSearch free to use?

Yes, you can start with OpenSearch for free. Pricing model: Free and open source.

### Can I self-host Algolia or OpenSearch?

Algolia is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. OpenSearch can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Algolia and OpenSearch?

Algolia: expensive at scale; proprietary. OpenSearch: feature divergence from Elasticsearch; operational complexity.

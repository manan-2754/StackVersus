## Detailed Breakdown

### Terraform

_Infrastructure as code with HCL by HashiCorp_

**Pros:**

- Huge provider ecosystem
- Declarative HCL
- Widely adopted

**Cons:**

- Moved to the BSL license in 2023
- State management complexity

---

### OpenTofu

_Open-source Terraform fork under the Linux Foundation_

**Pros:**

- Drop-in Terraform compatibility
- MPL-licensed and community governed
- Adds features like state encryption

**Cons:**

- Gradual divergence from Terraform
- Smaller vendor support

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Terraform — infrastructure as code with HCL by HashiCorp. OpenTofu — open-source Terraform fork under the Linux Foundation.
- Licensing differs: Terraform is source-available while OpenTofu is open source.
- **Deployment:** Terraform — CLI with optional HCP Terraform cloud. OpenTofu — CLI.
- **Pricing:** Terraform — free CLI; paid HCP Terraform. OpenTofu — free and open source.
- **Signature strength:** Terraform — huge provider ecosystem. OpenTofu — drop-in Terraform compatibility.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Terraform better than OpenTofu?

It depends on your requirements. Terraform is a strong fit for multi-cloud infrastructure with the largest provider ecosystem, while OpenTofu suits teams wanting Terraform compatibility with an open-source license.

### Is Terraform free to use?

Yes, you can start with Terraform for free. Pricing model: Free CLI; paid HCP Terraform.

### Is OpenTofu free to use?

Yes, you can start with OpenTofu for free. Pricing model: Free and open source.

### Can I self-host Terraform or OpenTofu?

Terraform can be self-hosted. Deployment options: CLI with optional HCP Terraform cloud. OpenTofu can be self-hosted. Deployment options: CLI.

### What are the main drawbacks of Terraform and OpenTofu?

Terraform: moved to the BSL license in 2023; state management complexity. OpenTofu: gradual divergence from Terraform; smaller vendor support.

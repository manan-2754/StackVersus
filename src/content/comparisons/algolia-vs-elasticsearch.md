# Algolia vs Elasticsearch: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Algolia is the better pick for e-commerce and content sites needing fast hosted search. Elasticsearch is the better pick for large-scale full-text search and log analytics.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Algolia

_Hosted search and discovery API_

**Pros:**

- Very fast hosted search
- InstantSearch UI libraries
- Merchandising and analytics

**Cons:**

- Expensive at scale
- Proprietary

---

### Elasticsearch

_Distributed search and analytics engine_

**Pros:**

- Powerful queries and aggregations
- Scales to huge datasets
- Large ecosystem

**Cons:**

- Operationally complex
- Resource-intensive

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Algolia — hosted search and discovery API. Elasticsearch — distributed search and analytics engine.
- Licensing differs: Algolia is proprietary while Elasticsearch is open source.
- **Deployment:** Algolia — managed cloud. Elasticsearch — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Algolia — free tier, then usage-based pricing. Elasticsearch — free open source; paid managed cloud.
- **Signature strength:** Algolia — very fast hosted search. Elasticsearch — powerful queries and aggregations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Algolia better than Elasticsearch?

It depends on your requirements. Algolia is a strong fit for e-commerce and content sites needing fast hosted search, while Elasticsearch suits large-scale full-text search and log analytics.

### Is Algolia free to use?

Yes, you can start with Algolia for free. Pricing model: Free tier, then usage-based pricing.

### Is Elasticsearch free to use?

Yes, you can start with Elasticsearch for free. Pricing model: Free open source; paid managed cloud.

### Can I self-host Algolia or Elasticsearch?

Algolia is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Elasticsearch can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Algolia and Elasticsearch?

Algolia: expensive at scale; proprietary. Elasticsearch: operationally complex; resource-intensive.

# Snyk vs Semgrep: Head-to-Head Comparison

## Quick Verdict

> Snyk is the better pick for developer-first dependency and code scanning. Semgrep is the better pick for security teams writing custom rules.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Snyk

_Developer security platform_

**Pros:**

- Dependency, code, container and IaC scanning
- IDE and pull request integration
- Fix suggestions

**Cons:**

- Can get expensive
- Alert noise without tuning

---

### Semgrep

_Fast, rule-based static analysis_

**Pros:**

- Easy custom rules
- Fast scans
- Supply chain and secrets scanning

**Cons:**

- Advanced features in the paid platform
- Rule tuning needed

---

## Key Differences

- **Positioning:** Snyk — developer security platform. Semgrep — fast, rule-based static analysis.
- Licensing differs: Snyk is proprietary while Semgrep is open-core.
- **Deployment:** Snyk — managed cloud. Semgrep — self-hosted or managed cloud.
- **Pricing:** Snyk — free tier with paid plans. Semgrep — free tier with paid plans.
- **Signature strength:** Snyk — dependency, code, container and IaC scanning. Semgrep — easy custom rules.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Snyk better than Semgrep?

It depends on your requirements. Snyk is a strong fit for developer-first dependency and code scanning, while Semgrep suits security teams writing custom rules.

### Is Snyk free to use?

Yes, you can start with Snyk for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Is Semgrep free to use?

Yes, you can start with Semgrep for free. Pricing model: Free tier with paid plans.

### Can I self-host Snyk or Semgrep?

Snyk is offered as a managed service: managed cloud. Semgrep can be self-hosted. Deployment options: self-hosted or managed cloud.

### What are the main drawbacks of Snyk and Semgrep?

Snyk: can get expensive; alert noise without tuning. Semgrep: advanced features in the paid platform; rule tuning needed.
